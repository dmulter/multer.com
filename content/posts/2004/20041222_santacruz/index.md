Charlotte took everyone to downtown Santa Cruz for a bit of shopping and such.
I'm not sure if they actually bought cookies to go with these photos in front
of the best smelling place on Pacific Avenue. Though that honor may be in
jeopardy with the recent arrival of a Coldstone Creamery just down the street.
Of course Noah's Bagels is pretty awesome as well.

![01](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2004/20041222_santacruz/01.jpg)

A late night swim over at La Madrona's is a nice way to top off the day. Max
and Gabe are looking for trouble in the hot tub.
