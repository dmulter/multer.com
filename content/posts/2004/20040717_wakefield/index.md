I brought my Serotta up north this summer so I could really pound back the
miles. I didn't trust the airlines with my Trek, but the steel frame made it
no problem. Turns out there's a century ride in Wakefield called the North
Country 100. The Serotta is way too uncomfortable for 100 miles, but 100
kilometers is perfect. Quite a few less mountains then I'm used to, but it was
a beautiful ride around Lake Gogebic.
