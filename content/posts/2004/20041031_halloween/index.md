Monica is doing the Gothic witch thing this year. She came upstairs to show me
her costume while I was working on the computer, and I was totally freaked
out. I immediately had very scary visions of the teenager to be. Of course I
calmly told her she looked great. Meanwhile Gabe is still looking cute as a
button in his snake costume.

Charlotte took them down to the wharf during the day as a warmup for the real
event later tonight. There's even a couple of classic Woodies behind them.
Later that evening we walked around the local streets and then hooked up with
the Peysers to collect a bit more candy on the other side of the
neighborhood.
