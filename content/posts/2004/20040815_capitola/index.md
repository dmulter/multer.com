Gabe is still in withdrawal from the constant fishing in Michigan, so we head
out into the bay off the Capitola wharf. Monica is along for trip this time
also. We catch our usual collection of small fish, though the big ones still
elude us. We caught a lot of very small halibut, so I'm hoping we're getting
closer to catching the big fish.
