Gordy, Donna, Annie, and little Gordon are in town visiting for big Gordon's
80th birthday this weekend. They're staying with us for a few nights, and it's
sure great seeing them sometime in addition to the usual summer trip to
Bootjack. Charlotte took them down to visit with Gordon at his place in
Capitola. It's always a great trip when you get to visit the beach. Luckily
the rain gave us a break today.
