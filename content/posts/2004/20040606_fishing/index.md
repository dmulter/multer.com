Gabe and I haven't been out fishing for quite some time, so it's time to break
out the poles. Gabe got up bright and early at 4:30am this morning. No way I'm
getting out of the house before 6:30am. It's a great day and there's no one
else out for some reason. I'm sure happy to not see the usual mob scene down
at the wharf. Once out on the bay, we start catching kingfish almost
immediately. We even snagged a pretty big octopus this time, though the
picture is a bit blurry. Nothing like a nice cup of cocoa to help pass the
time.
