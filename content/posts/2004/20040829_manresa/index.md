We finally stopped by a local sports store and bought some decent boogie
boards. We picked up a wetsuit and board for Gabe so he could join in on the
fun as well. Down at Manresa state beach it's time to break in the new
equipment. I think Gabe is addicted too now. Margot and her family joined us
for what was a great day even if the fog never broke.
