The second annual SVEF Mountain Challenge century ride is coming up again very
soon. Last year's ride was a big success and raised quite a bit of money for
the Scotts Valley  schools. This year we've got lots more riders and hope to
blow away our previous fundraising efforts. Charlotte has been spending a ton
of time helping out with the organization of the ride. I just have to ride the
bike, which I love anyway. I'm out for a nice long ride with people like Matt,
Craig, Eric, Kevin, Ron, David, Chimene, John, Mary, and Bill.
