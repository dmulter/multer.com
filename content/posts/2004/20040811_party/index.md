Charlotte's very good friend Kim is unfortunately moving away along with her
kids Lily and Jack. We'll all miss her very much. Charlotte, Paula, and many
more of her friends have a going away party where people can say goodbye.
We'll try to see them as much as we can for the few more days she's still in
town.
