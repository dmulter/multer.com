Charlotte, Gordon, and Valri head up to San Francisco to check out the modern
art at [SFMOMA](https://www.sfmoma.org). Unfortunately it turned out that a
lot of the best exhibits happened to be closed, but it sounds like the trip
was a success anyway. How can any trip to San Francisco not be.
