I've been doing four hour training rides from Bootjack every other day that
take me past places like Mohawk, Gay, Eagle River, Lake Linden, Copper City,
Five Mile Point, and Big Traverse. I've wanted to do a loop that gets all the
way out to Copper Harbor, but it's a bit too far from Bootjack. Charlotte took
me out to the Tobacco River park near Gay to cut an hour out of my ride. This
ride took me to Lac La Belle, up the hill to the covered drive into Copper
Harbor, up Brockway Mountain Drive, down to Eagle Harbor, and then back.
Really a beautiful ride and finally a few hills!
