My brother is heading up north to his place in Ishpeming, so we're heading
south for a quick visit. The [Bishop Baraga](https://bishopbaraga.org) shrine
beckons to us from the highway. If it weren't for the swarms of black flies,
it would have been nice to hang out for a while. This guy has got quite a
view.
