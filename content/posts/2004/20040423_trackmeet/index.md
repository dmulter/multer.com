Nick enjoyed cross country a great deal at the start of his first semester.
This semester he's doing track and seems to like shot put and the long jump.
His first meet is over at San Lorenzo High School over in Felton. It's an
awesome day and I decided to take off early from work to be there. It was fun
seeing Joe and other friends from school. The sprinklers came on late in the
day just to make things interesting.
