Charlotte goes for very fast long walks in the redwoods of Henry Cowell park a
few times a week. Today she's doing the walk with Paula, Margot, Sonia, and
Dyana. I often go with Charlotte on Sundays, so I know very well how
incredibly beautiful these trips are. We sure are lucky to live in such a
great place.
