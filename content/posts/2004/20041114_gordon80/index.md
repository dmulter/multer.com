We're off to George's house to celebrate Gordon's 80th birthday. Gordy and his
family, Karen, Valri's family, Bea, Renee and her family, and our family will
all be there for the big party. Charlotte took Gordy and his family up to San
Francisco for the morning before meeting up at George's place.
