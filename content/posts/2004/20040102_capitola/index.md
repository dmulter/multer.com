Val and her family are off to ski country and Max is staying over with us. Abby
always enjoys having a friend over to play. Sure wish Joe was here too.

![01](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2004/20040102_capitola/01.jpg)

In between storms, we head over to Capitola to check things out. The rain
breaks for long enough to have some fun on the beach. No chance of getting a
tan this time of year. Plenty of driftwood and shells worth checking out.
