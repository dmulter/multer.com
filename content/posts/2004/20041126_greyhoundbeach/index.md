George and his family stayed with us for Thanksgiving this year. Following a
huge meal over Valri's house and a restful sleep at ours, we head up to
Greyhound Rock beach north of Santa Cruz. George and Ellen haven't been to
this beach before, and it's one of our favorites. I'm sure we'll be taking
them back there again sometime soon. There were plenty of fun things to keep
everyone busy. Climbing on the rocks, mining for crystals, exploring
tidepools, and just soaking up the sun.
