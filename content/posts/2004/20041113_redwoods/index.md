Charlotte took Gordy, his family, and Gordon out to Henry Cowell for a walk in
the redwoods. It's a great loop trail with plenty of fun for the kids. The big
tree half way through is always a treat. It's hollow on the inside like many
old redwoods, but this one is big enough for quite a few people to fit inside.
It's a bit of a crouching down effort to get in, but there's plenty of room
once you're inside. Annie and Gordon certainly enjoyed it.
