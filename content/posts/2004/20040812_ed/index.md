It's been almost 20 years since we last saw Ed Van Buskirk and his family. We
all worked together at Burroughs in Boulder. Since then we've been living in
California and Ed and Barb have been in Kansas City. They came out for a
whirlwind tour of the Bay Area and we're so happy they stopped by Santa Cruz
for a visit.
