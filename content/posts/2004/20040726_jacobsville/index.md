It's a beautiful night so we're off to Jacobsville some swimming, fishing, and
maybe a campfire. We're working the same spot Gabe caught last year's pike,
but we're not having any luck so far this year. It's really shallow on the
beach side and Monica really loves heading way out even if the water is really
cold.
