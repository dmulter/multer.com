These photos were taken a few days before Halloween, or as our neighbors call
it, 'The Devil's Holiday'. Annabel dressed up as a pink poodle for Halloween
(sorry no photos of that - duh!) and got lots of candy. She smells each one
for a hint of peanut butter. Annabel cannot tolerate peanut butter in any form
or fashion. Funny stuff!
