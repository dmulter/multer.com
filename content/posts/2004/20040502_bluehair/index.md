I offered to color my hair blue for the recent SVEF bike ride in exchange for
large donations. A few people were generous enough to take me up on my offer,
so I didn't want to disappoint. It didn't come out as dark as I expected, but
you get the idea. Thanks to those that helped me raise over $1000 for our
schools.
