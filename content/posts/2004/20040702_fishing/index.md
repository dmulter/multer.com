Time for another summer trip to Bootjack. One of Gabe's favorite ways to spend
his time up north is out on the dock fishing. Most of the time it's rock bass,
but here he's caught a smallmouth bass. If you're really lucky you can catch a
northern pike. Gabe is hoping to beat last year's gigantic pike. Keep working
those lines.
