Scott is up at the Bolger place in the Michigan North Woods Club for a long
weekend. It's a great excuse to see Scott, Bridget, and Annabel.

![02](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2004/20040716_mnwc/02.jpg)
![03](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2004/20040716_mnwc/03.jpg)
![04](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2004/20040716_mnwc/04.jpg)
![05](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2004/20040716_mnwc/05.jpg)

The community has a number of private lakes with boats tied up to a dock for
member use. Scott takes us out for a bit of fishing on the largest of them.
The rain managed to restrain itself while we enjoyed the great outdoors.

![06](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2004/20040716_mnwc/06.jpg)

Back at the house we're all recovering from a very active day. Bridget works on
one of her fabulous meals and we're more than happy to wolf it all down. I'm
so glad we finally managed to see each other while in Michigan even if it was
only for a short visit.
