Charlotte heads down to Gordon's place in Capitola with kids in tow. To raise
the level of fun just a bit higher, Val and Kim's kids join in as well. You
really can't tell how cold the ocean really is from the way everyone is
playing in the water. Take my word for it, it's freezing.
