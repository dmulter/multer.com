Charlie just got a new airgun and has been wanting to head over to our house to
try it out. He invited just a couple of his friends to have the pleasure of
being fired on with a stream of hundreds of pellets per second. Some of these
kids take this pretty seriously. I'm glad Nick is on Charlie's team.
