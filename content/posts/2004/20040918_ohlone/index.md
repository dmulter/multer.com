Gabe was very excited about heading out to Ohlone Day over at Henry Cowell
park in Felton. I guess it was a big deal at school and he was particularly
interested in the spear throwing he had heard about. We brought Doug along to
make sure the day was a big success. First stop is a demonstration of starting
a fire without a match or a flame thrower. Gabe's attention appears to be
drifting as the guy is taking way too long to get the fire going.

![01](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2004/20040918_ohlone/01.jpg)
![02](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2004/20040918_ohlone/02.jpg)

Now this is more like it. Throwing small spears at pretend targets two people
roll between each other. Gabe has a lot of luck with nailing the deer, coyote,
and the rabbit. The Ohlone children learned to hunt this way. Gabe has a bit
more practice ahead of him before joining in a real hunt.

![03](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2004/20040918_ohlone/03.jpg)

Finally we make it to the real spear throwing area. A docent demonstrates the
use of the spear and the atlatl. Looks easy doesn't it?

![04](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2004/20040918_ohlone/04.jpg)
![05](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2004/20040918_ohlone/05.jpg)
![06](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2004/20040918_ohlone/06.jpg)

Gabe and Doug strategize on their chances of success with such a complicated
sequence of actions. Gabe concentrates hard and gets off a few good throws.
Unfortunately the reality of throwing a real spear like this is well beyond
the attention span of my collection of young boys.

![07](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2004/20040918_ohlone/07.jpg)
![08](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2004/20040918_ohlone/08.jpg)
![09](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2004/20040918_ohlone/09.jpg)
![10](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2004/20040918_ohlone/10.jpg)
![11](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2004/20040918_ohlone/11.jpg)

The pump drill is more to Gabe's liking. It takes some effort to drag him away
from this eventually.

![12](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2004/20040918_ohlone/12.jpg)

While Gabe is drilling holes in small pieces of wood, Doug and I enjoy the best
event of the day. The native dances and songs are quite excellent. All in all,
a very nice way to spend part of the day.
