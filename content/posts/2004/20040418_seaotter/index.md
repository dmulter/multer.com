I talked the family into heading down to the [Sea Otter Classic](https://www.seaotterclassic.com)
in Monterey. I tend to overload them with bike stuff, but I'm convinced they'll
really enjoy this one. The drive down is very scenic. Should have rode down on
the bike.

![01](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2004/20040418_seaotter/01.jpg)

This mountain bike area was quite the collection of technical ramps and jumps.
These guys have amazing bike handling skills.

![02](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2004/20040418_seaotter/02.jpg)
![03](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2004/20040418_seaotter/03.jpg)
