We headed out to the boat show in Oakland again this year. It was plenty of fun
as usual, and I'm drooling over a new fishing boat called a [C-Dory](https://www.c-dory.com)
these days. After spending the night at Jack London Square, we headed down to
visit with George and his family. Charlotte went out for a hike with George
and Ellen over at Crystal Springs.
