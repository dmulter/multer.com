Charlotte took her dad Gordon down to Moss Landing to enjoy the day. I think
they had lunch over at [Phil's Market](https://philsfishmarket.com), which
makes me really jealous. They checked out the harbor and the vessels of the
Monterey Bay Aquarium Research Insitute ([MBARI](https://www.mbari.org)). The
institute does some amazing research and they get to play around with some
really great high-tech toys. Makes me think about those years long ago when I
considered a future in marine biology.
