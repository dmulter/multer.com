Gordon is taking my family out to the big lake today for a nice trip and some
fishing experiments. We're going to try for some salmon using the downriggers.
I think everyone enjoyed the boat ride. Monica and Gabe even had a turn
driving the boat.

trout. A fish always puts a smile on Gabe's face.
