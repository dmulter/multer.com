I've been taking Monica out for a long ride down to Wilder Ranch lately. The
rest of the family isn't quite up for that long a ride, but we decided to meet
up with them to ride along the San Lorenzo river bike path. This ride is the
big day when Monica makes it all the way back to the house. We've got a few
hills to conquer at the end of this ride. Go girly.
