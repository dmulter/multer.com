Charlotte has to have some of the most unique feet around. As far as I can
tell, she actually has 4 pinky toes on each foot. Maybe this is the future of
feet and we'll be seeing more feet just like these. I have heard that from an
evolutionary perspective, we actually only need one toe on each foot. She
doesn't appear to have any problem walking around on these beauties. It
doesn't look like any of the kids have inherited these peculiar digits, but we
may have to wait and see. I have of course come to love these very special
feet along with the rest of my wonderful wife.
