The babies are growing fast, but not fast enough with this nasty bird formula.
It really brings back the memories of the kid's formula days. Fortunately
these babies don't wear diapers. They are really funny when they eat. They bob
their heads a mile a minute until their crop looks like it's ready to blow.
They'll be feeding themselves really soon now.
