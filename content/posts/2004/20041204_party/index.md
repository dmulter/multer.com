The Silverback Systems holiday party was held at the [Plumed Horse](https://plumedhorse.com)
restaurant in Saratoga. It's pretty rare that Charlotte and I have a chance to
get really dressed up for such an event. It's also a rare opportunity to see
many of the engineers in something other than jeans and t-shirts. That's
certainly my standard attire in addition to the usual sandals.
