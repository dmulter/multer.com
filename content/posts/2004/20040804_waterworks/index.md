Another great close destination is the Calumet waterworks. We love to search
for Lake Superior agates and other cool rocks. You really want to go on
cooler, windy days to avoid the bugs.

![02](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2004/20040804_waterworks/02.jpg)

Charlotte finds a few old photos of local cyclists. I didn't run into groups
anywhere near this size when I was here.
