We just had to squeeze in a bit of fishing before Gordon heads back to
Michigan. The start of salmon season is the perfect excuse. I'm trying my best
to forget that I just did the [Tierra Bella](https://tierrabella.org) century
bike ride yesterday and have to get up at 4:30am for this fishing trip. The
trip out to the fishing spot takes forever, and it's unbelievably cold.

The trip back is a solemn one given the poor results. The guy next to me caught
three, George caught one, and I think 2 other people caught one. I had one on
the line, but the line broke near the boat. We had way too many crossed lines
and I lost my tackle two or three times. Let's just say it wasn't one of my
better trips. It was great going out with Gordon, George, and Charlie though.
It would have been really great if  Gabe    could have come, but I think it
was the right choice to not bring him.    Had to include a gratuitous shoe
shot.
