Charlotte took Valri and Gordon over to San Jose to visit the
[Rosicrucian museum](https://www.egyptianmuseum.org). They've been making
regular visits to a variety of local landmarks while their dad is in town. The
museum and library really are unique and well worth the visit. Monica's class
was just there on a field trip a week or so ago. Sure wish I could join in on
one of these trips sometime.
