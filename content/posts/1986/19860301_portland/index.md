It's a big transition time right now. The Boulder days are drawing to a close
with the shutdown of Burroughs, but Charlotte and I are getting closer every
day. I was invited out to Portland for an interview with Intel. They put us up
for the whole weekend, and luckily for us it was a beautiful weekend. Here's
Charlotte posing in front of the Columbia river at Crown Point. on our way to
the coast for the day. I can't believe we rented a Firebird.
