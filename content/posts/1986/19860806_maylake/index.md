Charlotte and Lynn headed out to Yosemite to hike in[Tuolumne Meadows](https://www.nps.gov/yose/learn/nature/tuolumne.htm).
They took a trail that went to May Lake. They're both working hard to see what
California has to offer in comparison to Colorado.
