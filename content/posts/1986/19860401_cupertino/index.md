We're all moved out from Boulder to Cupertino now that I'm working for
[Apple](https://www.apple.com). We found a nice house to rent near the mountains and
local park hiking such as Stevens Creek Canyon park. It's still close to work
for both of us. This is also the sacred shrine where Charlotte and I first
lived together.
