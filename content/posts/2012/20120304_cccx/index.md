It's so nice to have a great, local road race right in our area, and I think
Gabe is starting to really enjoy the course. He kept his position pretty well
coming into the finish, though the winner had quite a burst of speed to finish
cleanly in front of everyone. Gabe pushed hard all the way to the end to take
fourth place.
