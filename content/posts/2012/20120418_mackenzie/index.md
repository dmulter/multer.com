I just got back from a brief but wonderful visit with one of my oldest and best
friends who now resides in Napa, California. Yes, that does mean I have an
awesome excuse to visit the beautiful wine country just a short car ride away
from Berkeley. She attends the Culinary Institute of America in Napa training
to be a chef and baker! Yes, she is that cool.

![01](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2012/20120418_mackenzie/01.jpg)

She showed me the place where she gets to learn how to cook and make pastries
at the CIA and I was a little jealous. It is so different from the life I am
living at college here at UC Berkeley that it was refreshing and new.

![02](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2012/20120418_mackenzie/02.jpg)

It was so great seeing her because she is about to head out on an amazing
adventure to Nantucket where she will be doing her externship. Though I will
miss her dearly, I know she is going to have so much fun. I know our last
little visit sure was fun, including chasing giant rabbits, walks on railroad
tracks, long talks, awesome food, lots of laughs, and best of all swimming at
night in a pool with all our clothes on! So here is my little ode to Macky. I
miss you already and have a spectacular time in Nantucket.
