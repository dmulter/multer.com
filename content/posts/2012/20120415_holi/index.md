Yesterday at UC Berkeley was a celebration of the India holiday called Holi. At
Uc Berkeley and many other places this festival is considered a celebration of
the coming of spring. ANd who is it celebrated you might ask? By a bunch of
people gathered together in one place to throw handfuls of color at each
other.

![02](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2012/20120415_holi/02.jpg)

This is the before photo where we are all nice and clean. It didn’t last long
at all.

![03](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2012/20120415_holi/03.jpg)

And the after photo. Random people just throw color at you and try to get you
as dirty as possible. I think it is one of the few times in which someone will
be happy and thank you for throwing something at them.

![04](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2012/20120415_holi/04.jpg)
![05](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2012/20120415_holi/05.jpg)
![06](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2012/20120415_holi/06.jpg)

It was a lot of fun and I look forward to doing in the years to come here at
Berkeley. I think I still haven’t gotten all of the color off of me yet but I
regret nothing.
