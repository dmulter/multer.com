After a short reprieve from school to visit my home in Santa Cruz, I am back in
Berkeley.

Back to campanile escapades, rolling down hills, blowing bubbles out my window,
and for the rest of the summer, work. I am really excited to start working
this summer and see all of the things I will discover.

I am halfway through college and it hurts my heart to even think about that. A
lot has changed and I am starting out the last half of my college career in a
totally new vantage point. New jobs, new house, new roommates, new
responsibilities but the same school and a lot of the same people I have come
to know and love with all my heart.

So here is a toast to the second half of my college career, I hope it goes just
as well as the first two.

But I do miss Santa Cruz already. My two homes, how I love them so. So another
goodbye to Santa Cruz and another new adventure away from home.
