Summer is winding down to an end and with that is a serious of desperate
attempts to fill the dwindling amount of time with as many adventures as
possible. Today’s mini adventure was to the Saratoga Japanese Hakone Gardens,
the oldest historical Japanese tea Garden on the west coast. This small
expanse of tranquil land fashioned with Japanese Maple Trees, koi filled
ponds, and bamboo gardens is a luxurious little escape from the everyday.

![01](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2013/20130816_hakone/01.jpg)

On a clear hot day in the valley my mom and I explored just about every inch of
the Hakone Gardens. The cascading waterfall was my favorite aspect of this
little treasure.

![02](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2013/20130816_hakone/02.jpg)

A bamboo forest encloses the walkway with swaying bamboo giants being blown
back and forth by the wind.

The Hakone Gardens were a great little mini adventure to have. I really enjoyed
walking along the koi ponds and through the bamboo forest. Just one of a few
mini adventures to come!
