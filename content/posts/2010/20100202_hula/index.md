I saw you on the beach  dancing around with a hula hoop playing catch with your
dog. You seemed so happy and care free, you didn’t care who saw you or what
they thought. You were just there to have fun. I was a little worried you
would steal my dog, you seemed to like her a lot and wanted to play with her.
Sorry I couldn’t stick around keep up the good attitude. I hope to see you
again some time hula hooping on the beach.
