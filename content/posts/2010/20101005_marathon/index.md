Dear Friends and Family,

Hey everyone! I will be walking/running my first half marathon, next week in
San Francisco. This will be my first time participating in the Nike Women’s
Half Marathon. This event is to raise awareness and funds for the Leukemia and
Lymphoma Society.

![02](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2010/20101005_marathon/02.jpg)

There are so many people whose lives are touched by this illness and this is my
way of doing my part. I am writing to see if I might gain your financial
support. A personal donation or sponsorship from you at any level will be much
appreciated. I hope you will help me support this amazing cause. Thank you
all!

Checks should be made payable to:

**The Leukemia & Lymphoma Society**

Thanks in advance for your participation. Please let me know if you have any
questions.

Sincerely,
Monica Multer
