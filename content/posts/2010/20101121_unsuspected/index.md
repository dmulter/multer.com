This picture is at Pictured Rocks National Park in Northern Michigan. In a
place of such pristine beauty, were the sandstone rock walls stand like
monoliths looming over Lake Superior, there is wonder in everything. Most
people would expect a picture of these cliffs, but my favorite shot when I was
there, was not of the towering monoliths or the emerald green waters, but of
the forest hidden behind it all. People will tell you where to find the most
beautiful sights in places like this, but the real beauty is found in the
unspoken places. Down the little untreaded paths were no ranger or tour guide
will lead you. It is up to you to discover it and keep the little wonder a
secret in your heart. Find your own beauty, mine lies in the forest. Where the
trees are white as snow and the ground is red with fallen leaves and pines.
This is were I find beauty, in the unsuspected place.
