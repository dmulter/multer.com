We found a coyote laying down on top of a snow hatted rock that just looked
really amazing. We decided that we would wait for him to stand up on top of
the rock because the composition of the shot would be perfect. He seemed
pretty content just laying there though, trying to take a nap.

![02](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2010/20100319_yellowstone/02.jpg)

He really was beautiful and I almost missed him. I hadn’t even noticed him at
the time because I was watching another coyote out in the fields when another
person pointed him out to me. It is funny how easy it is to miss things. It
makes me wonder what else did I miss, what other wondrous creatures did I
pass by as I was consumed by some other thing?

![03](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2010/20100319_yellowstone/03.jpg)

He was watching us, it was amazing because you could almost see what he was
thinking as he eyed us warily.

![04](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2010/20100319_yellowstone/04.jpg)

His nice little nesting place did give me an opportunity to try out and
practice my newly learned photography skills.

![05](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2010/20100319_yellowstone/05.jpg)

He unfortunately did not work with us quiet as well as we had wanted. Instead
of standing up as we had hoped he would, he sort of nonchalantly slide off
the backside of the rock. We missed a possible great shot but still we did get
to watch him for a while. I wish he would have stood up though so I could be
showing you much better pictures. Oh well. Thanks Mr. Coyote for posing.

![06](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2010/20100319_yellowstone/06.jpg)
