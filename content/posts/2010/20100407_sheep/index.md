I don’t know why but in Oregon there are so many sheep! We passed by dozens
upon dozens of sheep flocks throughout Oregon. I must say they are some of the
most adorable babies. They resemble my dog…

We pulled over when I spotted this bunch of  sheep, they were too cute to pass
by. So I hung out the window to get these pictures of the little guys and
their mom. They seemed somewhat amused at the entire spectacle.

![02](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2010/20100407_sheep/02.jpg)

These two little cuties were the best. They were adorable but I have to say
they didn’t look very smart at all. They just sat there with their mouths
hanging open and staring at me. I guess it is warranted that a seventeen year
old girl was hanging out of a car with a camera pointed right at them cooing
about how cute they were. Either way they looked like a few screws were loose
up there and sadly that made them look so much cuter.

![03](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2010/20100407_sheep/03.jpg)
![04](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2010/20100407_sheep/04.jpg)

I think more farms in California should have sheep, it makes road trips so much
more enjoyable. I get tired of cows… seriously. Get sheep much cuter, much
more entertaining to look at. My mom and I were almost considering kidnapping
a baby sheep and putting it in the back of the car to take home. We decided we
shouldn’t though because Gracie would be jealous. One day though, one day I
will own a sheep and enjoy its stupid stare all day long.
