While in Yellowstone I was searching for Big Horned Sheep. We didn’t have much
luck because they always sat in the same place up on top of a mountain where
we couldn’t get good pictures of them. Finally on our last day though we
spotted a herd of big horned sheep grazing on a hill.

![02](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2010/20100315_yellowstone/02.jpg)

They were so interesting to look at, they really are unique creatures. It was
really fun to watch them chomping on grass.

![03](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2010/20100315_yellowstone/03.jpg)

I got to experiment with using the 600 lens some more while taking pictures of
these guys. I guess I owe it to them my learning more about using my camera to
its full potential. So thank you big horned sheep for your lovely
composition.
