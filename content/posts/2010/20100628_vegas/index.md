Day one of my journey to Michigan by car began today at 4:30. I miss the rest
of my family already and my dog. The first day of our trip ended in Las Vegas
Nevada. First thing I am going to say is this: **IT WAS 111 DEGREES OUT WHEN WE
ENTERED NEVADA!!**

![01](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2010/20100628_vegas/01.jpg)

I thought it being sunny was brutal but this heat was unbearable. I kept seeing
cars on the side of the road overheated and I felt so horrible for those
people and grateful at the same time because if we hadn’t gotten the new car
that would probably be us on the side of the road. Our trip out was relatively
uneventful but fun. I watched the Hangover in the car to prepare myself for
staying in Caesar’s Palace.

![02](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2010/20100628_vegas/02.jpg)

These hotels on the strip are so amazingly cool in my mind because last time we
went to Vegas we spent a majority of the time in an R.V. park. So in
comparison it is amazingly nice. Sadly I didn’t feel good at all for the first
part of our time in the Hotel. I had to get over feeling bad though because my
favorite part of Vegas was coming up. Eating at the Bellagio buffet. Sad I
know that I look forward to food so much but this buffet is the best thing in
the entire world. My biggest regret last time I was in Vegas was not getting
to  try the desserts because they looked amazing.

![03](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2010/20100628_vegas/03.jpg)
![04](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2010/20100628_vegas/04.jpg)

Naturally I fixed that this year by taking every type of desert they had and
taking one bite out of each. It was fun but to say that I was stuffed by the
end is a gross understatement. So much fun! We spent some time walking around
the Bellagio and checking out there cool gardens which consisted of animal
sculptures made from flowers and giant glass flowers.

![05](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2010/20100628_vegas/05.jpg)
![06](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2010/20100628_vegas/06.jpg)

The frog was my favorite out of the bees, lady bugs and snails. After the
Bellagio we went back to Caesar’s to do a little shopping. It has been a long
day and tomorrow is going to be a longer one. Stay tuned for more stories
about the rest of the trip!

P.S. I will be posting more elaborate stories on the parts of this day in later
posts when my schedule isn’t so hectic.
