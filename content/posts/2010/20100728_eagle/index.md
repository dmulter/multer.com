One of the most amazing things about Michigan is the majestic bald eagle. The
bald eagle is a frequent sight were I stay in upper Michigan and we love every
second of it. These birds are truly amazing and beautiful. I have to say
though they seem a little creepy at times because they are always so aware of
your presence. Whenever I go near them they always watch me and never take
their eyes off until they fly away. I love seeing them and for those of you
who don’t get to see them often, here is one of my best shots of him this year
for all of you to enjoy!

This is the eagle down on the point. He is always sitting in this tree watching
us no matter what time it is. He just took of when I got this picture and you
can still see his watching eyes.
