I saw you from the balcony at my hotel in Hawaii. I was actually quite
concerned because I had been watching the empty stroller for a good ten
minutes wondering where the baby was and why the stroller remained. I was glad
to finally see you and your baby. I just have one question, why take the baby
and leave the stroller outside? Why not take both?
