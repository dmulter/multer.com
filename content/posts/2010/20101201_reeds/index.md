At Natural Bridges State Park in California there is a spectacular little
lagoon that looks like the ground it is so filled with mosses. The entire
water is green and looks solid enough to walk on even though there are ducks
swimming in it. Life seems to spring from it, with long stretching reeds that
reach outward like arms and old logs reposing in the green water. There is
something about this green lagoon that is eerie and beautiful, maybe it is the
illusion, or maybe it is just the presence of life all around. I hope to go
back very soon and see it again.
