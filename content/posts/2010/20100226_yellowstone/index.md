Yesterday was a big coyote day, and today is going to be all about river
otters. Before we meet the otters, a few random shots from the hotel and
wandering about in the park.

ducks and a group of otters. They were too far for photos most of the time,
but we'll head back to Lamar Valley and their lodge to catch some good shots.
