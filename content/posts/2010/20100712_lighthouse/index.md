Right near our homes in Bootjack is White City, a park on the Lake Superior
with an amazing pier stretching out between the Portage Channel and the vast
depths of Lake Superior. There is an interesting lighthouse at the end of the
pier that some of my cousins like to jump off of; It is really high up, and
the water is really cold.

My cousin brought up KT and I got some good pictures of her leaping off the
lighthouse. She definitely had the best form and was the most graceful.


![02](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2010/20100712_lighthouse/02.jpg)
![03](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2010/20100712_lighthouse/03.jpg)
![04](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2010/20100712_lighthouse/04.jpg)

Others took a less graceful leap, like Doug with his flying squirrel style.

![05](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2010/20100712_lighthouse/05.jpg)

In the end the jumping style didn’t matter but the fact that they jumped. Soon
I am going to get over my fear and jump it too. Till then I will have to
vicariously live through my cousins.
