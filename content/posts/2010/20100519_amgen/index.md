It was a blast watching the Amgen Tour of California yesterday in Santa Cruz,
and today we're off to San Jose to watch the riders start from downtown. We
get a few glimpses of Horner and Hincapie, then Gabe camps out right in front
of the Saxo Bank team RV and lucks out. There's Andy Schleck, Jens Voigt, and
Fabian Cancellara. He's even got a signed tshirt to prove it!
