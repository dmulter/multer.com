Charlotte and I are off for a big road trip to various national parks. Our
first stop is [Zion](https://www.nps.gov/zion/). After a long drive from Santa
Cruz and a peaceful hotel night just outside the park, we head to the Angels
Landing hike bright and early. The only problem is that it's actually dark
outside when we start hiking. Let's just say we've got a few switchbacks to
traverse before getting to the top of Scout Lookout.
