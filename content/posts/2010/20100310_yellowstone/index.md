When I was in Yellowstone, I saw some of the most beautiful scenery in my whole
life. Valleys of snow and brush, trees lit by fading sunlight, and the bluest
sky you will ever know. Montana is called big sky country for a reason (yes I
know Yellowstone is Wyoming but we stayed in Montana). This tree was so pretty
even though it was barren, the sunset hit it in a beautiful light making
something dead look beautiful beyond words. I wish this photo could have
captured it better but here it is anyway.

![02](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2010/20100310_yellowstone/02.jpg)

This is  a grove of trees we camped out near when we were waiting to see the
river otters. It was so cool and a bunch of the trees had been knocked over by
beavers or almost gnawed down. Very interesting.
