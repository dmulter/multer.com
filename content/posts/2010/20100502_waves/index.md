Everyone as a kid was a wave runner. Playing tag with the in coming waves. It
makes me smile to watch a little kid doing exactly what I used to do. Dancing
with water on a sunny day. Sometimes you have to be reminded of what makes you
happy in life. I had forgotten how much I missed that and I have to thank this
kid for reminding me that you don’t have to be a little kid to be a wave
runner.
