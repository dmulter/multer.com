You guys made me smile. It was so clear you two loved each other and were
having so much fun together. You and your son were trying to walk with one
shoe shared and having a lot of fun doing it. It just reminds that there is so
much good and so much love between people even in the darkest times.  Yet
again, you still make me smile and for that I thank you both.
