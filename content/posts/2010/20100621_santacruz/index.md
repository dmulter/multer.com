It is an odd thing traveling across the country because I always find myself
looking for towns just like Santa Cruz. No matter where I go I always see
Santa Cruz in every place I go. I love Santa Cruz because it always feels like
home and I hope someday I find a place that makes me feel just as at home as
my first love and home, Santa Cruz.
