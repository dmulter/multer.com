I was watching you at a track meet the other day. I didn’t really notice you
until you crossed under the line where discus was being thrown and your mom
had to come save you from being hit by a discus. I watched you for a while
after that, you seemed very attached to you lollipop. You sat there watching
the discus like your mom but I could tell you weren’t really watching, you
were just trying to act like your mom. IT was very amusing actually. I hope
you enjoyed your lollipop and the rest of the track meet.
