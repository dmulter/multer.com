It seems as if all of the most childish and supposedly innocent things are the
most twisted. One of the most twisted is the kids game Twister. It seems so
innocent, but it actually is a perverse and awkward game unlike any other.
Hidden under the guise of childhood innocence, it is deviously deceptive in
its twisted ways.

![01](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2010/20101203_twister/01.jpg)

Case and point, need I say more? Yes, I do. The main problem about this game is
it’s total lack of boundary space. There is no such thing as personal space in
this game, with arms and legs crossing and criss-crossing across numerous
people. Nothing is sacred.

![02](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2010/20101203_twister/02.jpg)
![03](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2010/20101203_twister/03.jpg)

This game is meant for little children, so for teenagers it was slightly
difficult to fit on the mat making the space issue even more of a problem as
you can see. There is always something awkward waiting around the corner in
this game.

![04](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2010/20101203_twister/04.jpg)

The main way to win is to get into such an awkward position that your opponent
can not continue due to death by laughter.

![05](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2010/20101203_twister/05.jpg)

It just makes me wonder the people who invented this game, they must be twisted people indeed. The name twister is perfect for what this game actually is, a perverted and twisted thing. But damn is it fun to watch!
