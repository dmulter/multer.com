Yellowstone is a beautiful place, it can be both a snowy wonderland and a snowy
wasteland. While I was there I got to see both sides of the coin in numerous
occasions. At many times it would just be huge stretches of white nothingness,
empty and solitary. However at some moments you would see bright fields of red
brush or a flower poking through the snow. You never know which side you will
get, just cross your fingers for a wonderland where the color splashes across
the snow. You will find me there waiting, as always for those who seek out the
third path. Not black or white, but the colors that lie between.
