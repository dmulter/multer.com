I couldn’t really tell what your float was about but the people on it with you
were very interesting. Your mask was enchanting something utterly demonic
about it and at the same time it was almost childish. It matched your tattoo
very well on your arm. You seemed like a stoic protector over your float. Like
a silent ghost set down there, you hardly moved, just stared forward with your
mask unmoving. You captivated me, thanks for being there, and thanks for the
mask.
