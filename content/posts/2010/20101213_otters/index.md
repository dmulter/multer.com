Otters are definitely one of my favorite animals along with hedgehogs and
ferrets. They are beyond adorable and are so fuzzy and tranquil looking. I
have been trying for quite some time to get some halfway decent photos of some
sea otters since I already got some good river otter photos. I decided that
sea otters are much cuter than river otters. Sea otters just have such a
cuteness about them, and how can you not love an animal that floats around on
its back with stubby little arms.

![01](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2010/20101213_otters/01.jpg)
![02](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2010/20101213_otters/02.jpg)

I found this adorable little creature out at Moss Landing on my way down to Cal
Poly. I keep seeing them there and always forget my camera. But I came
prepared this time and ran over to capture his cuteness with my camera.

![03](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2010/20101213_otters/03.jpg)

It started to rain on him, he didn’t seem very happy about so he decided to
roll over. First he did this cute little fist pumping action with his arms by
holding them out from his body and moving them up and down. It was probably
one of the cutest things I have ever seen.

![04](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2010/20101213_otters/04.jpg)

Then he proceeded to roll over on to his stomach with his hands held against
his nose. It really looked like he was praying or something.

![05](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2010/20101213_otters/05.jpg)
![06](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2010/20101213_otters/06.jpg)

It just made my whole day seeing this otter. I love wildlife and capturing
pictures of it to show other people why I love it so much is worthy standing
out in the rain for however long it takes to get my shot. Lesson of the day
anyways, otters are unearthly cute and I want one as a pet. It makes me sad to
know I can’t have one, maybe some day I will befriend an otter and visit him.
Dreams, oh well. I love otters and if you don’t… there is something seriously
wrong with you.
