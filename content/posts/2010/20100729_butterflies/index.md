are so creepy yet so beautiful. It is this deceiving nature of the butterflies
beauty that I find horrifying just how easy it is to look at a butterfly and
fawn over its beauty only to realize just how truly scary looking and ugly
they really are. Here are a few more photos of butterflies that illustrate not
