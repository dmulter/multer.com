It's the time of year again for everyone that rides in Scotts Valley to raise
money for our local schools while enjoying our great mountain roads. This year
we've got a few changes. Charlotte got a lot of help from Aura, Mike, and
others in pulling off this great SVEF event. And this year Gabe joined me in
riding the 50km route. I'll miss doing Mt. Charlie with the usual crew, but
riding with Gabe is a treat and there's plenty of climbing to enjoy.
