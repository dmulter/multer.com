This was the first year I got to attend the Capitola Begonia Festival.

![01](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2010/20100906_begonia/01.jpg)

This first part consisted of the Sand Castle Contest. Some of them were quite
interesting.

![02](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2010/20100906_begonia/02.jpg)
![03](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2010/20100906_begonia/03.jpg)
![04](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2010/20100906_begonia/04.jpg)

My favorites where the giant great white sand castle, and the giant sand
sculpture of Poseidon and the BP oil spill.

![05](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2010/20100906_begonia/05.jpg)
![06](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2010/20100906_begonia/06.jpg)
![07](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2010/20100906_begonia/07.jpg)

This one was definitely the best, it had a message about the BP Oil Spill and
of course the quality of the sculpture was amazing. The eyes and teeth made
out of shells where so amazing and life-like. The seaweed oil spill was an
interesting and unique touch as well. It was the last one on the beach and
looking out over the whole crowd was quite a spectacle.

![08](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2010/20100906_begonia/08.jpg)

It was interesting looking at all of the people. It was a mob of beach goers
all looking and appreciating the work of these sand artists.

![09](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2010/20100906_begonia/09.jpg)

I am glad I went, Part two will be coming up next.
