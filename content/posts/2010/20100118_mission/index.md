I love visiting missions, there is always something magical about them. The
feeling of history. Knowing that this place held so much meaning to so many
people. There is always a hint of sadness, an emptiness that surrounds them. I
have posted some photos of other missions like Mission San Miguel and Mission
Hallways. This mission in San Juan Bautista is one of my favorites. Not just
because it has a red door, but because I went there as a kid. It was the first
mission I ever went to and I still loving going there.
