Yesterday was a great day of hiking in Zion, so this morning we're headed to
the Kolobs Canyon entrance for a hike on the Taylor Creek trail. The Double
Arch Alcove at the end was an impressive sight. I hope to come back this way
for some backpacking in the future. It looks like a fun multi-day hike across
the park.
