The first college we visited on our road trip through Oregon was [University of
Oregon](https://www.uoregon.edu). We toured around the campus while it was
pouring rain outside in Eugene, Oregon. We met up with Maddy, a first year
student of University of Oregon,  showed us around her dorm. I really enjoyed
it and was able to get a very good feeling of the school. I enjoyed it a lot.
After looking around we checked out downtown Eugene, which had an amazing
farmers/ craft fair going on which spanned over about three blocks. It was
full of all kinds of characters and talents. I really enjoyed it, even though
I was being rained on the whole time.

![03](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2010/20100403_colleges/03.jpg)
![04](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2010/20100403_colleges/04.jpg)

After Eugene we headed up to Salem Oregon to check out the tiny, but beyond
amazing liberal arts school of [Willamette](https://willamette.edu). This
campus is beautiful! I just wish I had been able to see it when it was full of
students. The buildings were gorgeous brick and were so interesting and old
fashion.

![05](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2010/20100403_colleges/05.jpg)

One of my favorite things were the white lawn chairs spread out around campus.
We visited an amazing library which had an overlook of the Willamette River.

![06](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2010/20100403_colleges/06.jpg)

The whole school had a feeling of tranquil genius :D. I look forward to
learning more about this school.

![07](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2010/20100403_colleges/07.jpg)

We then headed up all the way to Portland for a quick stop at [Lewis and Clark
University](https://www.lclark.edu). This school was so nice and quaint. I
really enjoyed it. We didn’t get to look around that much because we were so
tired, but what I did see was beautiful.

![08](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2010/20100403_colleges/08.jpg)

Tomorrow we will be visiting Reed in Portland and from there on out we are
winging it!
