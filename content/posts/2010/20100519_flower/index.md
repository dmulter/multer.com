This flower just looks like a big billow pillow to me. It looks so soft and
welcoming I really loved it. I love flowers if you can’t tell from my other
pictures. Flowers really just are fascinating because they remind me of
people. No one is exactly the same, the come in different shapes, colors and
sizes and each is beautiful in its own way.
