The Scotts Valley Grand Prix is a brand new NCNCA bike race we're very happy to
see come to our area. Charlotte and her Kiwanis buddies are helping with the
stage, food, and drinks.

a great Specialized presence, though it doesn't look like Larry was able to
make it to this race. A slight finish line malfunction made for an interesting
