I love living in Santa Cruz, the people, the culture, the lifestyles.
Everything about Santa Cruz is different. I love it because I enjoy people, I
don’t have to go far to find a character, someone interesting to talk to,
people who have lived life and want to share. There is so much potential here
and that is what I love. Art lingers beneath the surface of everything. This
is home.
