We started our morning out by going to Gonzaga University in Spokane
Washington. We walked around the campus and enjoyed checking out the church.
It was a pretty nice little school.

![01](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2010/20100806_seattle/01.jpg)
![02](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2010/20100806_seattle/02.jpg)

After that we decided to drive to Mount Rainer National Park.

![03](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2010/20100806_seattle/03.jpg)

After having a nice lunch overlooking Mt. Rainer we went out on a hike to
Nisqually Vista trail. It was an amazing hike through a meadow of wildflowers
and finally to an overlook of the Nisqually Glacier and the river that came
down from it.

![04](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2010/20100806_seattle/04.jpg)
![05](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2010/20100806_seattle/05.jpg)
![06](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2010/20100806_seattle/06.jpg)

Our next and final stop of the day was Seattle Washington. We went to Pikes
Place Market but it was closed for the day.

![07](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2010/20100806_seattle/07.jpg)

So tomorrow we are heading back before we go to Olympic National Park. It was a
long but mostly uneventful day. Tomorrow is going to be lots of fun and I
can’t wait to explore the Hoh Rainforest in Olympic National Park.
