My mom and I found a beautiful little niche in the woods full of rock piles. It
was astonishing. There were so many of them of all shapes and sizes. Even some
on tree branches. It amazed me because people took such care and time to make
this little corner of the woods into an amazing place. I just wanted to thank
those people for brightening up my day.
