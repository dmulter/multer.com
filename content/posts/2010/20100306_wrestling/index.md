Gabe joined the wrestling team along with Doug and a few of his friends this
year. He had a lot of fun with practice, but the meets were a bit too
stressful for him. I'm glad I got to see one of his meets before wrestling
became a thing of the past. Maybe one day he'll be back, as he's got some
natural ability.
