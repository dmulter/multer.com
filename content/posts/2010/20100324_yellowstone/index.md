While I was in Yellowstone, I met a new friend along the roadside. There was a
coyote trotting in the middle of the road when we drove by. He would not get
out of the road to let us pass. So we spent a good ten minutes with him. Every
time he would move to the side of the road we would make to pass him, but he
would dart out in front of us as if he was trying to keep us there. It was
very strange.

He was a beautiful creature but had clearly been in a fight, his ear was
mangled and bloody and had scars on his muzzle. I of course was hanging out of
the window trying to get a photo of him as I always do. My training on the
road trips to Michigan really paid off with my camera ninja skills.

We finally departed from our new roadside friend to go seek out more adventures
in the majestic Yellowstone.
