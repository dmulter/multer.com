You were an interesting person and made me a little sad. You felt out-of-place,
almost like you were heading upstream. You were headed somewhere but I do not
know where. Maybe it was the fact that you wouldn’t look anyone in the eye
that caught my attention. You just kept moving with your head held down and
tried to ignore the rest of the world around you. I wonder what you were
trying to hide from, who you were trying to block out. I wonder if it worked.
