We arrived at the North Rim yesterday, and this morning it's pretty cold, but
that certainly doesn't hinder any of the beauty of this place. Charlotte and I
hike around the edge of the canyon, but we don't have a lot of time today as
we've got a long drive up to Bishop for our last night of the road trip.
