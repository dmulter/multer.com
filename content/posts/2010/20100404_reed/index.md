Today we started our day with my favorite college of the visit, [Reed](https://www.reed.edu).
The campus was beyond gorgeous with old brick buildings and mossy surroundings.
We walked all over campus from the old dorms, to the new dorms, from hall to
student center, and all around.

![02](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2010/20100404_reed/02.jpg)
![03](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2010/20100404_reed/03.jpg)

We stopped on a bridge to talk with a freshman Reedie to get the down low about
the college. She said she loved the small classes and the way students got to
explore all avenues of their passions which is something that I am really
interested in. I really liked this school, I can’t wait for more information
in the future. I mean seriously the school had a swing set.. I am a sucker for
swings 😀

![04](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2010/20100404_reed/04.jpg)

Next was [Linfield](https://www.linfield.edu), a school out in the middle of
nowhere on the way to the coast from Portland. Yet again, another beautiful
school. We didn’t get to spend that much time but we got to look around and it
looked very nice.

![05](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2010/20100404_reed/05.jpg)

That is it for colleges we visited on our trip but all of the little adventures
we had in between have yet to be shown. Stay tuned for the rest of our trip. A
sneak peek of something we did, a stop at the Multnomah Falls in the Columbian
Gorge.
