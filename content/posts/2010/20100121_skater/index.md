I saw you walking around in the crowd with your buddies and your skateboards.
You joined the crowd to watch the waves crash on the rocks. The waves were
huge the other day but you hopped over the railing anyway. You went out onto
the edge even though it was dangerous. You just sat down on your skateboard
and watched. You were interesting, why was it so important for you to be right
out there next to the water when the waves were crashing so close. I bet you
got water on you. I had to leave though so I never got to ask, maybe I will
next time I see a skater-boy wave watching on a stormy day.
