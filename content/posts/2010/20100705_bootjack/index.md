Today was our last day on the road. We finally arrived safe and sound, just
slightly busted up (me) ready for fun in Michigan. We started out our day with
a nice ride along the Lake Superior coast in Ashland. We passed some really
cool old dock pilings out in the water mostly covered by fog.

![02](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2010/20100705_bootjack/02.jpg)

We are tired but very glad to be at our home away from home with out family. I
am glad to have the library and the time to relax finally.
