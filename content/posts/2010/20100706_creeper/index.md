You were adorable. It is amazing to see how such a simple thing as a spraying
water fountain can amuse and bring happiness to someone. You were having so
much fun trying to grab the water as it shot up. You seemed to have so much
fun even when you saw that the water simply slipped away because you knew in a
few seconds more chances to catch it would arise time and time again. It was
refreshing to see you having fun even though you never really could succeed.
