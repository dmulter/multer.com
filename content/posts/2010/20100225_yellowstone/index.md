We spent a good chunk of the day camped out watching a few coyotes in this
field. One of them was sleeping on a snow covered rock, while we waited for
him to wake. He looked really comfortable. He then treated us to a nice
successful hunting display. He really seemed to enjoy himself playing with the
rodent. I suppose if I had to work that hard for my food, I would savor it
much as he did.
