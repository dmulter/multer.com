It's our last day in Yellowstone as we head into the park once again. The
antelope finally make an appearance at the entrance, as well as a hungry raven
that keeps us company while we eat our lunch.

![02](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2010/20100227_yellowstone/02.jpg)

off a fish he just caught.
