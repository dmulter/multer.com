At first I wasn’t sure what you were doing but then I saw the little wooden cow
sculpture in your hands. You were making a wooden carving of the cow
sculpture. I wonder what made you choose this sculpture as your inspiration.
It seems a little bit strange to have a sculpture as the model for a
sculpture, but who am I to judge. You were interesting to watch as you marked
the places on your little cow that needed work and more carving. You were so
serious and intent on your work, it is nice to see an artist so committed to
their work. Keep it up cow man.
