You were definitely my favorite outfit that I saw at the Lady GaGa Concert. You
basically only had on underwear and caution tape. Not including the sunglasses
and the soda cans in your hair. It was amazing to see how willing people where
to bear all in front of a massive crowd of strangers in the name of their
dedication to a musical artist. Whatever your motivation behind this outfit, I
enjoyed it. Also, kudos for putting yourself out there, maybe it would be best
to do it in a slightly different way than walking around basically nude.
Still, kudos 😀
