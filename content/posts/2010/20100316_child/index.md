The moon is framed with reaching hands  
Holding out waiting  
To cradle the child’s leaning head  
When sleep hangs  
On the starry eyes  
Of Midnight’s child  
We will be waiting  
To catch you  
As you fall  
To wrap you up  
In lights warm embrace  
To cradle the fragile  
Fluttering heart of a baby bird  
We will hold you tight  
Until it is time  
To let you go  
Until your heart  
Grows strong again  
So close your eyes  
Child of the night  
We will cradle you  
As you sink  
Past the point  
Were you can  
Re-open your eyes  
Lay down your head  
In a cradle of hands  
We will hold you  
We will keep you safe  
Until midnight comes again
