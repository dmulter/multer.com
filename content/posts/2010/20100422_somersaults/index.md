I was watching you at the beach doing somersaults. You seemed so serious about
it though. It wasn’t a game I could tell. To most people a kid doing
somersaults is a whimsical thing, but with you it was different. There was
nothing really childish about it. I wonder why it seemed so serious a thing,
maybe you aspire to be a professional gymnast one day. It made me a little sad
to se you though. Have fun sometimes, it isn’t all serious business. You were
at the beach, be a kid and have some fun playing in the sand. Good luck in the
future and don’t forget the present on your way.
