Oh wolf eyed lover  
Look into my eyes  
And know  
I am not your enemy  
I am a part of you  
A dark soul  
To complete  
Your hollow entity  
Dance with me  
Oh wolf eyed lover  
Under the dying moon  
As the deserts burn  
And the ashes settle  
Sway with me  
Like the deserts  
Treasured flower  
Let the moon  
Bear silent witness  
As the earth stands still  
Oh wolf eyed lover  
Howl to the moon  
When I am gone  
Call me back  
When it has been  
Far too long  
You won these lands  
The barren deserts  
And forsaken lands  
Oh wolf eyed lover  
Don’t you see  
It is no longer  
A dog’s world  
But mans  
We are dying company  
Hunted and alone  
We can’t fight them  
Off forever  
Together we stand  
Oh wolf eyed lover  
The deserts dying flowers  
Float gently to the ground  
Where the ashes have settled  
And there is no longer sand  
Here we face the moon  
As our last stand  
Where once we danced  
Is now a deserted land  
Oh wolf eyed lover  
Can’t you see  
The world has moved on  
And left us behind  
There is no more room  
For happiness and love  
Only survival  
In these hard times  
Oh wolf eyed lover  
Don’t you understand  
This is the end  
The separation  
Of you and me  
Has finally become reality  
Our entity once whole  
Ripped to pieces  
To be no more  
Oh wolf eyed lover  
I will wait for you forever  
Turn on eye to the desert  
And one ear to the wind  
To listen for your cry  
Carried by the moonlight  
I will dance with the moon  
Wishing it was you  
Oh wolf eyed lover  
Save me a grave  
Under the cactus tree  
Where flowers fall  
And the moon watches  
As the dust finally settles  
Together we lay  
As it was meant to be  
Separated by life  
Brought together by death  
Oh wolf eyed lover
