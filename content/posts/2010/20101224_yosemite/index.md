My mom and I on our road trip to Yosemite saw many interesting things and
actually had a lot of luck with wildlife on the ride up, however you will have
to wait for those stories. Yes I know I am evil, lets just call it a
cliffhanger, high-five, then walk away.

![01](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2010/20101224_yosemite/01.jpg)

We were hoping there would be lots of snow in Yosemite but there was
surprisingly little. There was a lot of interesting fog and mist that added
another dimension to my photography. It provided an intriguing experience to
experiment with photography. It was breath-taking, the beautiful sites all
over the park that made both of us sad that we only two days in total for our
trip. We made the most of it though, running around after wildlife and chasing
reflections in frozen rivers. It was a lot of fun and the little dose of
adventure that I was yearning for.

![02](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2010/20101224_yosemite/02.jpg)

I decided that instead of just getting normal landscape shots of Half Dome that
I was going to try to get a new spin off of the original photos to spice up my
photography a bit. My idea rested on a hunt for Half Dome’s reflection.
Finding just the perfect spot on the river where Half Dome’s beauty could be
found was tricky but a fun challenge.

![03](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2010/20101224_yosemite/03.jpg)
![04](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2010/20101224_yosemite/04.jpg)

I think it worked splendidly. From Half Dome we moved onto Yosemite Falls. We
didn’t have time to go up to the upper falls so we settled for just the lower
ones. The rainbow being cast off from the falls was magnificent.

![05](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2010/20101224_yosemite/05.jpg)
![06](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2010/20101224_yosemite/06.jpg)

We could at least watch from afar the majesty of the upper falls that cascaded
down the cliff’s face.

![07](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2010/20101224_yosemite/07.jpg)

My mom was helping me by being my camera assistant the whole time. Helping my
swap out lens and helping out with the tripod. So a little shout out for my
best travel buddy, my mom, and my lovely camera assistant. Love You!

![08](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2010/20101224_yosemite/08.jpg)
![09](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2010/20101224_yosemite/09.jpg)
![10](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2010/20101224_yosemite/10.jpg)

It was a really fun little trip and I hope to go back soon and get even better
photos. Other photos from our  adventures in Yosemite and the road trip up
will all be coming soon!
