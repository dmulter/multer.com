I just loved your hair, it had such a life of it’s own and really complimented
everything about you. I saw you inside and didn’t get a picture of you and I
was really sad but as soon as I walked outside there you were just sitting
there eating your lunch overlooking Mt. Rainier. It was perfect. The wind kept
throwing your hair around and making the red really light up. Anyways I just
wanted to tell you, I love your hair and don’t change it.
