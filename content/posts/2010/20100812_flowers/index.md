My mom and I decided to dub North Dakota the sunflower state because all along
the road we were driving on where rows and rows of sunflower fields. They were
dazzling, just miles and miles it felt like were filled with the yellow faces
of sunflowers. It really helped brighten up a long drive, because who won’t
smile at a field of sunflowers.
