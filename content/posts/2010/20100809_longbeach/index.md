Yesterday we stayed over night in Aberdeen Washington in a nice location on
Heron Street. It was very aptly named because in the morning behind the hotel
on a really nice water way there were tons of great blue herons flying back
and forth.

![01](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2010/20100809_longbeach/01.jpg)

I love looking at these majestic great blue herons but I have to say, blue
herons make one of the most ugly calls I have ever heard from a pretty bird.
It is like the sound of an animal being strangled and it is just so guttural
and loud. It is a very gross sound for such a nice bird.

![02](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2010/20100809_longbeach/02.jpg)

After that we started heading south along the Washington coast all the way down
to Long Beach Washington where we were going to meet one of my good friends
Mackenzie and her family who stay in a cabin there sometimes. It was a huge
but wonderful coincidence that we were both there at the same time. We got to
hang out and go around Long Beach looking for adventure. Our adventures
started with driving on the beach; Long Beach, Washington is one of the
biggest driving beaches in the U.S. with 26 miles of beach driving.

![03](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2010/20100809_longbeach/03.jpg)

The beach was really nice but it was a little nerve-racking to drive on a beach
with a brand new car. We really didn’t want to get stuck. I enjoyed it a lot
because I had only been there once in eighth grade when Mackenzie took me
there in the summer for a week. It was so strange being back there after such
a long time and visit the places I had about four years ago.

![04](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2010/20100809_longbeach/04.jpg)

We headed over to Oysterville after the beach drive to take a look at the
massive piles of shucked oyster shells. It did smell a little but it was so
cool to look at just mounds and mounds of oyster shells.

![05](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2010/20100809_longbeach/05.jpg)

It was great to see my friend and after that we headed down to Ashland Oregon
where the Shakespeare festival is held. It was a beautiful city and we ate
right nest to a creek. It was a long day, but our last on our road trip back
home from Michigan.
