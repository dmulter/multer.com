I find myself standing downhill sometimes and feeling that the way to the top
is so very far. When you stand at the bottom of the mountain everything else
just seems so much bigger. Everything gets out of perspective and it is during
times like these that we need to remember that things can seem out of your
hands but nothing ever truly is. You just have to be up to the task. You have
to start walking up the mountain at some point. It will be a long and hard
journey, but the top will be insight eventually. Laying at the bottom of the
hill doesn’t make it seem any better and won’t get you any closer to the top.
So get up and climb. Let the journey begin.
