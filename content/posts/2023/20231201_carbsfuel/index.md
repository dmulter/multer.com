Gabe has finally figured out they key to satisfying his love of cycling and
entrepreneurship at the same time. He's launching a new sporting nutrition
company he's called [Carbs Fuel](https://carbsfuel.com). The concept is pretty
simple, give people the energy they need while exercising with the simplest
ingredients and the best price. Just give me carbs!

Now the execution is pretty complex and Gabe's attention to detail is totally
key to actually pulling this shit off. I got a chance to see the manufacturing
of the new gels in person. A pretty amazing process and very exciting to watch.

Gabe's first big event is at the [Sea Otter Classic](https://www.seaotterclassic.com), the
biggest cycling event in North America. Charlotte and I are here to help and
provide support. Starting a new business is always such a blast.
