An always popular destination when family or friends are in town is Carmel.
There's loads of shopping, art, food, and great beaches. I normally would have
to include golf, but given the scarcity of anyone that can play in the family
prevents me from mentioning it. There are a few very cool trees at the top of
the hill above the beach. A few of these are great shots.

Carmel has one of the few white sand beaches that I know of on the entire
coast. These aren't quite the warm east coast beaches that Ira and Karen are
used to, but our kids are totally at home. Nick is having a lot of fun burying
Monica, while Gabe and I comb the beach for interesting bits.
