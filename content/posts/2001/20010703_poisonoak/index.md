Back east the stuff to watch out for is poison ivy. Out here in Santa Cruz, the
nasty stuff is poison oak. All you have to do is rub up against a leaf or
branch and the oil gets on you or your clothes. From there you can spread it
all over the place without even knowing it. We're not sure where Nick got it,
but he clearly rubbed it into his face. Time to break out the cortisone. Poor
guy.
