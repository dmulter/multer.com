We had Thanksgiving at Val and Kim's house this year. George, Ellen, and their
family came down for the night as well. Here Kim and Gabe enjoy each other's
company for a moment. Then Gabe collapses from the effort.

Meanwhile the girls work hard on getting everything ready. Val, Charlotte, and
Monica are a great team.

Caroline and Zachary are getting ready to go after a great Thanksgiving at
their Aunt Valri and Uncle Kim's house. Zach is in a bit of a daze, maybe it
was too much stuffing.

Lindsey, Caroline, and Zachary head back to our house for the night. Nick and
Lindsey, Monica and Caroline, and Gabe and Zach pair off for what will
probably be a stay up late sleep over. Charlotte and I will try to stay at our
end of the house and let them have some fun.
