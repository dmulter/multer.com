We decided to meet up with George and his family a little further north this
weekend. Low tide at at the James Fitzgerald Marine Reserve at Moss beach is a
great adventure. We first had lunch (from the [Garden Deli](https://sanbenitohouse.com/garden-deli-cafe) of course)
at the picnic area and then headed down for the beach. There's also a very
popular restaurant nearby called the [Moss Beach Distillery](https://mossbeachdistillery.com). It's a
pretty interesting ghost story if you want to check it out. Anyway there are
some good shots of Lindsey, Zachary, and Caroline as well as our kids Nick,
Monica, and Gabe.
