Nick, Monica, and Gabe all attended Parent Participation Preschool (PPP) in
Scotts Valley. They all went for one or two years before kindergarten. We all
built great memories of the times spent at school, but the best memories for
the kids were clearly their friends and their teachers Kathy and her son
Nathan. We're very sorry to see that the school has closed, but it was a great
time while it lasted.
