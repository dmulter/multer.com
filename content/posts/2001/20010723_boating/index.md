I try to take one of the boats out on the lake everyday. The little metal boat
is always good for a quick trip for fishing, driftwood collection, or just a
ride. Sometimes we take the canoe if it's not too windy, and other days it's
the Sailfish if the wind is just right. A bit of wind today.

![01](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2001/20010723_boating/01.jpg)

Meanwhile the wind makes for great fun swimming in the lake. Nick, Monica,
Charlie, and Joe play in the waves. Hopefully the wind will keep picking up
and then it's really a blast.
