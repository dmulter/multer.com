It's a really fun, short boat ride over to Houghton. We typically tie up right
downtown and then hang out and maybe have lunch. The ride can be a bit cool,
and you have to keep an eye out for the weather. The kids of course love the
ride. On a warm day it's fun to jump off the boat and go for a quick swim in
the middle of the lake. You can get a glimpse of one of the [MTU](https://www.mtu.edu)
buildings on the south shore of the channel.
