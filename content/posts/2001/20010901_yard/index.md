Charlotte has been putting a ton of work into both the front garden and the
vegetable garden in the back. She restocked the river bed and put in a bunch
of new plants and mulch. It's nice to see things looking so good.

![01](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2001/20010901_yard/01.jpg)

Meanwhile the vegetable garden is really kicking into full gear. The tomatoes
are rolling and the corn is looking very tall.
