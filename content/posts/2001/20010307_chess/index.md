The Hoyems are over for a visit and Zach has challenged Gabe to a chess game.
Gabe makes the first move and can't contain his glee. Zachary looks very
concerned about Gabe's move. He tries hard to recall that particular
grandmaster opening. There's nothing quite as impressive as the ability of
these young champions to look dozens of moves ahead.
