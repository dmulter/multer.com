With grandma and grandpa staying at such an awesome beach, we have no trouble
getting the kids to visit frequently. Nick, Monica, Gabe, and Joe explore the
beach and the rocks. It's a little cool today, but that's Santa Cruz.

There's a basin carved into the cliff behind their house. It's a great place to
climb around. It fills with water at high tide and is home to all kinds of
great creatures.

![11](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2001/20010413_sunnycove/11.jpg)

The house itself is a lot of fun as well. Nothing like a new house to run
around in. It's also a great place to work on Easter eggs. Our house has great
acreage, but the view from this place is beyond belief. It has to be one of
the best locations in Santa Cruz.
