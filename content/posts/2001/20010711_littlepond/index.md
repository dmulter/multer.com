The little pond is at the edge of Valri's property by Bootjack Road. We often
ride our bikes, or the golf cart over for an easy adventure. There are loads
of frogs, tadpoles, and small fish in there and we never tire of catching
them. It's messy going, but Valri looks very prepared in her waders.
