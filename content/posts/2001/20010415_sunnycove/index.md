Our friends Kim and Pete and their kids Lily and Jack join us for fun at Sunny
Cove. The basin behind Gordon and Charlotte's place is a big attraction.
Occasionally we have to take cover when a big wave comes over the cliff.

temporary home for them for the day. I'm sure they'll be happy when it's time
for us to go.
