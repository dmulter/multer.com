We had a huge crop of pumpkins this year. The kids wrote their names on them
while they were still small. As they get big, the writing scars over and it
gets larger. A very cool trick. Selecting your pumpkin while it's still on the
vine is a big deal for each of the kids. With the corn stalks it looks almost
like Sukkot.
