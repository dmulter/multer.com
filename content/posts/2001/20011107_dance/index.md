It was a big day at Brook Knoll today. All the classes were performing a
variety of dances for everyone. Here Monica is a cowgirl doing more western
dances than I had ever heard of. She was occasionally having a little trouble
with her clogs falling off, but she did fantastic as usual. We never get tired
of it of course.

the boys are a little less enthusiastic about dancing with girls. Give him a
few years and let's check again on what he thinks.
