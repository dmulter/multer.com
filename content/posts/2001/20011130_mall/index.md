Gabe and Doug look very happy hanging with Santa at the Capitola mall. It's a
pretty rare thing for us to be at the mall, let alone do the Santa's lap
routine. I have to say though, that it made a big impression on Gabe. Doug
looks like he's enjoying it a whole lot too. Gabe's also sporting his favorite
spike hair style just for Santa. Not a lot of snow outside though.
