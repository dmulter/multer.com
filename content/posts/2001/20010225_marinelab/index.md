There's a local marine lab we like to visit when we don't want to do the drive
down to the Monterey Bay Aquarium. The [UCSC Long Marine Lab](https://seymourcenter.ucsc.edu) is a quiet
little place in west Santa Cruz with a nice selection of displays and
interactive exhibits. Another nice day in Santa Cruz.
