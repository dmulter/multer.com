Charlotte and Gordon are spending a month or so in Santa Cruz. We arranged an
awesome house to rent at Sunny Cove. Valri, Renee, and Charlotte swing by to
see how things are doing, and to catch a few rays.

![01](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2001/20010318_sunnycove/01.jpg)
![02](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2001/20010318_sunnycove/02.jpg)

I think Charlotte is saying thank you, but I'm fine. Meanwhile Gordon looks
like he's settling in very nicely. I bet he's thinking about when it will be
time to go fishing. I'm ready when you are Gordon.
