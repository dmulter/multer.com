We headed north today to check out the old town of Redridge in Houghton.
There's a very cool old dam, though I didn't get any pictures. We hung out at
the site of part of the old copper works. Lots of fun and dangerous stuff to
climb all over.

There's also plenty of old mining garbage like this tangle. I thought it was
kelp like back in Santa Cruz, but you certainly won't find any seaweed in Lake
Superior. Turned out to be a tangle of wire. Pretty interesting.

![09](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2001/20010718_redridge/09.jpg)

After a long day of fun in the sand and water, Gordon and I got the traditional
lunch ready. We cooked blackened potatoes and lots of hot dogs. Gordon throws
the potatoes right into the fire. You peel away the blackened portion, load it
up with salt, and enjoy.
