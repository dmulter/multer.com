We met up with George and his family this weekend. The rendezvous point for
this part of the day was a place called Maverick's. If you're any kind of
surfer, I'm sure you've heard of this spot. Normally you don't think of Half
Moon Bay as a surf mecca, but some of the biggest waves on the planet roll in
here and it attracts only the most fearless. There's even a [book](https://www.amazon.com/exec/obidos/ASIN/081182652X/102-2322051-5290508) on
the place.

We didn't bring any boards, but there's more than enough fun anyway. It's kind
of a long walk out to the beach along the north side of Half Moon Bay. We used
to live not far from here, but amazingly we never got to this beach. George
told us about it and off we went. It's pretty gray and cold as usual, but
nowhere near as bad as getting into the water.
