This summer the kids all attended soccer camp. It really was a lot of fun even
with the huge number of kids all playing at the same time. Nick, Monica, and
Gabe each played in separate groups. There was a fair amount of waiting on the
sidelines for your turn.

![03](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2001/20010627_soccer/03.jpg)
![06](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2001/20010627_soccer/06.jpg)

Once a group was called out, there was no shortage of running around. The
coaches did an awesome job coordinating this many kids.

![04](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2001/20010627_soccer/04.jpg)
![05](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2001/20010627_soccer/05.jpg)

And when they got tired of playing soccer, there was always playing in the
river. That's quite a pack of little ones with Gabe on the verge of getting
very wet.
