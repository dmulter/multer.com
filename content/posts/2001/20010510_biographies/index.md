Monica had a school biographies project where she played the part of Hellen
Keller. She looks so pretty with her hair up. I've also included a number of
her friends: Colleen as John Muir, Heidi as Pocahontas, Hudson as Lincoln,
Justin as Edison, and Ocea as Eleanor Roosevelt.
