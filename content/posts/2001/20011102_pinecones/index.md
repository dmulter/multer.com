Gabe is having an awesome time painting pinecones. It's a beautiful day and
there's nothing finer than making pinecones even more beautiful. It's kind of
like doing a coloring book. There are so many little facets you can paint all
different colors. Gabe wants to be an artist one day like his uncle Scott.
