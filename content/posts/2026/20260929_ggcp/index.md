Today I had a great hike in Golden Gate Canyon State Park. I started on the
Horseshoe Trail up to Fraser Meadow. First I have to say - I love, love, love
Fraser Meadow in the fall for the aspens, and the middle of winter for the
snow beauty. Then I went to the Mule Deer Trail up to Black Bear Trail and
back.

I was lucky to see a bull moose first thing. He surprised me coming right down
the trail, so I took a quick picture while I scrambled up off the trail to get
away from his path.

I ended up on top of one of my favorite overlooks to the back range. It was
pretty stormy looking, so you couldn’t see the mountains well.

I enjoyed being back in the higher country so much. Great day!
