We get some pretty rip roaring storms up in Bootjack. Other times we just get
some great winds and waves that make for fun watching. Every once in a while
there's a great mixture of sun and storm that gives us a spectacular full
rainbow. I'm pretty sure there's a pot of gold, or at least a nice school
of walleyes at the ends of this baby.
