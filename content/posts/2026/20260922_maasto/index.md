Some of the best trails in the area are up in Hancock at
[Maasto Hihto & Churning Rapids](https://www.cityofhancock.com/recreation-maasto.php). It's getting to the
end of our time in Bootjack, so I'm trying to get in a few more hikes with
Charlotte before we leave. This area is pretty awesome for year round hiking,
mountain biking, and skiing. It's certainly making me think of bringing a
gravel or mountain bike up next year.

The trees and plant life are so enjoyable, though it seems we have to watch
out for overly aggressive goshawks. Luckily we made it out alive.
