Usually I get dropped off for a bike ride on the Eagle River Cutoff, but today
I'm hiking with Charlotte. The Monk Trails take us along pretty paths that
lead down the Jacob's Creek gorge to the waterfalls and the [Jampot](https://poorrockabbey.com).

The Jampot is literally jammed with people, so no brandy-soaked treats for us.
It's back up the gorge and over to the ruins around the old Arnold mine. The
monks left us lots of cute little benches to enjoy on our stroll through the
woods.
