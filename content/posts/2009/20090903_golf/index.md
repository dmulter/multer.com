So here is another incident after the Ghosts and Stupidity, there will probably
be many more like this.

Today during golf practice I was wheeling around my golf bag on one of the old
man bag cart pushers. On hole five I was pushing it in front of me when the
bottom caught the ground, stopped dead and hit me right in the neck.

**Funny? Yes. Painful? Yes. A really stupid moment? Definetely.**

Not only did I jab myself in the throat with the handle, but I made the most
ghastly noise when it happened. It was a mixture of a dying cat and what I can
only assume to be the llama mating ritual noise.

**YES IT WAS THAT BAD.**

Worst part, almost everyone heard the noise. Everyone just turned around and
was like What the heck was that? I just laughed and laughed. No I am not an
idiot, I am just unmindful of my surroundings, namely old man golf bag
carriers.
