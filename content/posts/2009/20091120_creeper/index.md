The boy without a pet, how sad. Every kid needs a little animal to love and
cherish for however short amount of time that creature is destined to spend
with them. This picture makes me sad yet happy in a way. The poor kid just
looks like he wants that little hamster so badly. He has the look of a kid who
comes here everyday just to stare at that hamster. Not any hamster mind you,
but that hamster in particular. I bet he named it too. I hope one day he gets
his hamster and it is all he hoped it would be.
