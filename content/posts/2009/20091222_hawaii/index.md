Today we're having lunch at the [Mauna Kea Resort]
(https://www.maunakearesort.com) before heading over to Hapuna Beach for some
fun in the surf. Lunch is always great at the Mauna Lani, but it's nice to do
something different. The waves are great for body surfing, but I wish I had my
boogie board from back home. It sure is nice not wearing a wetsuit for a
change.
