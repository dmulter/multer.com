Lynda and I are off for a hike out near Lancaster this morning, before I head
back to California. It's been a great visit and I'm sorry to go, but this is a
nice way to spend the day before a long flight. The Middle Creek wildlife area
is a bit of a trip, but I see why Lynda enjoys making the drive out here
regularly.
