Gabe's previous track meets were practice for the real thing today. He's had
plenty of opportunity to get used to throwing the 1.6kg discus, though he
really was looking forward to breaking the area record for the 1kg discus. Now
he's got a new goal to keep him motivated. Today he got first with 67 feet and
is aiming for the record next year.
