My new gecko friend, I named him Phillip. I spotted him on my way to go on bike
ride around our hotel, the Mauna Lani in Hawaii. This little guy is so cool
looking, my favorite part of him is his eyes. And of course, his little toes.

These geckos are all over the place but I got really close to this one. I hope
to see you again Phillip, but then again I probably won’t even know it is
you.
