The prologue stage in Sacramento was pretty awesome, but today the race is
coming to Santa Cruz! I'm pretty torn about seeing the race on the climb up
Bonny Doon, but the local Peloton Club was a great way to support and get
involved with the race locally. So it's off to downtown to enjoy the finish
along with a ton of other people. The local bike fair was a nice change from
the usual walk downtown. If only it was always like this.

After the race, downtown was still closed to traffic and was filled with people
and bikes. What a glimpse of how great it would be if downtown did this on a
regular basis. It was so much fun to see bikes take over even for a short
while. Thanks again to Matt and everyone that helped bring the Tour of
California here to Santa Cruz. I'm really hoping next year will have Santa
Cruz again, so I can try other ways to help with the effort.
