Pike Place Market in Seattle really was an extraordinary experience. There were
so many people, so many colors, and so much life. People just offer you food,
sing and laugh, it really is a place to see. There will be many more photos
from here coming up so stay tuned.

Oh and a lady walked into the back of a car and whipped out. Funny but sad.

Here are some color pictures of the market. The first is a dried pepper chain,
(mom bought one) and of course the classic fruit line up. I can’t wait to go
back again.
