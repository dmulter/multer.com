The camera’s eye  
Swivels and rotates  
Focusing and unfocusing  
Trying to capture you  
In your purest form  
Or your darkest moment  
To swallow you whole  
In the blink of an eye  
It is watching you  
With it’s great eye  
Extending and retracting  
The glass reflects  
Back on you  
It reaches out for  
Your sprit  
Your very soul  
That brilliant shine  
The smile  
Always on your lips  
The laugh  
Bubbling out of you  
And the life  
That never dies  
Now lives on  
In the camera’s  
Unforgiving eye  
That swivels and rotates  
As it watches  
Be careful  
It is always watching
