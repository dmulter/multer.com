This is Arnold, he was the baby raccoon we found in Michigan.

His mommy died so we gave him a home and fed him until he was old enough to
live on his own.

I miss him. He lived in the Peyser household. He is still out there somewhere.
He was so cute and small.

I was just thinking about him and wanted to wish him well where ever he is.
