Today we're headed south to check out the volcanoes. There are a few funny
sights along the way, but overall it's just a really long drive in dreary
weather.

![20](20.jpg)
![21](21.jpg)
![22](22.jpg)

[Hawaii Volcanoes National Park](https://www.nps.gov/havo/) was pretty cool,
even if we didn't see any red hot magma, and it was raining, and everyone was
grumpy after the long drive. It sure was one big smoking hole in the ground.
The walk through the lava tube was definitely very cool. It reminded me of the
copper mines in Michigan.
