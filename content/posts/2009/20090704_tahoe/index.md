Charlotte and Monica have fully loaded and decorated the car for the long drive
to Bootjack. The Ritchey is riding on the back, while my road bike (her name is
Sweetness) enjoys the luxurious interior. Time to load the dog and hit the
road.

First stop is the [Jelly Belly](https://www.jellybelly.com) factory near
Sacramento. I know they've bought at least a few bags of belly flops.

After checking in at the hotel in Truckee, it's off to Lake Tahoe for a run
around the [Tahoe Rim Trail](https://tahoerimtrail.org). Plenty of people
lined up for the big fireworks display, but the girls are a bit too tired, and
there's another long day ahead tomorrow.
