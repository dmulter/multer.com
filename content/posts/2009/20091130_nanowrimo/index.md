This year I found out about NaNoWriMo which is National Novel Writing Month,
where writers try to hit a word goal for a novel they are trying to write in
month. Sadly I found this program halfway into the month so I wasn’t able to
really truly do as well as I wanted but I still hit my word goal so
technically I won but I definitely do not have a novel. So here it is, my
award. I guess.
