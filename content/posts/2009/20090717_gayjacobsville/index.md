Parade, and other sites. I often ride my bike through this tiny little town,

It's been a really long day in the saddle, as you can see from the huge salt
stains on my arm warmers. It was a great ride out to Copper City via Brockway
Mountain Drive, and back through Lac La Belle and Gay. Can't wait to put in
some quality recovery time.
