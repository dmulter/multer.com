Another great day awaits in [Yellowstone](https://www.nps.gov/yell/) after
yesterday's visit. Mark has been wanting to get a great photo of some coyotes.
These two may have given him his opportunity, but yet again they are a bit too
far away for my lens.

![01](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2009/20090220_yellowstone/01.jpg)
![02](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2009/20090220_yellowstone/02.jpg)

We did a beautiful snowshoe excursion out to the fork of the Yellowstone and
Lamar rivers and spotted some fresh wolf tracks. I got my first nice closeup
of a raven that waited patiently for any scraps we might accidently leave from
our lunch.

![03](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2009/20090220_yellowstone/03.jpg)

A cold wind was blowing really hard today. I tried to catch an image of the
blowing snow, but no luck. It's still a really beautiful scene.
