Nick and I just got in from Califorina after a good amount of excitement. I
just finished up with a week of backpacking in Alaska you can read about in
previous stories. You can probably tell there's still a little bit of stubble
on my face. I figured it was pretty novel, and the family would enjoy it. Nick
did not enjoy the flight out here given the drama of our departure. I flew
through SFO a few too many times and headed to the wrong airport this morning.
Let's just say we had a very fast car trip down to San Jose, but we just
managed to make the flight. Now that was a little stressful.
