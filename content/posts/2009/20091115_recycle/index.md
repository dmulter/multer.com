You know you are in Seattle when one room in a building has four different cans
for recycling. I mean seriously, that is a little absurd. I am all for
recycling but four? Really, four?

This is in University of Washington for those wondering where this phenomena
occurred.

I guess this post is for my dad, the recycling man of our house. I guess we
don’t have it so bad, because he could have four different cans for us to
recycle with. Don’t get any ideas.
