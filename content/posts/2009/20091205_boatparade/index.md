Charlotte, Gordon, and I decided it was about time we went to see the annual
[Santa Cruz Lighted Boat Parade](https://scyc.org/lighted-boat-parade) down at
the harbor this year. We bundled ourselves up, grabbed a little food, and
camped out on a bench to enjoy the show. I headed out to the end of one of the
docks to get some photos. They didn't come out great, but I do like the
colors. The highlights had to be the hula dancers on the outrigger, and the
kayaks. It was also pretty funny when the end of our dock started to sink with
all the people crowded on the end.
