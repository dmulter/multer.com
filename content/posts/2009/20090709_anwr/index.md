We hiked our hardest day yet in rain to the pickup spot. That night the wind
and rain really picked up, so we huddled down in our tents waiting for Dirk to
arrive. There's not a lot to do during 21 hours in your tent trying to stay
warm and dry. I figured it was a good opportunity to make some strange faces.
It's been an awesome trip, and I'm excited to think about the next time I head
to Alaska.
