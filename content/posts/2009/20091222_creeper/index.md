I must say this image sickens me slightly. The fact that this little girl,
maybe four years old, is dressed in high heels and black spandex tights. Is it
just me or is something wrong with this picture? I don’t understand how
parents could dress, or let her dress like this.

Not to mention it was in Hawaii, it was so hot out I don’t understand how she
could wear this without overheating.
