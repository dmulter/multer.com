This was a tree my mom and I found on the side of the road on our way to Bend
Oregon for our trip back from Michigan. We passed it and had to turn around
and go back because it was so cool looking. I have seen telephone wires coated
with shoes, but never a tree so covered in them. The shoes were like leaves
there were so many. I would have hung up my own shoes but all I had were my
new shoes and sandals. Very cool.
