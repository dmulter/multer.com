I'm joining Matt, Mark, and Emily on their trip to [Yellowstone]
(https://www.nps.gov/yell/) this winter. I haven't been here since a
backpacking trip to the Grand Tetons and Yellowstone with Charlotte many years
ago. Charlotte visited with the kids on their last drive to Michigan, so now
it's my turn. We flew into Bozeman and then drove down to the hotel in
Gardiner. It's a bit gray out this morning, but I'm looking forward to my
first day in the park.

![01](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2009/20090218_yellowstone/01.jpg)
![02](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2009/20090218_yellowstone/02.jpg)

Matt and Mark are pretty serious photographers, so I'll be doing my best to
improve my skills on this trip. Hopefully there will be a noticeable
improvement over the course of my trip. This morning we start in Mammoth Hot
Springs trying to catch the drifting steam.

![03](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2009/20090218_yellowstone/03.jpg)
![04](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2009/20090218_yellowstone/04.jpg)
![05](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2009/20090218_yellowstone/05.jpg)

The calcification deposits are very cool up close.

![06](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2009/20090218_yellowstone/06.jpg)
![07](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2009/20090218_yellowstone/07.jpg)
![08](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2009/20090218_yellowstone/08.jpg)

Each day we'll be driving between the north entrance in Gardiner and the
northeast entrance by Cooke City. It's the only road that's open during
winter. We got our heart rate up hiking down to the Gardiner river from the
Sheepeater Canyon bridge just outside Mammoth.

![09](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2009/20090218_yellowstone/09.jpg)
![10](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2009/20090218_yellowstone/10.jpg)
![11](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2009/20090218_yellowstone/11.jpg)

A few nice views from the road. I'm looking forward to breaking out the
snowshoes soon.

![12](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2009/20090218_yellowstone/12.jpg)
![13](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2009/20090218_yellowstone/13.jpg)

We'll be seeing lots of Bighorn sheep and bison on this trip, along with elk,
coyote, wolves, eagles, and more. By the way, it is as cold as it looks.
