Wire teeth  
Frame a smile  
Pulling up corners  
Like a curtain  
Let the show begin  
Feather plumed lips  
Fall into a pale face  
With cheekbones of glass  
Each jutting out  
Ragged yet beautiful  
Stained glass eyes  
Gleam in refracted shards  
Color shines through  
Empty yet bewitching  
A gate into a hollow soul  
Spider web hair  
Brushes her face  
In the breeze  
Shifting and changing eternally  
Soft as silk  
And fine as diamonds  
Here you are  
The portrait girl  
They love you for  
Your eyes  
They love you for  
Your smile  
They love you for  
All the wrong reasons  
Until you die inside
