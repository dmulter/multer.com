Last year's anniversary in Napa was so much fun, we're heading up to the same
area again this year. This time we're off to the Sonoma valley area right next
door. It's a much smaller valley than Napa, which makes for an even nicer
trip.

There's nothing as nice as a big hike after a few hours of driving. We drive up
to [Sugarloaf Ridge](https://www.parks.ca.gov/?page_id=481) state park just
north of Kenwood. After a bit of confusion with the map and ranger directions,
we head up to Red Mountain.
