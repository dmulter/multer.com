Alright remember my post, Creeper Photo: I Got Creeped? I must say I was quite
sore about having been so scared by the Tiki Guy, so I decided I had to get
revenge on him by scaring him back. My plan was to scare him somehow and it
worked perfectly.

I snuck up behind him while he was lighting the torches and used my flash on my
camera to blind him. He got really scared and surprised. My revenge was
complete, except for one little detail:

**It wasn’t the same guy**

Apparently they switch off people who light the torches. I did not know this,
so sorry for flashing you with my camera.
