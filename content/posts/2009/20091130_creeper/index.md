I guess you got married today, congratulations. You waited in line like
everyone else with all your bridesmaids around you. I wonder how it feels, did
you feel free or did you know this might be your last moment of childhood
leaving you behind. I loved your roses and I bet your wedding was beautiful. I
wish you a good marriage and good luck because not everything will be a
carousel ride.
