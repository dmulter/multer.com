I think it is about time to start thinking about what I really love. I have
been writing since fourth grade. On my birthday during my freshman year I
received a book. A nice leather bound, four hundred page journal for me to
write in. I decided that what I was going to put in it were two things, one:
Poetry, two: Book Ideas.

So since my freshman year I have been writing in this little book here, and the
two year mark is coming up on my seventeenth birthday. I am almost done with
the book, I have fifty pages left in it. I never thought I would actually get
this far with my writing. I never imagined that I would have my own blog, but
here I am. I am inching my way toward what I believe is my future.

So here is what I am trying to say. I want to make a book out of my poetry. I
have already started self publishing it on my blog, but I want a real book
full of my work. I don’t know how or when, but I wanted to share with all of
you this idea, that I soon hope will become a reality.

If anyone knows anything about publishing please inform me, because I don’t
really know where to start except to have my writing.

What comes next? I am not sure, probably more work.
