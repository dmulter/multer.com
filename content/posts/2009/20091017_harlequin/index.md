title = 'Harlequin’s Lullaby'
author = 'Monica Multer'

Dark hands  
Reach for you  
Suffocating, drowning  
But yet you see  
There is nothing  
I can do

Water rushing up  
All around you  
A dark embrace  
Never letting go  
Just another puppet  
In a marionette show

Puppets dance  
Jointed dolls twirl  
Then fall  
Broken limbs, empty eyes  
A shattered face  
A ruined disguise

Strings plucked  
Dark hands stream  
The chords of your soul  
Any moment to break  
To fall back to earth  
Just the devil’s stake

Frowns pulled  
Into fake smiles  
Masks drift towards you  
Just people turned puppets  
Never ending nightmare  
Nothing to do to stop this

Marionettes twirl you about  
Just pathetic toys  
For a dark twisted God  
Torn from the world  
Into this evil façade

Dark hands  
Empty eyes, broken masks  
Sway on stage  
An empty abyss  
A song rising  
Can’t you hear it  
Drifting on the wind

Puppets sing, scream  
Do you hear  
Their desperate cry  
This my dear  
Is the  
Harlequin’s lullaby
