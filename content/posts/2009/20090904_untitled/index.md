A tall tree  
Like a sentry guard  
A world full  
Of controversy  
And falling stars

Yelling and screaming  
You tear out my hair  
Betrayal afoot  
A friend afraid  
To cry  
Shattered pieces  
Wings are torn

Pictures of friends  
Ripped to pieces  
Wake up  
And smell the ashes  
There is blood  
To be had

Once friends forever  
Are no more  
A promise never upheld  
Sadness builds another wall  
Forming a shell  
Hiding away from all
