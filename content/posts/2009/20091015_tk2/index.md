I've been wanting to pickup a new bike to use at the velodrome for a long time
now. I finally spotted a great deal on a barely used Felt TK2 someone in Santa
Cruz was looking to unload on Craigslist. I jumped all over that action. Now
my current road bike (Sweetness) has someone to keep her company. Can't wait
for the next trip down to the velodrome.
