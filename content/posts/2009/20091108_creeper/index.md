This is my favorite creeper photo I have ever taken. It was in the airport on
my way to Seattle. Her hat was what caught my attention. There is just
something sad and mysterious about her that I love. It made me wonder why she
was going to Seattle and who she was. She has the honor of being my first
creeper photo.
