You didn’t seem very happy for this carousel ride, you should be because there
are only so many times in your childhood that you get the chance. One day you
will grow up and wonder where your chariot has gone, where your carousel ride
went off to. Part of me thinks it was a show for your father. Playing the
spoiled kid, the one who doesn’t appreciate what you are given. I could see
that somewhere inside of you, you did though, you loved it. You cheered up as
you went around. Enjoy it princess before your fairy tale is gone.
