It was a fun day hiking the Sonoma valley area yesterday. Last night we spent
our anniversary at the [Kenwood Inn](https://www.kenwoodinn.com), thanks to a
recommendation from George. It's a beautiful spot, and there were no shortage
of couples enjoying their own getaways.

look around, and found herds of sheep keeping the grass clipped. Each area had
one of these gorgeous sheepdogs keeping watch. What a total cutie!
