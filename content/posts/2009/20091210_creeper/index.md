You were so cute on your bike. You were trying so hard to stay ahead of
everyone. I don’t know if your face was sunburnt or maybe it just looked red,
but you were putting so much effort into it. I congratulate you on that but
what made you so bent on trying to be first? Maybe you are just trying to get
your training wheels off, but still, the effort is what counts. Keep it up
kid.
