A blossom bloomed  
Opening slowly  
Shyly  
Showing it’s new found beauty  
Opening to the sun  
Reaching up high  
To the bright blue sky  
Not knowing  
That the sun  
Doesn’t always shine  
When the rain comes  
The blossom wilts  
Hiding its beautiful face  
Turning from the pain  
The heavy drops  
Weigh it down  
Pulling away  
It’s beauty  
Petal by petal  
Until nothing is left  
The blossom  
Lays broken  
Shredded on the ground  
In its place  
A bud pokes through  
A new chance  
A new face  
A new beauty  
As a blossom blooms
