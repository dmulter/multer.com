Cream in coffee is good for my mom because she can get her caffeine and sugar
high at the same time, I like it for photography. Same difference….. well
maybe not. I feel like I should have gotten murdered in Seattle because I
don’t drink coffee. It almost seems mandatory when you visit, let alone live.
What can I say, i just don’t like coffee. Looks like marble to me. Thanks
Starbucks for the shot.
