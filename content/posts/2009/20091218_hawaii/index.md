This afternoon I'm heading up to the top of Mauna Kea to visit the
[observatories](https://www.maunakeaobservatories.org) and get in a little
observing on slightly smaller scopes just below the summit. It's a crazy long
drive to the top at over 13,000 feet, and the upper parts are all gravel. I
didn't see any invisible cows, but I knew Monica would love this sign. I
thought the very unique Silver Sword plant was quite interesting. It only
grows around the visitor center and higher elevations. I also got my first
view of Mauna Loa here above the clouds.

Our timing is perfect as the sun sets for the day. The big Keck dome opens for
a busy night of observing I'm sure. Once the sun is down, we head down a few
thousand feet to setup some scopes for an observing session of our own. I can
see why everyone builds their observatories up here, as it's the clearest and
darkest skies I have ever seen. It's amazing how many stars you can see as
people once could long ago.
