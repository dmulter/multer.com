Some where out there, is the world’s largest sand crane. I have seen it with my
own eyes, got to say slightly disappointing. I was expecting truly gigantic,
this was big, but not that big. The sign under the statue cracked me up
though.

Steele, North Dakota I believe, the middle of nowhere. Still now I can say I
have seen the world’s largest sand crane. Beat that.
