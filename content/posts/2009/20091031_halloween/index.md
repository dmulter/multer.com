Gabe and Doug are planning an early Halloween around the neighborhood in an
attempt to bring in a big haul. Gabe's also decided to go as a Playboy bunny.
I'm not sure he really looks like one, but he sure does pull off the young
girl look look nicely. The bunny mask really just makes me frightened.
