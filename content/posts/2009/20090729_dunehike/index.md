Lindsey and Monica are two girls you don't want to mess with while they are
online.

![01](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2009/20090729_dunehike/01.jpg)

The plan today is to drive all over the Keweenaw and check out some new places
to hike. First stop is the Cliff Mine, a place I often ride past on the bike.
After a few initial water obstacles, we find lots of rock scrambling and old
stone foundations.
