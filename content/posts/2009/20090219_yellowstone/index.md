Yesterday was a great day in [Yellowstone](https://www.nps.gov/yell/), and
today we're off for another day of driving and photography. We pass our first
big herd of elk on our way over to Mammoth. Sure wish I had the big
telephoto.

The cascading hot spring terraces pose for some nice photos. This is part of
the lower terraces area.
