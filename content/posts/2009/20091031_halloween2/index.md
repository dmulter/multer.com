title = 'Halloween'
author = 'Monica Multer'

tags = ['poetry']
+++

Don your masks  
your alter egos  
it’s time for the dance  
a dark waltz  
full of mischief and grief  
hide your face  
and smile anew  
the night is yours  
no light lies between you  
no boundaries  
just a moon  
and a cry  
to keep the night alive  
grinning faces  
with twisted smiles  
children running  
tricks to be played  
under the night’s watch  
for a night  
you are who you truly are  
when the masks go on  
they finally are off  
let out your darkness  
to share with the world  
close your eyes  
pull down your veil  
it’s time to play  
let out a howl  
it Halloween day!
