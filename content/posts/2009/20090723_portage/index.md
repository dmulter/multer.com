Today we're heading over to Houghton via Portage lake and the channel. It's a
lot more enjoyable than the usual drive to Lake Linden and around. A whole lot
shorter as well. The view of the old mining operations from the channel is a
whole lot better as well. Time to tie the boat up and grab some lunch.
