I love how in college, people become so dependent on bikes. If everyone in the
world did what college kids do (well a majority of college kids) then the
world would be a whole lot greener. People need to stop trying to just
advertise everything as green and just get out and do something about it.

Advertisement gets you no where, action gets you everything. So people… ride a
bike, take the bus, carpool, recycle and all that good stuff.
