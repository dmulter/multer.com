You were sitting in a hammock with your boyfriend at the beach. You looked so
peaceful, I had seen you walking around with him, standing on the tide pools.
You two looked very happy together. You might have been sleeping but I wasn’t
sure. I wonder what brought you here to Hawaii, maybe your boyfriend was going
to ask to marry you here, or maybe you came here to get to know him better.
Whatever your reason I hope your stay went well.
