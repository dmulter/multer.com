Here we are, yet again without a purpose for writing this. I am feeling rather
morbid and ill contented so bear with me. I just had kind of a crappy day.
Why? I am not really sure.

I hate it when you feel like you had a good day but in the end you look back
and say,

**What the Hell, that sucked.**

That is where I am right now. Individual divided up, my day was alright. I had
some fun with Nooshers in Math, and fun but time consuming day in Art. But
here I am now thinking to myself,

**Why am I in such a bad mood?**

I really don’t know, maybe it has to do with being tired. When I am really
tired I can either be a really funny person to be around or a miserable one.
Sometimes I get really hyper and pumped up because I am just so tired I act
the exact opposite, or I am just such a downer and a whiner nobody wants to be
around me. Yeah, that pretty much explains my life.

**Being Tired Sucks**

That is all I am going to say about that. I am tired of being tired all the
time.
