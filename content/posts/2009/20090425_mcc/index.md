This year is the seventh running of the most excellent SVEF Mt Charlie
Challenge. I've been doing it every year, even before the current route up Mt
Charlie. It's always a ton of fun for me and for Charlotte, though she puts in
a ridiculous amount of time to pull the event off. Charlotte, Paula, and Sally
really anchor the whole effort, and it's always worth a big thank you to them
for making this the best fundraising event for SVEF for the past seven years.

We've got about the same size field again this year. Scott's Valley's finest
lead us out for the ride up Bean Creek. Now this is always quite the treat.
I'm tough to spot in there, but think of it as one of those Spot Waldo
puzzles.
