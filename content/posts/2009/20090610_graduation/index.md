Today is a very big day for Nick as he graduates from [Scotts Valley High
School](https://svhs.scottsvalleyusd.org) and moves on to the next big things
in his life. Grandma Phoebe is here visiting from Pennsylvania for the big
occasion. Seems like a good excuse for some family photos.

There's a great procession of speakers today, all giving great advice to all
the students as they move on to their next big things. Antares Yee and Johnny
Jih shared the valedictorian recognition. They get the award from me for the
best speech, as they shared the tale of their close friendship and plan to
share this recognition from the very start of their high school years. Very
cool.
