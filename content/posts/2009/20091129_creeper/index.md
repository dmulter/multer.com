You were really nice and let me take a picture of your dog. Your husband scared
me because he said I would have to pay a dollar to take a picture. that is
why I normally don’t ask for permission to take these photos but you seemed so
nice so I had to ask. Your little puppy was so cute all bundled up in your
shirt. You said she was tired from playing on the beach. Her owner loves her
very much, it makes me happy to see love like that even if it is a tired love.
It is nice you know you can sleep safely in the arms of a loved one. 😀
