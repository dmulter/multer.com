Love truly is blind, this woman in Pikes Place Market was singing on a corner
and was truly blind. There was something really sad about this, yet she seemed
so happy. Maybe she just loved blindly enough that she didn’t need to see, she
could just believe. Some times it is better to live that way. It is easier to
believe blindly than have to stare the truth in the face everyday. I wonder
which kind of person she is, a believer or a seeker.

Which are you?
