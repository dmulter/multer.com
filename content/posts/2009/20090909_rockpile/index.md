One of my hobbies is making rock pile art. It is a lot of fun but can be
frustrating at times. Especially when there is a pack of wild little children
Hell bent on knocking them over every time you build a new one!

But that isn’t the point. I make rock pile art all over the place. Just
spreading a bit of man made beauty.

These are some rock piles that I made on my family’s trip down to Las Vegas.
Fun idea, not such a success in reality. But hey, we tried it, that has to
count for something doesn’t it? We did see some cool things on our way but
five people in a R.V. is just not for me. Say what you want but I would much
rather go in a car than an R.V.
