So today is my birthday and I am seventeen years of age. This is a shout out to
all of my friends who gave me things, hugged me, and said happy birthday. I
love all of you!

Basically two of my best friends gave me a boat load of balloons, literally. I
think there was like fifteen. They swallowed me and everyone who was brave
enough to come within a five meter diameter of me. (yes I said meter because I
think American measurements are stupid).

Anyway, I have a ton of sweets that my family are quickly devouring as I write,
and today was a great birthday thanks to everyone.
