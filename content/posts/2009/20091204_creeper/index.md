You were watching the sun set over the pier. You looked like a man waiting for
something. What I do not know. You seemed content in your isolation, everyone
was so happy around you but you were all alone sitting in your chair watching
and waiting. I don’t know how you sat there staring at the sun, I couldn’t it
was so bright. What were you looking for on that horizon. Whatever it is I
hope you find it out there somewhere, someday. Just don’t stop looking, one
day you will find it but it might not be what you thought you were looking
for.
