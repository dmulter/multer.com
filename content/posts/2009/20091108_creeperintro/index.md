So my little brother had the great idea to have a section for Creeper Photo of the Day because I take so many. So starting today there will be a new photo every day of some random person that I have never met but took a picture of without their knowledge of me doing so. A warning, none of these are meant to be stalker photos or anything. These photos are solely of people I found fascinating in some way or another and wanted to remember them for it.
