You seemed upset, maybe you missed your ride home, maybe your boyfriend stood
you up. You were just standing there next to the road at a bus stop. There
were lots of people but you stood alone. Maybe your anger isolates you. Or
maybe you just wanted to be alone. I wish I had asked. I like your scarf by
the way.

Maybe I will write a poem about you, if so I will post it later so you won’t be
alone.
