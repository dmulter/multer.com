I had the pleasure of meeting an extremely talented African Gray Parrot at
Pike’s Place Market. On a street corner a man was performing with his bird. It
was amazing. He could play dead, be thrown up into the air and do a 360, meow,
and do flips on your hand. Truly incredible. I even got to hold him.

Just one of many weird things we encountered in Pike’s Place Market. Here is a
