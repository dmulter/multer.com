Tonight is the SVHS senior awards event. It's a bit of a mystery as to why we
received an invitation, as we don't know what his awards will be. It turns out
to be a great event honoring all the seniors that stood out through academics,
community service, and athletics. It's great to see these exceptional students
recognized for all their years of hard work. Nice job Nick! It's also good to
see a bunch of Monica's friends helping out at the event.
