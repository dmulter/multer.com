A Bar Mitzvah is a major right of passage for any Jewish boy. It's also a great
excuse for a party with all your friends and family. It also entails a huge
amount of preparation. All my years of Hebrew school built up to the study for
my Torah and Haftarah reading in front of one huge congregation. Services were
held at the [Suburban Temple](https://tbtwantagh.org) in Wantagh, NY.

![01](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/1975/19750215_barmitzvah/01.jpg)
![02](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/1975/19750215_barmitzvah/02.jpg)

Rabbi Robert Raab and Cantor Walter Lewis were both there to point out any
mistake I might make. Reading Hebrew is hard enough, but they leave out the
vowels in the Torah. And trying to sing the Haftarah must be quite a laugh for
those in the audience.
