Another stormy day, so we head indoors for some rock climbing at the
[Pacific Edge](https://pacificedgeclimbinggym.com). The kids love clambering
around on the bouldering section even if it doesn't go very high. Gabe refuses
to stop climbing even when he gets tired. I have to pull him off a few times
when his arms and legs start shaking. Man this is fun.
