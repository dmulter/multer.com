Charlotte has been looking forward to selling her wares at the church craft
fair this weekend. Manufacturing kicked into full swing in preparation for the
event. Plenty of bath salts, essential oils, and a whole collection of eye
pillows and such. We're not quite sure what will sell best.

![01](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2002/20021018_craftfair/01.jpg)
![02](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2002/20021018_craftfair/02.jpg)
![03](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2002/20021018_craftfair/03.jpg)

Charlotte got a table way in the back, but we're hoping the fragrance will draw
them in. Monica is spending most of the day with her mom to help out.
Everything sure looks good. Her neighbors are sporting quite a few crosses.

![04](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2002/20021018_craftfair/04.jpg)
![05](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2002/20021018_craftfair/05.jpg)
![06](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2002/20021018_craftfair/06.jpg)
![07](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2002/20021018_craftfair/07.jpg)

In the end, the fair was a great success. Charlotte sold about half of all the
product she brought, and was the number two seller for the entire fair.
Hopefully next year she'll get a better location.
