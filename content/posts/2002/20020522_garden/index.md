Charlotte's got quite a crop planted this year. Lots of tomatoes, lettuce,
eggplant, corn, peppers, zucchini, green beans, peas, pumpkins, and more.
We've added a few more planting boxes this year. Charlotte's also done a lot
with filling in the paths and adding lots of flowers. We also added two
apricot trees, and some berry vines. This is also the only place we can grow
roses with the deer in the area. Can't wait to start harvesting.
