Before the startup bug consumed me and all my free time, I loved road
bicycling. Its been quite a few years, but I finally made the commitment to
make it part of my life again. I was amazed at how quickly I was able to climb
the mountains again and get back the strength and endurance I built over many
years of riding. I'm almost up to riding every day again and I'm feeling
great.
