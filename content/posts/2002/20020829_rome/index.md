I've been to quite a few places around the world, especially Europe, but I was
really looking forward to my trip to Rome. I was headed out to Italy for a
standards group meeting for the Open Mobile Alliance. I won't bore you with
what went on at the meetings, but unfortunately they chewed up most of my free
time. I did manage to get a full day away to check out the city, but of course
it had to be raining that day. Who really cares about getting wet anyway.

I had a taxi drop me off somewhere in the city, and I then proceeded to do my
new city tour. I just start walking and don't stop until I'm exhausted. I then
grab a taxi back to the hotel. I walked along the Forum and headed for the
Colosseum first. Those diagrams of the Roman empires boundaries have always
looked impressive to me.

![18](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2002/20020829_rome/18.jpg)

Here's one of the many plazas I walked past. You'll notice I never remember the
names of these places. I tend to think it's the experience that matters and I
usually blow off the rest. The streets are very cool, and I really enjoy the
aimless wondering.

![19](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2002/20020829_rome/19.jpg)

The Pantheon was an amazing building. The dome is tremendous and I love the way
it's open to the sky. The plaza in front is a great place to hang out as
