Another favorite trip when we're up in Bootjack, is driving somewhere on the
northern shore. There are lots of great rock beaches for agate picking. The
black flies tend to be much worse, but you just have to stay alert. Those
suckers will take a pint of blood without you even knowing. Here Gabe is
having some fun by the waterfalls.
