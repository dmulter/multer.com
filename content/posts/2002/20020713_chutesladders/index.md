here's a place in Houghton called Chutes and Ladders. It's an awesome
playground we take the kids to sometimes. It has a nice little beach where the
you can see Gabe enjoying himself. Charlie looks like he's ready to leave
though. That's the Ranger in the background coming back from a trip to
[Isle Royale](https://www.nps.gov/isro/).

What you can't see unfortunately is the huge slide structure that is the basis
for the place's name. It's a huge towering set of ladders climbing to the top
of 3 or 4 long slides. It's well worth the climb. The beach is a good cooling
off break after sliding for a while on a hot day.
