Gordy sent out the call for all kids to head for Houghton. We were heading for
the surplus store for an unspecified mission. Charlie was to captain our
trustworthy vessel under the watchful eye of master seaman Gordy. I'm not sure
what's behind Gabe's expression, maybe he had his mouth open and a big
mosquito flew in unexpectedly.

There were plenty of fun things to check out at the store. I had a load of fun
in the hats section. Time to stock up on the fur hats before winter comes.
