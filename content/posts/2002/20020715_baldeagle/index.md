The past few years we've seen an increasing number of bald eagles up north.
There's a pair of adults with a whole pile of adolescents. They appear to hang
out in a specific tree just a bit south of us. Here are a few shots of one of
the young eagles. He let us look for a little while before he decided we were
a bit too close for comfort.
