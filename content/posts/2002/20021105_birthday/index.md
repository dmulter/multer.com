After a traditional Multer birthday night at for pu-pu platter at the local
Chinese restaurant, we head home for presents and cake. Monica picked out a
cookies and cream ice cream cake that was so good, I'm sorry we got it. The
presents were a big hit as well. Charlotte made her an awesome new blanket.
She also got the sheep dog stuffed animal she had been longing for, the
Spiderman movie, some software for her new PC, and jewelry. Nick and Gabe got
a few party favors as well. The slinky was a big hit with Gabe.
