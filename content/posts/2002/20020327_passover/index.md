Our family unfortunately didn't make it out to aunt Carole and uncle David's
house for Passover this year, but someone was kind enough to forward these
pictures. In other words, don't complain about the picture quality. Looks like
we missed out on a bunch of fun. John and Joni came down, and my dad
(Ira), Karen, and Max came from North Carolina. Even my sister and her family
came including Steve, Kyle, and Rachael. I love the shots of Karen nodding
off. What a bonehead, we should have gone.
