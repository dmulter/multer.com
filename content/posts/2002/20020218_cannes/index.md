No, I wasn't off to Cannes to accept an award for some great new movie I
directed. I was scheduled to speak on a panel at the 3GSM World Congress. It
was a real shame that Charlotte couldn't make it out with me for the week. I
wound up in a hotel in Moujin called [Le Mas Candille](https://www.mascandille.com). The hotel was
awesome, though the only photo I took was from my balcony. I really didn't want
to rub it in.

![01](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2002/20020218_cannes/01.jpg)

The conference was very busy, being the biggest telecom event in the world. The
room I presented in was a giant super plush theatre. I guess this is where all
the big stars come during the [Cannes Film Festival](https://www.festival-cannes.com). It was very cool
being up on that same stage.
