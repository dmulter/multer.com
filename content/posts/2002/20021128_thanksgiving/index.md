George and Ellen are over the house for a short visit before heading over to
Valri's house for Thanksgiving dinner. We've got a few dishes for the meal to
prepare and some relaxing to do before we go. The kids spend their time
jumping on the trampoline, while George and I catch up on things out on the
deck.
