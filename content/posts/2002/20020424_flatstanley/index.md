Flat Stanley was a school project for Lynda's daughter Rachael. She made this
cool little guy and then sent him on vacation with his cousin Gabe in
California. We took a number of photos of Gabe and his new friend having fun
at our house. He bounced on the trampoline, played with Abby, climbed on our
playground, and went on the swings. Gabe tells me he and Stanley had a great
day. Maybe we can have Stanley over again some other day.
