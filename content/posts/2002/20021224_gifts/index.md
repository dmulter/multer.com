There are so many gifts under the tree, we finally give in to the begging and
let the kids open a few early. Uncle Scott gave Gabe the biggest ball he's
even seen. It's constantly blocking our hallways, but it certainly is his
favorite present. Meanwhile, Charlotte, Karen, and Max strike a pose.
