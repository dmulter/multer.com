Lynda gives me a call and suggests we meet in Chicago for Rachael's birthday.
Good chance for a trip to see Annabel and Monica loves to travel. How can I
say no. We managed to pack quite a few events into our short trip, including
this visit to the [Field Museum](https://www.fieldmuseum.org). I think this is
the T-Rex that Johnny Cash wrote a sing about, or maybe that was a man names
Sue. The last two shots are a glimpse into what it was probably like for our
early ancestors in the Stone Age. Notice the prominent brow ridges.

We spent a good part of the day toruing around on the free shuttle buses they
have downtown. It's a great ride on a beautiful day, but the traffic was
horrendous. There was a big event going on this weekend, guess we'll take the
car next time.
