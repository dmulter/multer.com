I hadn't seen my uncle Jeff and aunt Eileen in such a long time. When Eileen
called me to say she was coming to Santa Cruz for a dog show, I tried my best
to talk Jeff into coming as well. To our great pleasure, even Ari came along.
Eileen has got quite a travel setup for her three dogs Mary Jane, Luther, and
Lacy.

The kids loved hanging with the dogs, though Abby seemed a bit overwhelmed with
the sheepdog pack that came to town that day. Here Gabe can't take his eyes
off Mary Jane.

![01](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2002/20020818_dogshow/01.jpg)

Monica immediately fell for the dogs and followed Eileen 
    everywhere, helping where she could. Monica couldn't resist Mary Jane either 
    and had a bit of fun dressing her up.

show dog expert.
