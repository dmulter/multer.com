School is over for the day, and now it's time to play trick or treat in the
neighborhood. Nick went for the scary mask this year, rather than the skeleton
outfit. He's finally outgrown it and it's time to pass on the tradition to
Gabe. Monica chose some type of spider woman outfit. I think she's still hot
on that look since playing the spider in the school play James and the Giant
Peach. Charlotte and I load them up in the car and head over to aunt Valri's
house to hook up with their cousins.
