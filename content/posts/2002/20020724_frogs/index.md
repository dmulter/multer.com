Probably one of the kids favorite pastimes is catching frogs. I'm sure we had
some collection of frogs and toads in the sauna pretty much every night. They
caught so many of them that they stopped giving them names. Of course I'm sure
we saw the same frogs quite a few times. Gabe is showing off his frog in his
new frog facts shirt. Can you tell what little boy loves frogs?
