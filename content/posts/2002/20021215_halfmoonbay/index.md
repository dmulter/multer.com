We headed out to Half Moon Bay today when most people stayed home to wait out
the storm. I enjoy being outside on days like this. The waves are really
rolling in and we enjoy the view from the car for a little while. The seagulls
are intriguing with how they line up into the wind while standing around.
There's also no shortage of puddles to play in.
