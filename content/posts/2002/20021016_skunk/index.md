My worst fears were realized this morning. I had a good run of luck catching
raccoons, but I finally caught what I didn't want to catch. Now I have to
figure out what to do with this stinky creature. I won't go into detail on the
unfortunate series of events that followed, but let's just say I spent the
rest of the day dealing with this mess. In the end, I got rid of the skunk and
traded it for a very smelly sauna. I think I'll take a break from catching the
local wildlife for a little while.
