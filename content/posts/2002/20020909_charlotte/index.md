Charlotte is very happy now that she's got her computer all to herself. The
kids and I used accounts defined under Windows XP and would switch as needed.
Most of the time I would be competing for time with Charlotte. Now each of the
kids have their own computers and everyone is happily clicking away in each of
their rooms. I finally made the switch over to my Mac, so Charlotte is on her
own. She sure looks happy.
