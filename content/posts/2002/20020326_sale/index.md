Charlotte has been making eye pillows and hot/cold packs for friends for some
time. She's expanded her repertoire recently and had an opportunity to sell
some items at a friend's home. Manufacturing went into full swing on a whole
line of eye pillows, packs, oils, and bath salts. They're becoming very
popular in the area and she's starting to sell quite a few. Here are those
humble beginnings.
