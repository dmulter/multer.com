The big drama this year was Gordon's leg. He got some kind of infection near
his ankle from some mysterious source. Unfortunately his regular doctor was
not available, and the first diagnosis was some skin infection. He took
medicine for a long time without a lot of success. He made quite a few visits
including this trip to the hospital to pump him full of antibiotics and make
sure he rested. We all stopped by to raise his spirits.

When his doctor did get back, he took him off the antibiotics given the lack of
improvement. His guess was that it was some type of spider bite and he put him
on anti-venom. It seemed to quickly heal up after that and I'm happy to report
his leg is all better. I think he spent nearly a month with the leg trouble.
We unfortunately missed our fishing trip to Standard Rock. It'll have to wait
till next year.
