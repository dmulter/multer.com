Now hail in January might not be an extraordinary thing in other parts of the
country, but I can't think of when it's ever happened here in Santa Cruz. We
used to get killer hail when we lived in Colorado. The kind of hail that
totals a car. We tried to save some pieces, but it really was too small and
melted as soon as you picked it up. Quite a treat.
