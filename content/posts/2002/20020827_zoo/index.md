While I was in Rome for the week, Charlotte tooks the kids up to the
[San Francisco zoo](https://www.sfzoo.org) with their cousins. I hear that
they've rearranged things quite a bit since I was last up there. Charlotte
went for our usual secret parking spot close to an entrance, but they closed
that gate. Sounds like they've done a great job on the new layout. Guess I'll
have to take the whole crew up again sometime soon.
