Monica loves her new American doll we got in Chicago. She's decided to call her
Samantha, or Sammy for short. Here she is modeling her many adorable outfits.
First up is her ice skating ensemble. Next is her urban outfit for trekking in
the big city. Then she moves into her tennis outfit so she can meet up with
the other American girls at the club. Finally she gets into her pajamas and
it's time for bed.
