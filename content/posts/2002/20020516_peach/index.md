Monica played the part of the spider in Roald Dahl's story *James and the Giant
Peach*. If you haven't read the story, or seen the movie, go get it for your
kids. The *Stinky Cheese Man* and his version of the *Three Little Pigs* are also
big favorites in our house. Monica did an awesome job as did all her
classmates.
