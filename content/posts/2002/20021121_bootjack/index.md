Charlotte, Valri, and Charlie headed out to Bootjack for a visit with Gordon
and Charlotte. It's getting pretty cold out there, but they've certainly got
that holiday spirit going. This is quite the festive truck in front of the
Office Shop in Calumet.

![02](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2002/20021121_bootjack/02.jpg)

I'm not quite sure what Charlotte was thinking with this picture, but that sure
is a nice pile of rocks. There's always plenty of rocks in the U.P.

![03](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2002/20021121_bootjack/03.jpg)

And besides rocks, you can always count on fishing too. I love how the rest of
the country has soda machines, while up north you can find bait machines.
Anyone for a can of night crawlers or leeches?
