It's not quite Halloween, but it is time for some scary skating. They're having
a free school night at the roller rink in Scotts Valley and everyone gets
dressed up. The thought of all those kids attracted by free skating for this
one night only, is all the scaring I need. Gabe and Monica are very excited
about going, and I'm thankful my awesome wife is willing to take them.
