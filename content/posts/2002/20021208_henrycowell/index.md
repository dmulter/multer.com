The campground at Henry Cowell may be closed, but that just means less of a
crowd. We pack up a lunch and head out to the campgrounds followed by some
great hiking. It's mostly downhill to the river and then a long uphill hike
back out. The kids held up great. Everyone made it out on their own two feet,
even if just barely. It's a great way to spend the day before the rain hits
tomorrow.
