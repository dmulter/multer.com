We celebrate both Hanukkah and Christmas in our house, so I suppose it
shouldn't be surprising that our kids have learned to be flexible in showing
their holiday spirit. It sure was a shock when I saw the decorating job they
did on themselves. They sure are having fun though.

Along more traditional lines, everyone helps out decorating the tree after a
quick shower. Doesn't look too bad for a fake tree. We'll having a few
problems with the lights this year. May have to hang the menorah on the tree
if it gets too dark.
