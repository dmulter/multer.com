Charlotte and Lynn headed out to [Sequoia National Park](https://www.nps.gov/seki/) to do some
backpacking out to Moose Lake. The girls are missing Colorado a whole lot and
need an alpine fix. It's a very exposed area, but the terrain is very
stunning.
