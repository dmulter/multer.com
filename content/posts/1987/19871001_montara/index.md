Our very first home is so awesome. It backs onto state land, which gives us
loads of room. It's really a welcome change from renting in Cupertino.
Especially after coming from Colorado. There's a great path going further into
the woods that passes a beautiful flower field. In the other direction we
often walk to the beach or up Montara mountain.
