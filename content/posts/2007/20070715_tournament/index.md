Charlie laid down the big challenge to Gordy on who was the better fishing
team. Charlie and Gordon took one boat. Gordy, Gabe, Annie, and I took the
other. While we hauled in a few nice walleye and other fish, Charlie came up
empty. Best not to mess with that many years of fishing experience.
