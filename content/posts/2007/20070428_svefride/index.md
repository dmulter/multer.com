Today is the big day. There are around 70 of us that will be doing 100km in the
mountains of Scotts Valley and out to Corralitos. It's a fantastic day and one
of our biggest fundraisers for local schools. Charlotte and her SVEF team have
been working their tails off to make sure the ride is a big success. For me,
it's another chance to enjoy a beautiful ride with plenty of friends.
