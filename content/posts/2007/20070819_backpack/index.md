Our last day will cover just over 13 miles, and boy are our feet sore. Time to
suck it up and hit the trail. Fortunately it's mostly downhill today. The
trail along the river makes for plenty to see along the way. A few trout make
Gabe wish he had a fishing pole.

Well we finally made it. Now we just have to crawl the last few hundred yards
to my car. In just three days Gabe has become a super hiker. The whole trip
was at least 32 miles, and I hear that it's close to 4000 of climbing and 7000
feet of descending. Now that's one awesome accomplishment!
