A really cool fog bank moved in over the glacier this morning. After being
socked in for a short while, it cleared off in a most spectacular fashion.
It's amazing how many ways this place can impress you.

Today we have a big day of hiking ahead of us. We're going to head out to
Anderson pass along the ridge. We figure it will be more fun than walking the
river bar the whole way. The pass promises a great view of where the glacier
turns, and a chance to see more of the huge mountains of the Alaska Range.
When we get close to the pass, the wind is blowing like crazy. Little did I
know our campsite was getting blasted at the same time.
