We're treating ourselves to a special Christmas gift this year, a trip to Napa
Valley. Dad and the crew have never been, so we thought staying at the
excellent [Harvest Inn](https://www.harvestinn.com) in St. Helena for a night
would be very special. Stopping in Yountville for lunch is as beautfiul as
ever.
