Nick, Monica, and I are in the DC area for Max's wedding, while Charlotte and
Gabe work at this year's SVEF Christmas tree lot. Nick and I worked out butts
off earlier in the week unloading the trailer truck, so now it's time to start
selling them. If you're local, make sure you stop by and buy a tree while
helping our schools.
