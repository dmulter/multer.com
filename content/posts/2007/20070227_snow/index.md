Gabe is off to Science Camp this week and unfortunately forgot his coat in
someone's car. Charlotte and I head off over the summit towards Eureka Canyon.
Charlotte is wearing the right shoes for the trip, and I'm thinking twice
about my choice of sandals once we see that it's snowing on the summit.

![01](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2007/20070227_snow/01.jpg)

The road closure up on Highland Way is worst than I remember. Maybe the dark,
the ice, and the snow are making it seem a little scarier than when I was on
the bike. Then again maybe it always was. I know plenty of riders that will
really enjoy the adrenalin rush this part of the SVEF Mt Charlie Challenge
Ride route will provide. What a fun place to work on rock piles! I'll have to
ask Monica if she wants to try it up here.

![02](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2007/20070227_snow/02.jpg)
![03](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2007/20070227_snow/03.jpg)

![05](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2007/20070227_snow/05.jpg)
