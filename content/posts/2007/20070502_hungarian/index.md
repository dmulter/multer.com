Charlotte headed back to Bootjack with her dad this week. I'm so jealous she
gets to Michigan before the rest of the family. How can a hike as beautiful as
this be fair? She doesn't even seem to mind being the only one that can make
sure she's in some of the pictures. The bare trees make for even better
views.
