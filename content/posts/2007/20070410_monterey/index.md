Charlotte headed down to Monterey with Valri and her dad to enjoy the day.
She's been heading off on different trips with the kids since they have off
for Spring Break this week. They spent a little time at the aquarium, and the
rest of the day hanging out for lunch and other destinations. I think Gabe
took most of these photos. There are a few really great ones.
