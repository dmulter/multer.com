Gabe's been playing the trumpet in the school's new beginning band class with
Mr. Kurrus. It's been such a pleasure having a music teacher dedicated to
helping the students across our entire school district. All the hard work
comes together at tonight's Fall Music Extravaganza. Pretty impressive for
kids that just started playing three months ago.
