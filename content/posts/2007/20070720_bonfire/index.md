Our neighbors the Rice family are finally selling their cabin. They told us we
could grab anything we liked from their shed, including this very nice little
sailboat. The Peyser boys were happy to take on the project.

![01](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2007/20070720_bonfire/01.jpg)

A nice boat ride over to Houghton for some fun over at Chutes and Ladders. It
was a little too cold for some, though Doug found a good place to hide.
