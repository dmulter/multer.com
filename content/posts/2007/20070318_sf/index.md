Charlotte and I are off for a rare night away from home on our own. Our
anniversary is tomorrow and we'll be enjoying ourselves in fabulous San
Francisco. A few interesting faces on the drive up.

for dinner. A very cool place and the food was excellent. The garlic was
killing me the rest of the night.
