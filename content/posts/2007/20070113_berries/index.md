The birds have been going crazy over the berries in the bush outside our front
window. They've been making a racket and we recently noticed that they've been
gorging on them so much that they've been crashing into the window regularly.
The bushes are full of robins and cedar waxwings getting wasted on ripe
berries.

While Gabe and I log some quality Playstation time.
