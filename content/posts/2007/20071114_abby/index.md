Charlotte was out hiking with Sonia recently. They brought the poodles(Abby and
Phoebe) along for the fun. Unfortunately Abby was having so much fun running
through the woods, she managed to rupture all the ligaments in her right front
wrist. Charlotte had to carry her out of the woods on her shoulders as Abby
was no longer able to walk. The trip to the vet hasn't produced a happy
result, so it's a splint until we can find out what the surgical options are.
