Unfortunately I accidentally packed the camera at the bottom of my backpack
this morning, so we only have photos from when I unpacked at the campsite.
Let's just say we were a little sore from the first day, and the ten more
miles into[Big Basin State Park](https://www.parks.ca.gov/?page_id=540) was a
long trip. Today's hike was probably the best so far. Once the trail entered
the park, we had quite a bit of uphill through excellent redwood forests. Once
we crested the top, we were treated to an entirely different ecosystem. Gabe
really enjoyed climbing around on the smooth sandstone mounds. I couldn't wait
to get out of the heat. Our little campsite at Jay Camp was very private,
including our own private hollow redwood bathroom.
