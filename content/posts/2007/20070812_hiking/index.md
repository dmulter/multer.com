Gabe and I have a big backpacking trip coming up, so we need to do a lot of
hiking to prepare ourselves and our new boots for the trip. This is one of
Charlotte's usual hikes up to Bear Mountain and then down to the San Lorenzo
River and back. A nice three hour hike is always fun.
