Charlotte and Paula drove the whole course today as the big day is fast
approaching. The washout at Highland Way is an amazing sight. They are closer
than I plan on getting when I go by on my bike in two weeks. Weather is a
little nicer than the last time Charlotte and I drove by this past winter.
