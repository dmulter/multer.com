Today is the beginning of our great backpacking journey along the
Skyline-to-the-Sea trail. We probably spent about a week getting equipment,
preparing, and packing everything we would need for our three day trip. We'll
be heading from the top of the mountains all the way to the Santa Cruz coast.
Charlotte dropped us off at the trailhead in [Castle Rock State Park](https://www.parks.ca.gov/?page_id=538).

I never get tired of how beautfiful it is here just about every day. Our first
day we'll be covering about ten miles and camp at Waterman Gap.
