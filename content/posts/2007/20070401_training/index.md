It's time for another Sunday morning training ride to help people get ready for
the SVEF ride at the end of the month. A lot less riders today as many people
went to a local century ride. It was fun hooking up with Steve Miller this
time. He had a great story about Floyd Landis' hip replacement. Dave Imboden
went down Eureka Canyon for a longer ride while we headed down Soquel San
Jose. I was really sorry to hear later that he had a bad crash. I hope he
starts feeling better soon.
