Thank goodness I managed to get a solid block of sleep on my first night in the
park. I definitely needed it, and I know the rest of the trip is likely to be
a problem when it comes to sleeping. We had a nice view of the imposing
Muldrow glacier from our campsite. And how are we ever going to cross that
monster?

that isn't from the safety of the bus. Nice bear.
