It's time for the next big SVEF event, the Ice Cream Social. This event is a
lot more complicated than the bike ride, so Charlotte and countless volunteers
have been very busy for some time now. This morning is the last big push to
get everything setup and ready for the big crowd. It's a great day and things
are almost ready.
