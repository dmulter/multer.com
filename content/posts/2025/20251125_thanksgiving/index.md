A family visit always includes a family hike. Now that we're living in
Colorado, the weather is a bit colder this time of year and a bit higher in
elevation. Let's take it easy and enjoy hiking along Clear Creek just outside
downtown Golden.

![01](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2025/20251125_thanksgiving/01.jpg)
![02](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2025/20251125_thanksgiving/02.jpg)

This year I'm doing a brined turkey with all the fixings from scratch. Big
thanks to my favorite online chef Brian Lagerstrom for
the [recipes](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vdH_3TWYpJI). It's awesome
having Doug and Dallas visiting this year as well. We've been encouraging all
our family and friends to visit anytime since we've got lots of space and new
places and things to do here in the fantastic state of Colorado.
