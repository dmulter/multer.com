We finally broke down and got rid of the crappy Suburban. It took a while to
get a decent deal on that piece of shit, but Charlotte really pulled off an
amazing negotiation effort and landed a beautiful new Honda CRV. What a
concept, great mileage, minimal service, and very flexible.
