If you can take the cold, the cross country skiing up north is pretty
fantastic. It's very remote and not too hilly. I wish I was up there with
Charlotte. It's a great way to spend the day together. I don't think anyone is
using the deer blind this time of year, but it's best if Charlotte moves on
before she's mistaken for Bambi's mother.
