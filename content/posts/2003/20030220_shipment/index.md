Charlotte's mom sent us a whole palette of stuff recently. Not sure how long
the Abe Lincoln look-alike painting will last in the family room, but the
rocker and the dresser look great. Charlotte did a whole pile of work
restoring the dresser. I was pretty skeptical when it first arrived, but
Charlotte really pulled it off as usual. Lots of odds and ends have been added
to the menagerie scattered about the house.
