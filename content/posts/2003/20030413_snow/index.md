The family is off to the east coast for Passover vacation. First stop is New
Hope on our way to visit my mom and my sister. We got into Kennedy late and
decided to stop at a hotel in New Hope rather than waking my mom. The kids
were thrilled with the sight of snow still on the ground. The weather was
pretty much the same as what we left in Santa Cruz, so it was surprising to
see some of the white stuff was still holding out.
