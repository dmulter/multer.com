Gabe and I are up early again for some fishing out on the bay. No luck with the
big fish again on this trip, but we did catch similar stuff as last time. The
first one was such a cute tiny fish, I couldn't resist taking a picture.
Normally I would be embarrassed at having caught something this small. The
unique catch for the day was Gabe's octopus. It was quite a trick getting it
out of the boat. Those tentacles sure can hang on.
