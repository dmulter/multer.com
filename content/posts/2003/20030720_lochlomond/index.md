We promised Gabe a fishing trip, and Loch Lomond is a nice warmup before
heading for the big ocean. Charlotte worked hard at stocking Gabe's freshwater
tackle box, so we're off. Nick and Charlotte do a bit of hiking while Gabe,
Monica, and I go for a bit of rowing. The fish aren't biting, but it's fun
anyway. We top it all off with some BBQ and melted marshmallows.
