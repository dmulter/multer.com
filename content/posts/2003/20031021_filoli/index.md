We unfortunately don't have a lot of pictures from Mom's latest visit, but
Charlotte managed to snap a few on their trip up to the [Filoli Center](https://filoli.org).
We used to drive by this place all the time when we were commuting from Half
Moon Bay, but never managed to stop by. It's supposed to be even more
impressive in the spring when the flowers are blooming, but it looks pretty
awesome to me even this time of year.
