It's birthday time again for David. We did the Chinese food dinner thing this
year. The kids really enjoy doing the pu-pu platter. There's something about
food warmed over a nice sterno fire. Back at the house I break open the
booty and enjoy cake. I got a bunch of DVDs, books, and those JBL speakers I
really wanted. My bro managed to get a pair of them for me as well. Now let
me see what else I would love from Amazon.
