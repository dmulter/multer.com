We didn't have much luck growing pumpkins this year, though we did manage to
get a nice one out of the garden. The rest we picked up at the market for the
kids to carve. The carvings turned out great, and the kids always enjoy the
pumpkin seeds. Gabe went for the anaconda look, Monica the vampire queen, and
Nick the gigantic and terrifying bone man.
