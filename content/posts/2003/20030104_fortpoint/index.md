Before heading back home, we make one last tourist stop at [Fort Point](https://www.nps.gov/fopo/).
Charlotte hasn't wanted to see this place for some time, and I'm glad we
did.It's a beautiful walk along the bay to the fort.

walkways, hidden corners, and tremendous views. Charlotte tries her hand at a
few artsy photos as well. I think the archways came out very good.
