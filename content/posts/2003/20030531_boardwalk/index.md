Monica's been begging to go to the boardwalk for so long, we really had to go.
It's so close to the house, but the crowds are always a drag. We headed out
early while the fog was still burning off. There were no lines, so it really
was easy. Gabe loves the little flying rides like the helicopters. Nick goes
for the high speed spinning rides. I like the roller coasters and the log
ride. Anyone up for a big drop?
