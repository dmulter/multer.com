Gabe's been wiggling his front tooth for what's got to be a week. We were about
ready to take him over to his Aunt Val's house for her patented removal
technique, but fate intervened. A handy bump while on a trip in the car did
the trick. Good thing he'll be asleep when the tooth fairy shows up. If she
saw some of these faces, she might fly off and never return again.
