Charlotte took Gabe on his field trip to [Happy Hollow](https://happyhollow.org) in San Jose.
It's really a very fun place for the kids, though it can be quite a mob scene.
There's a zoo, rides, and it looks like some kind of play. I sure would have
loved to take him on this field trip.
