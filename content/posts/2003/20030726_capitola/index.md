The day has finally arrived. Gabe and I are off for some fishing in Monterey
Bay. We've rented one of those small boats from the Capitola pier. I don't
think we'll be heading out anywhere near as far as when Gordon and I went out
for salmon a few years ago. A great blue heron is there to see us off.

![01](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2003/20030726_capitola/01.jpg)
![02](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2003/20030726_capitola/02.jpg)
![03](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2003/20030726_capitola/03.jpg)

No big catches on this trip, other than a few big smiles. We get a nice
assortment of fish, but the end of the day brings a bit of disappointment for
the big fisherman. I think he wanted to bring a 300lb tuna back.
