Charlotte, Val, and Gordon headed up to San Francisco with Doug and Gabe today.
The kids have off for Veteran's Day, the sun is shining, and it's time to make
the most of the day. The skulls exhibit at the [Academy of Sciences](https://www.calacademy.org)
is very cool. Wish I was there.

George works up in the city and fortunately had time to hook up with everyone
for lunch down at [Fisherman's Wharf](https://www.fishermanswharf.org). Doug
and Gabe sure do enjoy themselves. They make quite a pair.
