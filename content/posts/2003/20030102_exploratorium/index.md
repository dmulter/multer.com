We decided to head up to San Francisco for a couple of nights while the kids
still had time off from school. Charlotte worked a great deal at a hotel near
Pier 39, but first we made a stop at the [Exploratorium](https://www.exploratorium.edu).
The building is from the old Pan-Pacific Exposition and is one of the major
sites in San Francisco. The crowds were a bit much for a visit to the museum,
but the park is a lot of fun.
