Somehow we've never been drawn into the 150 foot diameter nexus of weirdness
before. With Lynda in town, we generated a list of places worth visiting and
the famous [Mystery Spot](https://www.mysteryspot.com) happened to make the
grade. I was unfortunately at work that day and had to make do with the tales
of strangeness. The spot is really a house built on a slant that throws off
your perception big time. An added bonus is that it makes you sick to your
stomach. No one figured it out, so I guess it's still a mystery.
