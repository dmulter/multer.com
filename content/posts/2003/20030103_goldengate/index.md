We decided to spend most of the day over at [Golden Gate Park](https://www.nps.gov/goga/) today.
There's plenty to do in the park including the [arboretum](https://www.strybing.org) and
the [Academy of Sciences](https://www.calacademy.org). It's a shame about the flash
reflection, that big octopus was awesome.
