Time for a bit of post-holiday roaming, even if the weather isn't cooperating.
We swung by a very cool knitting place called The Golden  Fleece near
downtown. Charlotte has been knitting lots of scarves, and this is one of her
favorite places to oogle yarns and things. Gabe and I hang out nearby after
checking out the [Roaring Camp](https://roaringcamp.com) train that just went
by. He's either not happy about the rain, or me taking his picture. I'll let
you decide.
