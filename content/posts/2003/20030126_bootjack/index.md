Charlotte is off to Bootjack again to visit with her parents. Her mom is not
doing very well and I'm glad to see Charlotte spending time with them while
she can. There's certainly no shortage of snow or cold this time of year and
Gordon has his work cut out for him keeping up with mother nature. I'm sure
glad I never have to do this kind of thing in Santa Cruz.
