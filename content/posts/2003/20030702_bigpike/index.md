It's pasta night at our cabin. We still call it Rose's and I suspect we always
will. There are a lot of hungry kids lined up out on the porch.

Gabe really went and did it this time. Everyone heads out to Jacobsville for
some fun out by the lighthouse. Gabe of course has to squeeze in some fishing
at the opening of the channel into Lake Superior. After about 4 casts with a
big lure, he announces he's got a fish. A little while later, with help from
Uncle George, they haul in a 42.5 inch, 17 pound northern pike. It's the
biggest pike anyone has ever seen up here. It's no fish story, and we've got
the pictures to prove it!
