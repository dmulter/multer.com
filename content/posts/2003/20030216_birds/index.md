I really won't go into the tales of woe regarding our previous bird pets, but
Nick sure does love these creatures. Charlotte caught wind of a woman that's
really into birds and had a few she was looking to unload for free. It was too
hard to say no, so two more animals join the ranks of the Multer's pet
menagerie. I'm trying really hard to focus on how much the kids love them, and
not the crap (literally) they leave all over the house. Gabe and I work
them into our castle action figure play. I don't think they're going to hold
up as well as those plastic figures.
