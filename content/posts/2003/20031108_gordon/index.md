We celebrated Gordon's birthday at Michael's restaurant. Besides great food,
friends, and family, we had the pleasure of dining alongside walls full of
Val's artwork. It was such fun having Bea, George, Ellen, and Carol here to
join us for this occasion. The food and conversation was loads of fun as
usual. There's always plenty of great stories with this crowd, especially with
George in the mix.
