After settling in at the hotel, we checked out the local sites including
[Pier 39](https://www.pier39.com). The Christmas tree was still up and
Charlotte wanted me to take a picture of this wierd silver guy. After
discreetly snapping a photo and walking off, the kids let me know he was
following me. Turns out he wanted money for having taken his picture, which
didn't sit too well with me. Charlotte ended the confrontation by giving him a
buck. The kids are still talking about how freaked out they were that he
followed us. Boy that was fun.
