Time to put in some miles on the bike before the feeding frenzy that is called
Thanksgiving. It's a nice change to be riding around Santa Cruz during the
week again. The rides from work are fine, but there are a lot more hills to
climb around home. Unfortunately George wasn't able to make it down early
enough to join me. It's always nice to have some company on a long ride.
