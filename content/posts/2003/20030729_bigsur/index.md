Charlotte and the kids decide to go for an overnight camping trip down to the
Big Sur area. Sure wish I didn't have to work.

![01](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2003/20030729_bigsur/01.jpg)

Enough of the tent, time to head for the beach. Pfeiffer state beach is very
nice, but I don't know that it lives up to its most beautiful beach status.
Give me a beach in Hawaii anytime.
