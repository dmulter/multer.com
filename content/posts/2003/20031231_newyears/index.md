In an effort to stay up late enough to see in the new year, we followed dinner
with a movie marathon. Plenty of action and comedy to keep everyone awake. The
minute there's the first signs of drowsiness, it's time to get some sugar into
the system. Charlotte bought all the fixings for some pretty heavy duty
sundaes. Nick and Monica show of their creations.
