Gabe is in desperate need of a fishing fix. We promised to do some crabbing and
fishing, but the Capitola and Seacliff piers were packed with people. Luckily
the Santa Cruz Wharf was pretty empty still. We didn't have any luck with
crabs or starfish, but there were huge schools of anchovies. I picked up a jig
from the bait shop, and proceeded to catch them as fast as I could get the
line down. I think we can count the trip as a big success. Should hold Gabe
over until next weekend.
