Nick has been excited for weeks preparing for the upcoming Julius Caesar play.
He's playing the part of Cicero and he has his lines all memorized. There's
plenty of preparations before the play starts. Lynn's up visiting and helps
Charlotte with things. Looks like I'm running a few minutes late.

Nick looks so grown up with his sideburns. If it wasn't for the toga, he would
be the splitting image of Elvis. I was very impressed with all the kids. They
memorized their lines perfectly and spoke very clearly and with great emotion.
They sure do grow up quick.
