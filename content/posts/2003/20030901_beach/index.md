No visit is complete without a visit to our favorite beach. It's even quiet on
Labor Day. George didn't meet us this time, so we had to make do with
sandwiches from Safeway instead of the Garden Deli. Of course any meal on the
beach is a great meal. Doug came along for his first visit to this beach.
