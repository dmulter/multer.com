Charlotte is off to Valri's to perform emergency restoration efforts on some
critical blankies. It may look hopeless, but Charlotte is a miracle worker
with the sewing machine. Val's got some new artwork that's worth checking out.
One of the many bunnies bids Charlotte goodbye.
