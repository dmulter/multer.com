It's the big morning and everyone is ready to start tearing presents open. The
excitement is nearly unbearable, but the wait is finally over. Plenty of great
stuff this year including a new bike rack for Charlotte, a new scanner for me
(and the website), lots of handmade scarves, movies, games, software, and
music. I'm looking forward to getting back to loads of scanning.

couldn't make it. Charlotte's friend Kim joined us this year in place of the
sick Kim. We tried something a bit new this year for dinner, lasagna. It was a
big hit, and will likely show up again in future Christmas dinners.
