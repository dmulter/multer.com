Charlotte and Lynn head up to San Francisco to enjoy the weekend together. Lynn
and Peter are headed to Spain for Peter's next post soon, so this is the last
chance for a get together unless we head out to Europe. They're staying at
the [Marine Club](https://marinesmemorial.org) near Union Square.
