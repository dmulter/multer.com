Val's bird Ruby was driving her crazy with his noises, so Charlotte decided to
bring him home for a sleep over. Turns out that once he got a glimpse of
Cheerio, it was love at first site. They were constantly nuzzling each other
and he would sing the most elaborate songs I had ever heard from these birds.
The only casualty in all this was Monica. Cheerio wanted nothing to do with
Monica anymore. Ruby would also go bonkers if Monica took Cheerio into her
room.
