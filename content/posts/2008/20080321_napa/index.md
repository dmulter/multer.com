Today we're doing some wine tasting. First it's over to [Joseph Phelps](https://www.josephphelps.com).
I loved the relaxed atmosphere and style. Sitting on a deck quietly enjoying
wine, while listening to the sheep in the vineyard was my idea of wine
tasting. A very nice collection of wine, but with a hint of sheep.

Next was lunch and a quick look around the [CIA at Greystone](https://www.ciachef.edu/cia-california/). Lunch was
really great, but I would have definitely enjoyed hanging out here for longer.
Unfortunately our next wine tasting appointment is calling us. Just have to
plan a bunch more time during our next visit to Napa.

Our last stop for the day was at [Quintessa](https://www.quintessa.com). Now
here's another winery I really enjoyed. The estate is very beautiful, and I
love being able to walk around at my own pace without a crowd surrounding us.
These guys are really focused on making one great wine, and it shows.
