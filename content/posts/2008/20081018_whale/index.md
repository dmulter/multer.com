Gabe and I are off for some fishing today, and I finally get my first close up
view of a whale. A few of them were in about 50 feet of water just outside the
harbor entrance. Gabe gets credit for first spotting them. I thought it was
some dolphin at first, but then one kicked up it's tail. It was really tough
getting a photo, but this is the best I could do. Take my word for it, they
were amazing.

![01](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2008/20081018_whale/01.jpg)

A few small rockfish round out the day. It was a little rough in the morning,
but by afternoon it was really nice. Of course it's always nice being out on
the Monterey Bay.
