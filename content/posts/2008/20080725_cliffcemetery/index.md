After a great lunch at [The Hut](https://www.hutinn.com), we head over to the
rock shop. George and Caroline have never been here, and it's a great chance
to see some very nice agates.

![22](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2008/20080725_cliffcemetery/22.jpg)

After heading home, the whole family heads over to the Dreamland for dinner.
Friday night dinner is a real Bootjack tradition.
