The SVEF Mt. Charlie Challenge Ride is coming up soon, so it's time to ratchet
up the training rides a bit. We've got a pretty good turnout today and the
weather is cooperating. All this posing is really for the Scotts Valley
Banner, but Charlotte manages to get a few good shots standing next to the
photographer.
