Our biggest turnout yet, and the official ride is just around the corner. Today
we'll be doing most of the real ride minus the Hazel Dell loop. So it's up Mt
Charlie, over Summit and Highland, down Eureka Canyon, back along Soquel, and
up Glen Canyon to Skypark.

We even had enough people for a separate training ride for the 50K event.
Outstanding!
