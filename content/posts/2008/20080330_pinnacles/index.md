Today we're heading down to [Pinnacles National Monument](https://www.nps.gov/pinn/) with Karen,
Valri, and George's family. I've ridden past this beautiful spot on the King
City bike ride before, and I've always wanted to check it out more thoroughly.
Time to load up the cars and head on out.
