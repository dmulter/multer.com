Renee and Marcel are having an 80th birthday party for Bea at their house in
Los Gatos. I can't say I know too many of the family assembled for this
fantastic celebration, but it is great to see a few familiar faces. It's
always such fun to visit with Bea and her family. I'm so glad Gordon is in
town for this as well.
