The last time I was up here was with Gabe for our backpacking trip about a year
ago. Charlotte's been looking forward to trying out some of the trails one
day, and that day is today. We did the full loop in about three hours, and my
legs are pretty tired. Some spectacular views that really brought back a few
of the backpacking memories. There were quite a few people rock climbing the
numerous cool rocks. As far as cool rocks though, nothing compares to the Moon
Rocks in Boony Doon.
