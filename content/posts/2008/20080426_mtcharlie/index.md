Today is the day for the big SVEF Mt Charlie Challenge Ride. It's the biggest
fundraiser for SVEF since it started six years ago. Though I'm somewhat
biased, it's definitely the most fun as well. Charlotte, Paula, and Sally have
been working their tails off for months getting everything arranged. A big
thanks to everyone else that helped make this year's event another resounding
success.

It's hard to tell from the photos, but around 80 people just finished riding
either 100K and over 4,000 feet of climbing, or 50K and maybe 1,000 feet of
climbing. My route took about four hours, and I thoroughly enjoyed myself. A
well earned lunch awaits, and hopefully I'll get lucky in the raffle.
