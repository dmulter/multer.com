After a tiring day of flying back from Bulgaria yesterday, it's time to
recharge with a nice hike through Pogonip and over to the [UCSC Arboretum](https://arboretum.ucsc.edu)
with Charlotte. They have such an awesome collection of flowers, I just
couldn't stop taking photos.
