The next stop is one of my favorite areas, [Grand Teton](https://www.nps.gov/grte/) national park.
It was quite a few years ago when Charlotte, Lynn, and I went backpacking up in
these mountains. They're still just as amazing as I remember, though these
recent photos do a much better job of capturing just how beautiful it really
is. One of these days I need to head back there with Charlotte.
