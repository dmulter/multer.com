On our way back home, we've decided to visit two places down in Napa. First
it's over to COPIA. We squeeze in a short tour of the place, and it's
immediately apparent to me that this is an all-day affair. Another one to put
on my list for the next trip to Napa. What a cool place, with just tons of
things to do.

One last wine tasting over at [Del Dotto](https://www.deldottovineyards.com). They have a great old cave
system, and our tour guide was a very entertaining man that does the blending
for the winery. It was a great chance to directly sample the effects of various
barrels on many types of wine.
