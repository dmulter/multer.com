nice time, especially sinceI haven't seen her for some time while she was
driving back from Michigan. I've wanted to stay at [Costanoa](https://www.costanoa.com) since it's
across the road from my favorite beach. First stop is over at the Pigeon Point
Lighthouse in Pescadero.
