Today we're heading out from Meadowood to explore the area. First we drove up
Spring Mountain Road and then back through Calistoga. We then drove up Highway
29 to Robert Louis Stevenson State Park. I was wearing my hiking sandals, so
we opted for the short hike up to the small monument commemorating his
honeymoon stay here so many years ago. Some very nice views, but the drive was
very busy just like the bike trails map said.
