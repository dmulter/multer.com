The Santa Cruz upper harbor has been pretty deserted lately with the lack of
salmon fishing and high gas prices. Just the thing for this local to finally
get a chance to keep my boat in the water. This sure is a million times more
convenient. She looks very nice in her new slip. Can you spot her from up on
the hill?

![02](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2008/20080906_nemo/02.jpg)
![03](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2008/20080906_nemo/03.jpg)

Though Gabe is much happier now that we're taking out the boat much more
frequently, we're not catching any bigger fish. It's still a load of fun.
