I can't believe we've never been to the Moon Rocks in Boony Doon before. I've
ridden by this place on my bike before, but never really understood what I was
flying by. Valri suggested we take Karen and the kids for fun, and it sounded
good to me. What a place! Definitely one of the most fun local destinations I
can think of, and the kids had a blast.
