Charlotte took Nick and Monica up to San Francisco today to take a campus tour
of [SFSU](https://www.sfsu.edu). I think it will be a bit to urban for their
tastes, but it's a good idea to see a broad range of schools.

the spectacular Taco Bell beach house in Pacifica.
