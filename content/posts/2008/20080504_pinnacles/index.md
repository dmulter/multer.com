Ever since our last trip to [Pinnacles National Monument](https://www.nps.gov/pinn/), Charlotte and
I have been looking forward to going back for a longer, tougher, faster hike
all the way to the top. Today is that day. This time we arrive via the east
entrance early in the morning. Unfortunately we weren't fast enough to get a
photo of the road runner that sprinted across the road as we drive in.
