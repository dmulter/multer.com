Once I graduated from [RPI](https://www.rpi.edu), I flew out to Europe for a
nice long trip. I packed one bag and hung out mostly in England for about a
month. I stayed at a few different small hotels around the city. I also stayed
with my cousin Emily for a while as well. I loved wondering around the city
and really got to know my way around the Tube very well. Plenty of great sites
to take in on my first ever trip abroad.

was a fun spot and I always enjoyed watching people play cricket. Never quite
understood the game, but it was a good way to relax.
