Charlotte made quite a few awesome backpacking and road trips while she lived
in Colorado. Here she's heading off to the [Grand Canyon](https://www.nps.gov/grca/).
Sounds like it was an amazing trip, though hiking there in the middle of summer
may not have been the best idea.
