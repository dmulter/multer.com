Nick was born at Mills Hospital in San Mateo at 5:02am. He weighed 8 pounds 9
ounces and was 21 inches long. He was born with the same size gigantic head I
was sporting. It proved quite an ordeal for Charlotte that resulted in many
days at the hospital recovering, followed by many weeks of recovery at home.
