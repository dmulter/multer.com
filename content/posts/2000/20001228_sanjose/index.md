The family came to visit me at work today. Gabe is trying to give me advice on
the current software project.

![01](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2000/20001228_sanjose/01.jpg)

We headed over to the plaza area of San Jose for the Christmas in the Park
event. Every year they set up displays for people to enjoy. There's also
skating and other things to do at this time of year.

![02](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2000/20001228_sanjose/02.jpg)
![03](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2000/20001228_sanjose/03.jpg)

We also headed over to the [Tech](https://www.thetech.org) museum. It's a fun
collection of technology related interactive displays. Learn how a computer
works, experiment with gravity and optics, see an IMAX movie, and make believe
your a [San Jose Sharks](https://www.nhl.com/sharks/) goalie just like Nick.
