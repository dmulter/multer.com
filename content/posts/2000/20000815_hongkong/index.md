Charlotte was able to join me on a business trip to Hong Kong. We stayed at
the [Conrad International](https://www.hilton.com/en/hotels/hkghcci-conrad-hong-kong/) hotel. I had to
spend everyday locked up with a bunch of computer geeks, while Charlotte
toured all over the city. I did manage to get away for one tourist trip to
Lantau. There's a beautiful monastery on the island where we wondered around
for quite some time. We then hiked up to the gigantic bronze Buddha that is
the highlight of the entire island. Pictures can't really capture the size of
it. Wish we had more pictures.
