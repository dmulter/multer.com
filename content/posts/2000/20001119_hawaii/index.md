Time for a real vacation! We're off to the Big Island of Hawaii with my mom. We
stayed at the [Mauna Lani](https://auberge.com/mauna-lani) villas. This place
is so unbelievably beautiful. I am definitely not going to want to go home.
Our place is on a series of inland ponds that the native Hawaiians built for
fish farming. The kids have tons of places to run around and play.

![01](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2000/20001119_hawaii/01.jpg)
![02](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2000/20001119_hawaii/02.jpg)
![03](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2000/20001119_hawaii/03.jpg)

We spent a lot of time over by the snorkeling area. We could spend the whole
day there on the beach. There was also a great little restaurant we enjoyed
lunch at regularly. I'm hanging out in my regular attire. I wish I had some
underwater photos from our many snorkeling trips. The reef is spectacular and
all the kids got the hang of it after a while.

![10](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2000/20001119_hawaii/10.jpg)

One day we headed out for a road trip. We drove north along the coast and then
cut across to the town of Hilo. We drove from dry lava surroundings, through
tropical rain forest environments, to the opposite coast. It was such a varied
collection of environments across a pretty small area. The cliffs on the coast
reminded me of Kauai.

![04](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2000/20001119_hawaii/04.jpg)
![11](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2000/20001119_hawaii/11.jpg)

Another day we headed over to the [Hilton](https://www.hilton.com/en/hotels/koahwhh-hilton-waikoloa-village/) for a look
around and dinner at the local restaurant. This was a fabulous hotel, though
more mainstream style than the Mauna Lani. They had a great dolphin area where
you could swim with the animals. There was quite a line. We enjoyed their many
pools and shopping.

![08](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2000/20001119_hawaii/08.jpg)

I sure wish we had taken a lot more pictures on our trip to Hawaii. It was such
a great place, I can't wait to go back again. I never did make it to
[Mauna Kea](https://www.maunakeaobservatories.org). I was dying to go, but it
would take the whole day and I didn't want to spend it away from the family. I
of course regret it immensely now, but I guess it's just a good reason to go
back sooner rather than later.
