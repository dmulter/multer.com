Lots of activities planned for today while we're at Lake Tahoe. I brought the
bike along for a ride around the lake. I get out early to beat the traffic and
the heat to enjoy all 73 miles and just over 4 hours of riding. Meanwhile Gabe
and Ari find plenty of sticks and rocks to play with in the back.
