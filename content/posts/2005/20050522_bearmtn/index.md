Charlotte and Paula discover fabled Bear Mountain in Roaring Camp during a hike
in Henry Cowell. The girls are always in search of new paths through the
woods, and today is no exception. Most people take the old steam train up to
this spot, but not these intrepid hikers. A few shots from the observation
post and then it's back down to civilization.
