Today we headed down to Watsonville to check out the Gizdich Ranch. After a few
treats at the ranch, we stopped at their berry fields to see what looked good.
We filled up on a pile of olallieberries, and stuffed ourselves while we
picked. Charlotte will be making a pie later tonight that everyone is really
looking forward to.
