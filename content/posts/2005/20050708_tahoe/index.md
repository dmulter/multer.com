We're headed up to the Twissleman's place in Lake Tahoe for the weekend. Paula,
Anat, and Stephanie have been coming up here regularly for many years with
their families. We're so happy they invited us to join them. After a long car
trip with a fun stop at the [Jelly Belly](https://www.jellybelly.com) factory,
it's time for dinner. Unfortunately the service is horrible at the restaurant
and we only manage to get out of here after two and a half hours. It sure is a
nice view while we wait though.
