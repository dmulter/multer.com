It's really cold out this morning, but the rain has finally stopped. That means
it's time to head over to [Hellyer](https://hellyervelodrome.com) and have
some fun. We went through the usual drills and races including another
Australian pursuit race. I was really happy with my 200m time and a couple of
race finishes. Charlotte and Monica came for a surprise visit during the race
part and then we enjoyed lunch together.
