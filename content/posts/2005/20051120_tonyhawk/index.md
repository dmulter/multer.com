A terrible accident recently took the life of local skateboarder Eric Costello
while he was teaching some kids over at the Scotts Valley skatepark. The
community has been pretty shocked by the accident as well. Turns out some
local businesses like Sessions talked with [Tony Hawk](https://www.tonyhawk.com) and arranged for
him to come out with his crew for a memorial event where everyone could
celebrate their love of skateboarding. Gabe was pretty jazzed to check out the
legend, and it's a fantastic day to enjoy outside.
