This year's 3rd annual [SVEF](https://www.svef.net) century bike ride has
finally arrived. It's turned out to be an awesome fundraiser for our local
schools, and a great local ride as well. Charlotte put in a ton of effort
along with the usual crew and the effort has really paid off. We had more
riders, and raised more money this year than ever before. This year we
included raffle tickets as an option for donations. I sure would have liked to
have won that Specialized bike CycleSport donated.
