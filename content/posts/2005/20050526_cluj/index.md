After countless hours crammed in coach seats, the day that started in San
Francisco finally ends with my arrival in Cluj, Romania. I'm not thinking very
straight right now since I can never sleep on the plane. A few shots from my
hotel room at the lovely Hotel Transilvania, then a quick bite to eat,
followed by hopefully many hours of sleep.
