Charlotte starts a spectacular day with some of her friends walking in the
redwoods. Today was a big hike all the way from Scotts Valley to Felton and
back.

![01](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2005/20050410_riodelmar/01.jpg)
![02](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2005/20050410_riodelmar/02.jpg)
![03](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2005/20050410_riodelmar/03.jpg)

We round up the crew and head down to Rio Del Mar beach. Monica is a bit
concerned about the rip currents down at Manresa, but this beach rocks too.
Gabe even spotted a location with an already dug pit. What a big bonus! Of
course there's a bit of territorial posturing as sand is kicked onto each
other's towels. Nick, Charlotte, and I monitor the unfolding action.
