We decided this morning to head over to Capitola and do a little fishing. No
boat this time, but it's a beautiful day for some wharf fishing. Charlotte
drops off Monica, Gabe, and me while she heads over to Mr. Toots coffee house
to help Valri with some of her paintings that are on display. I'm sure the
coffee is not really for Nick.

![01](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2005/20050116_capitola/01.jpg)

When Charlotte and Nick are done, they head over to the wharf with Gordon. A
great blue heron strikes a few poses while he waits for his lunch.

![02](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2005/20050116_capitola/02.jpg)
![03](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2005/20050116_capitola/03.jpg)

Gordon tried his magic fishing touch, but it doesn't look like anything is
really going to turn around our luck when it comes to catching fish. Of course
anyday is a nice day for fishing.
