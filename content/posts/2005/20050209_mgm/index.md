While Gabe and Charlotte battled a stomach virus, we attempted to enjoy our
last day at Disney World with a visit to MGM Studios. I didn't get many photos
as Nick and Monica started to come down with the sickness. We managed to
squeeze in one trip to Space Mountain while everyone could keep their lunch
down. The parade was a good chance to rest and take a few shots before heading
back to embrace the horror of the worst stomach virus we've ever experienced.
I did manage to keep well until our flight back the next morning, but did
manage to keep it together for the flight. It really was a fun trip as long as
I try to forget the last couple of days.
