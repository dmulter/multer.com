It's a tough choice where to go in San Diego with so many attractions and the
short time we're staying in the area. Legoland loses out by a nose and we're
off to [SeaWorld](https://seaworld.com/san-diego/). The dolphin show is a hit
and it's lots of fun watching people get dowsed. Pete sits with Gabe a few
rows closer tempting fate.
