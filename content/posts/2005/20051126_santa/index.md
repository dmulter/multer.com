Santa's got a unique style out here in California. He wears a Christmas color
Hawaiian shirt, rides in an outrigger canoe, and his reindeer are surfers. Of
course he's still got the hat, belly, and jolly laugh. Quite a few people show
up at Capitola beach for his visit, including Charlotte's friend Sonia and her
family. Doesn't look like we can expect snow anytime soon.
