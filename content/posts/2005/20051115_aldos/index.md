I've joined the usual lunchtime crew of Gordon, Charlotte, and Valri over at
[Aldo's](https://www.aldossantacruz.com) in the harbor. It's such a fantastic
day for enjoying the weather and the view. The food used to be just okay, but
the location more than made up for it. Now they've got a whole new menu and
the food is really excellent. I think this might be my new favorite place for
lunch now.
