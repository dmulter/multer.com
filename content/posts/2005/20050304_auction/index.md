Tonight is the big "Reach for the Stars" auction to raise money for Brook Knoll
elementary school. Charlotte has been helping a lot with the event, though
Valri's sounds like she's been putting in a huge effort on this one.
Everything looks ready for the big crowd and there are plenty of truly awesome
items people have donated or created. Charlotte and the SVEF crew also take
the opportunity to plug our upcoming fundraising bicycle event.
