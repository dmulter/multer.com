It's the big day and it looks like we're going to be very lucky with the
weather. The sun is starting to shine and a full double rainbow is welcoming
Gabe to his first day as a nine year old.

Next stop is the pinata. The little worm held up remarkably well considering
the heavy use of steroids on this baseball team. The twine I used to hang the
pinata didn't fair very well though. It was so weighted down with candy it
broke on the second hit. We used a jump rope and clipped it on the swings so
the violence could continue. The mad dash for candy when it finally breaks is
always the crowning glory of these events. I think Nick wishes he was given a
shot at the pinata.
