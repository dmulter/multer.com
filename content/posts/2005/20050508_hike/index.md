Instead of the usual breakfast in bed thing, Charlotte's idea of the perfect
way to start the day is to go for a giant hike in the woods. Charlotte and
Paula try a new route starting in Scotts Valley and going through Henry
Cowell, Pogonip, and then down to Santa Cruz. The first big obstacle is
getting across the swollen river. Of course they're already plenty wet from
the rain today.
