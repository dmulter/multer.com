It's that time of year again and today Charlotte is taking the kids and Doug to
Capitola to be in the Halloween parade.

First stop is Gordon's place to show off their costumes.

![02](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2005/20051030_parade/02.jpg)

Then it's time for the parade. It's not a very big affair, but something small
and fun for the little kids seems to be right in order.
