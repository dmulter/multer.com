Lynn is here to do the Nike Women's Marathon in San Francisco this weekend. All
those long walks in the woods with her friends have been great training for
the long walk in the city. Charlotte, Lynn, Paula, and a few of the other
regulars will be doing either the full or half marathon walk. Sounds like it's
going to be a really fun weekend.

![06](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2005/20051021_lynn/06.jpg)

A nice short three hour hike in the redwoods is just the kind of warm up the
