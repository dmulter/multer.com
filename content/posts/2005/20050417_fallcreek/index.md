Charlotte usually hikes over at Henry Cowell, but after hearing about Gabe's
field trip to Fall Creek, it was time to try out a new route. The photos are
from the kiln area at the furthest point of the field trip. Charlotte and
Paula headed out another few miles past this and really enjoyed the big steep
section.
