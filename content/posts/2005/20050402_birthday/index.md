Charlotte didn't have a big party with a bunch of her friends, but quite a few
stopped by the house to drop off presents and wish her the best. The family
wished her a happy birthday with presents and her favorite ice cream birthday
cake. Thank goodness for Baskin Robbins and Jamoca Almond Fudge!
