Charlotte, Valri, and Gordon headed up to San Francisco for two days of tourist
fun. Today was spent around [Fisherman's Wharf](https://www.fishermanswharf.org) and
[Ghiradelli Square](https://www.ghirardellisq.com). The lack of rain this weekend was a very lucky break.
