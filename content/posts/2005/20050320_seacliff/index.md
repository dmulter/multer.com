Charlotte optimistically scheduled the first bonfire of the year for tonight
down at Rio Del Mar beach. The series of storms making its way through the
area didn't bode well for the event, but we gave it a try anyway. We enjoyed
the twilight view from the car while we had a picnic in the car. Paula and
Matt stopped by for a few minutes, but no one else seemed crazy enough to give
it a try. We stopped by Sea Cliff on the way back to catch the sunset. I think
we'll give it another try sometime in April.
