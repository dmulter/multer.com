Charlotte took Gordon out to see the butterflies over at Natural Bridges state
park. It's actually the best time to see them hanging in gigantic groups from
the trees, but today is was very cold and they weren't cooperating. Of course
Gordon is used to weather a whole lot colder than this back in Bootjack.
