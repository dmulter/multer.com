My idea of a great day is a big bike ride followed by a family outing to the
beach. After a New York style breakfast of bagels and lox, we pick up a rental
wetsuit and board for Nick and head down to Rio Del Mar. I really wanted to
get Charlotte out in the water too, but my main goal was showing Nick what
he's been missing. Needless to say he's totally hooked now, and we're shopping
for yet another wetsuit.
