Last time we hiked, Charlotte was telling me how she always feels like the
redwoods are the kind of place where she expects to spot gnomes. I suggest we
make a regular effort to place some of her gnome collection in some of her
favorite places. Pumpkin gnome is the first to enjoy his new home out in the
woods. Hopefully people won't mess with them, and others can discover and
enjoy these pleasant forest dwelling creatures.
