Be careful where you tread  
Small life grows here.  
Fragile yet persistent  
Chances of success  
Wax and wane constantly.  
Look closely  
Don’t miss the tiny opportunity  
To witness something  
Greater than yourself.  
A tree can grow  
With roots born from stone.
