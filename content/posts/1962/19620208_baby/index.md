Thank you mom. If it weren't for you, both myself and this site could have
never been. David Lawrence Multer was born in Brooklyn, NY at [Maimonides](https://maimo.org)
hospital on February 8, 1962 at 2:10 AM. I was a big boy from the start,
weighing in at 9 lbs 2 oz and 21 inches long. They even went in with forceps
to grab my big fat head to pull me out. I'm still sporting that oversize
head to this day.
