Today was a day of reminiscing, we went back and visited many places that we
saw last year on our road trip to Michigan and loved. The first of our most
memorable revisits was Horseshoe Bend, a colossal gooseneck near Lake Powell.

![01](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110618_utah/01.jpg)

This extraordinarily impressive geological monolith is one of my favorite views
I have ever seen. Sadly the lightning was a far cry from last years and
resulted in shadowed photos. Good photos or not, this was beyond amazing and I
am really glad to go back and have another chance to see this unique
structure.

![02](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110618_utah/02.jpg)
![03](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110618_utah/03.jpg)

One of the most memorable parts about going to Horseshoe Bend last year was the
amazing lizard we found. This beautiful creature had spots and brightly orange
colored stripes. He was gorgeous and today I was on a lizard hunt in hope of
finding a similar lizard. In the end I did, see the next post for the full
story but here is one photo for now.

![04](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110618_utah/04.jpg)
![05](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110618_utah/05.jpg)

Our next revisit was to the Vermillion Cliffs, which includes the Navajo Bridge
and the Cliff Dweller’s Lodge. This wonderful park is surrounded by beautiful
red cliffs and blue skies.

![06](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110618_utah/06.jpg)
![07](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110618_utah/07.jpg)

The first part of the park contains the Navajo Bridge, which bridges the
beautiful Colorado River.

![08](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110618_utah/08.jpg)
![09](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110618_utah/09.jpg)

The Cliff Dweller’s Lodge is home to one of my favorite photos I have ever
taken. The old adobe home on the side of the road with turquoise door and
window frames that can be found at the Cliff Dweller’s Lodge is a truly
beautiful little piece of ancient times.  Before I gave the picture of only
the door and window, but now here is the entire house.

![10](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110618_utah/10.jpg)
![11](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110618_utah/11.jpg)

Surrounding this old home are the Vermillion Cliffs and these strange boulder
homes that have been built up and once upon a time, lived in.  These strange
homes are littered about in a hap-hazard fashion but are extremely intriguing
due to their balanced and fragile looking structure.

![12](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110618_utah/12.jpg)
![13](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110618_utah/13.jpg)

While exploring these old places my mom and I were having fun stopping at every
single Native American jewelry stand we came across. All over the side of the
roads are little stands full of gorgeous hand made jewelry, dream-catchers,
and pottery. We had a lot of fun shopping around and talking to the local
Navajo’s about their beautiful home.

![14](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110618_utah/14.jpg)

After a long day of reminiscing we took an unexpected turn and decided to go to
Death Valley.

![15](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110618_utah/15.jpg)

We had plans of going to Bishop and then Devil’s Postpile National Park, but
found out that it is still closed due to the snow pack they still have.  So
yes, a strange choice for the summer where even at night it is 107 degrees
outside, but we did it anyways. So tonight we find ourselves staying at the
Stovepipe Wells in the heart of Death Valley after driving down an ominously
straight and long road out into the desert.

![16](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110618_utah/16.jpg)

Tomorrow we will hike out before sunrise to see the sand dunes in the early
morning glow before the sun bears down on us with its unforgiving heat.

![17](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110618_utah/17.jpg)
