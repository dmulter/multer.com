Today we started out our day in Lake Havasu City, there I happened upon the
strange Gabriel species again. This one was giant, he dwarfed a lighthouse
that was on the edge of the beautiful lake.

![01](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110403_springbreak/01.jpg)
![02](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110403_springbreak/02.jpg)

Another strange and rare sighting of the Gabriel. After that we headed over to
the famous London Bridge. Yes, the real London Bridge in Lake Havasu City
Arizona. Strange right?

![03](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110403_springbreak/03.jpg)

So apparently the original London Bridge was becoming old and ruined so they
decided to rebuild it. This left the old bridge nowhere, so they put it up for
sale. Lake Havasu City bought it, shipped it, and then rebuilt it right here
on Lake Havasu.

![04](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110403_springbreak/04.jpg)
![05](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110403_springbreak/05.jpg)

Next on the day’s agenda was Historical Route 66. My dad and brother were going
on a long bike ride up route 66 and my mom and I were going running.

![06](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110403_springbreak/06.jpg)

I had never been on Route 66 before and it was quite the experience.

![07](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110403_springbreak/07.jpg)

We ran along a Wildlife Refuge’s lake that was very beautiful but extremely
hot. We also saw a coyote but sadly I didn’t have my camera with me.

![08](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110403_springbreak/08.jpg)

Then was the long drive around a beautiful hilly valley that really gave us a
taste of what Arizona is made of. There were towering monoliths or red craggy
rocks, hills full of yellow wildflowers and cacti, and long expanses of open
countryside.

![09](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110403_springbreak/09.jpg)

All along the way were interesting sights. Mostly things people would consider
eye sores or trash but they were really interesting and amazing. A lot of old
rusted out cars, old houses broken down, and well, trash.

![10](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110403_springbreak/10.jpg)
![11](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110403_springbreak/11.jpg)
![12](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110403_springbreak/12.jpg)
![13](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110403_springbreak/13.jpg)
![14](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110403_springbreak/14.jpg)

Probably the most interesting part of our day was visiting the tiny town of
Oatman. It was full of local color, tiny shops, and of course, donkeys. What
quaint little towns isn’t ruled by donkeys?

![15](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110403_springbreak/15.jpg)

They were everywhere. All over the streets, walking up to cars, people, and
trying to get food from everyone. They were adorable; there were babies all
over the place but it was so hot they all were taking naps.

![16](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110403_springbreak/16.jpg)
![17](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110403_springbreak/17.jpg)

It was quite the sight, watching all these donkeys just wondering around the
town in search of shade and food.

![18](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110403_springbreak/18.jpg)

It was a good day on Route 66 and there will be more adventure for tomorrow.
Tonight we are bedding down in Flagstaff, tomorrow…. the Grand Canyon!
