Let me introduce to you a very strange animal called a dik-dik.Yes, its name is
dik-dik. The even funnier part is what this animal actually is, a deer like
animal that is only about a two foot long. An antelope named for its shrill
call that actually sounds like “dik dik” or “zik zik”. Probably one of the
strangest animals I have ever seen, I had heard about them before but never
expected to see one in person when I went to the San Francisco Zoo. I am very
glad I got to see one, and now you can too.
