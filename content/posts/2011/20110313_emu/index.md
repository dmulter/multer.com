Because I have so many photos from our zoo adventure I am going to break ti
down for each animal we saw. The first is the Emu. An incredible creepy and
slightly gross bird. Similar to an ostrich but weirder. There is only one
thing I enjoy about emus, their eyes. They have such brightly colored eyes and
it is there only redeeming feature to me.

Within an animal's eyes lies all their beauty.
