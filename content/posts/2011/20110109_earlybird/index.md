Gabe's planning on doing all the Early Bird Criterium races this year, and here
we are for the first mentoring and racing session. A bit of warmup on the
rollers, and it's off to the start of the race. It's a good sized field, and
I've tried to pass on the key advice Gabe will need today. The pack wound up
getting shredded when a really fast Spokesman guy took off the front. After a
couple of laps between two packs, Gabe dropped to the second pack and finished
a strong first. Outstanding job Gabe!
