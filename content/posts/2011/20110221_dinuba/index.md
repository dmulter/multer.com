Yesterday Gabe did the Pine Flat road race, and this afternoon it's the Dinuba
Criterium. He'll be riding a small loop thirty times around very quiet Dinuba.
This huskie anxiously awaits the start with the rest of us.

trial. One guy manages to hang on for a couple of laps before running out of
steam. Gabe is staying right on Aidan's wheel, and the small group is working
