and island hopping today instead. There are loads of small hikes around the
small islands surrounding Isle Royale, and each one has it's own surprises.

We continue on the trail to visit with Rolf and Candy Peterson to talk about
the [wolves and moose of Isle Royale](https://www.isleroyalewolf.org). It was
such a treat to be able to talk at length with such outstanding scientists
that have spent so much of their lives devoted to understanding and sharing
their knowledge about wildlife. The huge pile of moose bones were pretty cool
too.
