Last year in Horseshoe Bend I found an amazing looking lizard while out hiking.
This year since we returned to the same spot I was hoping to again find a
lizard like that. To do this we began a lizard hunt. We found several
different types of lizard, the most common was this little guy with the black
banded neck.

![01](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110621_lizard/01.jpg)

We also spotted a nice small red lizard. He was enjoying his sun spot and was
extremely good at camouflaging himself.

![02](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110621_lizard/02.jpg)

There was a skink looking lizard as well who was very colorful. He was very
fast and not very interested in sticking around for me to photograph him.

![03](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110621_lizard/03.jpg)

Finally after searching for a long time we finally found the lizard I was
looking for. It was the same type of lizard as last year but unfortunately he
was younger and I didn’t have the amazing color that the other did. He had the
prettiest patterns and shapes including spots and stripes all over him.

![04](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110621_lizard/04.jpg)
![05](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110621_lizard/05.jpg)
![06](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110621_lizard/06.jpg)

He was posing all over the place, a perfect little model of a lizard. He was on
rocks, near old branches, and of course the beautiful red sand.

![07](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110621_lizard/07.jpg)
![08](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110621_lizard/08.jpg)
![09](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110621_lizard/09.jpg)
![10](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110621_lizard/10.jpg)

He really was funny, running all over the place, stretching, and doing
push-ups. It was awesome to see such a beautiful animal such as this lizard.
All of them were beautiful and it just reminds me how much I love taking
photos of animals and other wildlife.
