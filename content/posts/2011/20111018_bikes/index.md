Italy is a place of bikes where the alley ways and streets are filled with both
bikes well loved and abandoned. Some are tagged with graffiti, while others
are dainty and clean. Some are simply left abandoned, down a dark alleyway for
someone else to find.

![02](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20111018_bikes/02.jpg)

They are so interesting to look at with the a thousand different backdrops from
different city walls. The city changes but the bikes stay the same. They are a
monument to mobility that mark the streets without mercy.
