One amazing thing about Venice are its alley ways. You travel through the heart
of the city by walking down twists and turns of alley ways, some lead nowhere
while others bring you to your destination. Venice is a labyrinth of alley
ways filled with amazing little artisan shops. Every turn you take leads you
down a new one, and to new things.

![02](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110922_venice/02.jpg)

Some are filled with color, others are dark and dank. Some are peaceful and
romantic while others are crowded and chaotic. But you can always look up and
see beautiful blue sky.

![03](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110922_venice/03.jpg)
![04](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110922_venice/04.jpg)

Alley ways are not reserved just for ground, the waterways for intricate alley
ways as well with lines of houses and shops surrounding you.

![05](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110922_venice/05.jpg)
![06](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110922_venice/06.jpg)
![07](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110922_venice/07.jpg)

I love the alley ways in Venice because you never know what the next will look
like. There is such beauty and variety that you can never get bored.
