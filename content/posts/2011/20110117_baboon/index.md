Baboons are a very strange, colorful, and robust animal. They are very fun to
photograph because they have such amazing colors in the strangest of places
which I am sure I won’t have to mention because everyone is fully aware of
that already.

![02](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110117_baboon/02.jpg)

Yes, he is scratching his butt.

![03](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110117_baboon/03.jpg)

I really enjoyed the females the most even though the men have such interesting
looks I liked the females better. Their hair looked almost like it had been
crimped and dyed with yellow.  Their eyes are the most amazing par, wide and
yellow. They were fascinating to watch.
