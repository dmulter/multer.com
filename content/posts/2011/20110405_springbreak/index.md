I had visited the North Rim of the Grand Canyon last summer and now I finally
got to see the South Rim. It was pretty impressive and well, grand.

![01](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110405_springbreak/01.jpg)

It is amazing to stand on the edge of a huge precipice that seems to stretch on
forever knowing that I have now been on both sides. I would love to go across
the canyon at some point in my life.

![02](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110405_springbreak/02.jpg)

It is hard to imagine that there is so much beauty everywhere in the world.
Without seeing things like this, I would never have believed that just around
the corner is an amazing sight like this.

![03](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110405_springbreak/03.jpg)

Out on this white pillar I spotted another rare sight hidden in the trees. It
was making strange sounds and crawling around in the bushes. So of course I
went to investigate.  There in the bushes was…

![04](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110405_springbreak/04.jpg)

The Gabriel! He was only one of many interesting sights we saw. The Colorado
River could be sen far below in the canyon.

![05](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110405_springbreak/05.jpg)
![06](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110405_springbreak/06.jpg)

The colors were phenomenal and created such depth in the canyon that the entire
canyon was just breath-taking.

![07](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110405_springbreak/07.jpg)
![08](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110405_springbreak/08.jpg)
![09](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110405_springbreak/09.jpg)

At the edge of the East side of the canyon was the Watchtower. An impressive
tower that you could climb into and overlook the canyon.

![10](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110405_springbreak/10.jpg)
![11](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110405_springbreak/11.jpg)

We also found some ruins that were interesting. However, no Indian ruins seem
nearly as impressive as Mesa Verde was.

![12](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110405_springbreak/12.jpg)
![13](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110405_springbreak/13.jpg)

We ended our day by taking a quick visit to Sunset Crater Volcanic National
Monument. It was slightly disappointing because there was no crater you could
go see, just volcanic rock.

![14](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110405_springbreak/14.jpg)

It was a long but impressive day. Tomorrow we are exploring Sedona!
