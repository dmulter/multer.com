We're in the Sacramento area for another day of racing at the Bariani road
race. The weather is still threatening, but I think the rain is finally over.
Of course the roads are still pretty wet and the pavement is pretty chewed up.
Ethan gets a nice break early in the Cat 5 race of three laps with a ton of
wind on the course.
