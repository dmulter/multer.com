Charlotte and Gabe headed out to the central valley for the Snelling road race,
while Monica and I were in Yellowstone. Sounds like it was a cold, but dry day
to ride some beautiful roads with the other juniors. They started hammering
right away, and Gabe was happy with his 9th place finish.
