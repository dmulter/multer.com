Greenstone Ridge further west to Mt Ojibway. Plenty of great views, signs
along the trail, and a little bit of fall color.

The trail back to Rock Harbor isn't technical, but the roots and rocks make for
difficult walking. My heels are all blistered and it's pretty much a death
march for the last 5 miles. Check out the details of our long hike over at
[Strava](https://app.strava.com/activities/1595228).
