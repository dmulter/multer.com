It's the time of year again when the biggest road cycling race in the USA comes
to our neck of the woods, the Amgen Tour of California. This year Gabe is
working to get tons of autographs from some of his favorite riders on a
cycling hat. Some of his favorites include: Jens Voigt, Johan Van Summeren,
Laurens Ten Dam, Oscar Freire, Lars Boom, Bernhard Eisel, Taylor Phinney, and
George Hincapie.

After a quick pee break, Gabe and I are off to ride up Calaveras Canyon, then
up Felter and the back side of Sierra Road to watch the summit finish in San
Jose where Chris Horner showed the whole field how a great climber makes it
look easy.
