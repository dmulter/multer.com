Walking into Florence is like entering a labyrinth of colored houses, narrow
alley ways, and open market places. There is so much to see here that is just
simply astounding.

![02](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20111112_florence/02.jpg)

Everywhere you walk is something new and beautiful to look at; every corner
beholds a new and interesting facet to this city.
