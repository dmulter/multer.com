Time for the [Central Coast Circuit](https://cccxcycling.com) road race again.
Not much wind, but it's a typical cool, foggy morning as Gabe and Mack get
ready to roll in the Elite 4s.

![02](02.jpg)

Charlotte and I hike out towards the other side of the course, and catch a
glimpse of them coming down the hill before we continue up to the top.

![03](03.jpg)
![04](04.jpg)
![05](05.jpg)
![06](06.jpg)

There's a pretty cool lookout at the top of the hill, but Charlotte confirms
that yellow jackets are swarming just like the warning sign indicated. Time to
head back down the hill.

![07](07.jpg)
![08](08.jpg)
![09](09.jpg)

Mack did a great job staying at the front and attacking, while Gabe hangs with
the pack. His junior gearing makes it really tough when he hits the flat at
the bottom of the downhill. I hear Mack, Tobin, and Matt did great in the
Elite 3/4 race later in the afternoon.
