You thought our day was over? Nope, not even close. We then headed over to the
next part of the national park to go try and see some more slot canyons called
Peek-A-Boo Canyon and Spooky Canyon. From the top of the trail it was pretty
unassuming.

![01](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110615_utah/01.jpg)

Little did we know that we had to climb far down to reach the bottom of a
canyon in one hundred degree weather only then to reach the slot canyons. It
was brutal sliding down the cliff knowing that later I would have to climb
back up them with the sun beating down on my back.

![02](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110615_utah/02.jpg)

We then reached the first slot canyon which was Peek-A-Boo and realized we were
unable to do it. With a combination of clumsiness and heat exhaustion we were
unable to climb into the canyon. Obviously I was extremely upset about this
when faced with the possibility of having come all this way for nothing.

![03](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110615_utah/03.jpg)

After a long deliberation we decided to push on and go see the other canyon,
Spooky. It was amazing. Spooky is a true slot canyon meaning it is a
claustrophobic persons worst nightmare. You have to duck, crawl, and squeeze
your way to advance in these canyons but they are so worth it.

![04](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110615_utah/04.jpg)
![05](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110615_utah/05.jpg)

The sculpted walls of the canyon are beyond art and beyond all other natural
creations. Sometimes it is hard to believe that they were sculpted not by mans
hands but by something else.

![06](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110615_utah/06.jpg)
![07](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110615_utah/07.jpg)
![08](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110615_utah/08.jpg)

Like this one for example, the kissing rocks. These two sculpted walls from the
right angle look like people kissing. How could that be an accident? The
answer? Mother nature is amazing beyond all words

![09](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110615_utah/09.jpg)

It is quite the experience squeezing through this tiny little gaps. You can
feel ever line in the stone, the coolness as you brush your fingertips along
it, and you can hear the breeze passing through the canyon’s corridors.

![10](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110615_utah/10.jpg)
![11](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110615_utah/11.jpg)
![12](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110615_utah/12.jpg)

It really was amazing and a must have life experience. They are so unique and
marvelous, it is amazing to think that these formed naturally and are not
actually pieces of art sculpted by who knows what.

![13](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110615_utah/13.jpg)
![14](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110615_utah/14.jpg)

Our day however didn’t end happily, we were stuck on the side of the road in
one hundred degree weather with a flat tire. We were going to have to wait but
a wonderful person came and saved us! So we are behind schedule now but not by
much. We got our butts kicked by these amazing hikes but it was all worth it
in the end!
