Part two of our Napa adventure included the most amazing thing I saw all
weekend was the Castello de Amorosa. We visited this castle out in the wine
country was extraordinary.

![02](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110209_napa/02.jpg)
![03](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110209_napa/03.jpg)
![04](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110209_napa/04.jpg)

My favorite part of the castle was not actually the structure, but the animals
around it. Especially the sheep, they cracked me up and made it feel almost
like we were back in time at a medieval castle.

![05](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110209_napa/05.jpg)
![06](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110209_napa/06.jpg)
![07](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110209_napa/07.jpg)

It was so quaint and beautiful out there with all the animals. I really enjoyed
the sheep but I also made a friend with the castle’s guardian, a little orange
tabby cat. He was beautiful and was sitting in an olive tree outside the
castle walls.

![08](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110209_napa/08.jpg)
![09](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110209_napa/09.jpg)

There were also an array of birds including peacocks and chickens.

![10](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110209_napa/10.jpg)
![11](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110209_napa/11.jpg)

It was a lot of fun exploring Napa with my mom and hope to do it again some
time but for now the adventure in castles, food and wineries will have to do.
