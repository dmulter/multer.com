It's that time of year again for the SVEF Mt Charlie Challenge. I've been doing
this every year since the very start, and last year Gabe did the 50K for the
first time. This year he's definitely doing the 100K ride and he'll be kicking
my ass. Time to get the festivities rolling and get those riders out there in
support of our schools!
