It's another beautiful day on Isle Royale, so we're off for
    some great hiking on the eastern end of the island. We'll be taking the M.V. Sandy over
    to Lookout Louise, then we'll head along the Greenstone Ridge out to Mt Franklin for a great
    view. We get a nice view of the Ranger on our way back to Rock Harbor. Check out the
    details of our hike over on [Strava](https://www.strava.com/activities/1595227).
