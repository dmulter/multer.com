This year's Memorial Day Criterium is also the NCNCA Junior State
Championships. It's not a surprise to see Jack leading the pack in the 13-14
group. He eventually won it, but Adrien from Gabe's SJBC team did an awesome
job staying right on his tail and eventually getting second place.

Now it's time for the 15-16 age group to roll. Zeke, Tim, and Gabe will be
working together to help Zeke lock in his Junior Points Series (JPS) win.
Henry from Tieni Duro broke away early as he often does, but this time none of
the other teams were willing to work together to chase him down. SJBC had
their plan, Tieni Duro certainly wasn't going to chase, and Matt from
Specialized was on his own. I'm sure it was frustrating for a number of the
riders, but big kudos to Henry for winning state.
