Yesterday's stage of the Tour of California was a blast, so Charlotte and I are
back on the road to watch the riders leave Seaside for the long ride out to
Paso Robles today. Bob Roll is here too along with plenty of bikes.

![02](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110519_toc/02.jpg)
![03](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110519_toc/03.jpg)

Gabe is in school, so unfortunately he's not able to collect more autographs
for his hat. Plenty of great riders getting ready to roll from Radio Shack,
Garmin, Rabobank, and BMC. The start is so much quieter than in Livermore,
almost a bit like rolling out on a small training ride. Livermore sure did a
great job yesterday.
