We're headed south on a big loop in search of great cycling. First stop is the
amazing city of Solvang. Our first day will be a nice warmup doing the
[Solvang Century 100K route](https://app.strava.com/activities/2904766).

Meanwhile Charlotte and Monica get to enjoy the ostriches just outside of town.

The next day we're over to Los Olivos where Gabe and I head up over the
classic [Mt Figueroa](https://app.strava.com/activities/2924355).

![39](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20111226_solvang/39.jpg)

We head south to Santa Barbara where we drop off the girls at the train station
to head back home. Then Gabe and I continue south to Malibu where we fall in
love with [Latigo and Malibu Canyon]
(https://app.strava.com/activities/2942550). Finally we head to Fresno to hook
up with Gabe's friend Ryan Brooks for a nice [recovery ride]
(https://app.strava.com/activities/2962545) and then a great loop of
[Pine Flat](https://app.strava.com/activities/2986213) the next day.
