The town of Oatman was just before it really started to climb, and it sure was
a cute little western town. The tourists, cars, and burros pretty much choked
off the road. Time to load up on more water.

After a great decent, we decided to cut the route short as it was just flat and
bumpy for miles. This old rest stop in Cool Springs was the perfect place to
wait for Charlotte and Monica to catch up.

![30](30.jpg)

We lucked out finding Mr. D'z Diner on Route 66 in Kingman. The food was
awesome and Gabe really loved their homemade root beer.
