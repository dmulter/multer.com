The [Sea Otter Classic](https://www.seaotterclassic.com) is a major racing
event for cyclist from everywhere that just so happens to be in our backyard.
This of course means that the competition is going to be crazy, and I can see
that Gabe is hoping to just hang in there. It's a tough, hilly course and the
Specialized juniors dish our plenty of pain. I know Gabe enjoyed riding next
to Phil for a little while.
