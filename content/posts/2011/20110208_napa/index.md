My family and I went on a short trip up to Napa for my little brother’s road
bike race called the Cherry Pie Criterium. Yes, the winners do get cherry
pies, sadly not us though. This was my first trip to wine country and because
I am not yet of drinking age we had to find some pretty awesome adventures
other than wine tasting. First plan for adventure: visiting cool looking
wineries and taking pictures of fields with flowers.

![02](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110208_napa/02.jpg)
![03](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110208_napa/03.jpg)

We visited one winery with really interesting statues around it and it was
really beautiful. Not to mention the weather while we were p there, around 80
the whole time. Nice!

![04](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110208_napa/04.jpg)
![05](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110208_napa/05.jpg)

Just looking around Napa there is such beautiful architecture everywhere. I
wish I could have taken photos of all of it. The other amazing part about Napa
are the old trees. I love the trees there with their scraggly limbs and
reaching arms.

![06](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110208_napa/06.jpg)
![07](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110208_napa/07.jpg)

Next item on the adventure agenda was food of course! You can’t go to Napa and
not eat good food, I think it should be a crime not to get good food. Our food
of choice was the CIA, not the federal scary secretive thing but the Culinary
Institute of America.

![08](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2011/20110208_napa/08.jpg)

It was really amazing. We got two samplers, the entrée samplers and then the
dessert samplers. With a side of the best brussels sprouts I have ever eaten!
