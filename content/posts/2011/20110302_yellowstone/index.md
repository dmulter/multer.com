This year Monica and I are headed back to [Yellowstone]
(https://www.nps.gov/yell) to enjoy the awesome wildlife and scenery again. I
didn't collect a lot of photos this year, as Monica and I shared the large
lenses and she posted a lot of her work on her blog. You can read her entries
for day [1](/posts/2011/yellowstone-day-1), [2]
(posts/2011/yellowstone-day-2), [3](posts/2011/yellowstone-day-3)[4]
(posts/2011/yellowstone-day-4)[5](posts/2011/yellowstone-day-5)[6]
(posts/2011/yellowstone-day-6). I've put a few of my favorite photos here for
you to enjoy. Unfortunately the wildlife was very sparse this year, but we did
manage to get some great fox photos. We did have a load of fun, right up to
the point I got a bad fever and cough for the last few days. It was a treat to
meet Chris Miller and his wife for dinner and talk about genealogy and life in
Montana.
