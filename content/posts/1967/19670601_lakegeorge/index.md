We're off for a big vacation this time up to [Lake George](https://www.visitlakegeorge.com).
I think we stayed at the Holiday Inn.

![01](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/1967/19670601_lakegeorge/01.jpg)
