The big event was held at the [Half Moon Bay Methodist church](https://www.hmbcumc.org).
It's a beautiful old church and the perfect size for our relatively small
group.

![40](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/1988/19880319_church/40.jpg)

Everyone is gathering for the ceremony. Everything is kind of a blur to me. I
am so excited to marry the love of my life.
