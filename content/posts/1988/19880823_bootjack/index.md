The dock is so peaceful and inviting. One would think that vacationing in
Bootjack is all relaxation, swimming, boating, fishing, and all kinds of other
fun things.

![02](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/1988/19880823_bootjack/02.jpg)

You would be mistaken. Everyone needs to pitch in and work together on the wood
pile every year. Gordon relies on this weed for heating in the winter, and
it's a small price to pay for all the hospitality and good times. It's also a
great chance to work up a sweat and get bit by the really big mosquitoes that
buzz around the sawdust.
