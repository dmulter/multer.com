Scott's in town visiting, so we decide to take a hiking trip out to
[Yosemite](https://www.nps.gov/yose/). We start the hike at Glacier Point and go as far
as Scott can last. An old friend from our Colorado days is in town as well,
and he joins us for the hike.

Charlotte is busy working out our route, while I gear up for the trip.

![11](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/1988/19880830_glacierpoint/11.jpg)

Lots of spectacular scenery along the way. We pass quite a few waterfalls on
this trail. It's very nice starting at the top of the canyon rather than the
valley floor. You also get lots of fantastic views of Half Dome and other
peaks.
