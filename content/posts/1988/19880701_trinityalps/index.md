Charlotte and I are headed up to the Trinity Alps north of Mt. Shasta. We hear
that the backpacking is great up there, and it's remote enough to remind us of
hiking in Colorado. Mt. Shasta is a great site on the way up.

![01](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/1988/19880701_trinityalps/01.jpg)
![02](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/1988/19880701_trinityalps/02.jpg)

The hike is very steep as we climb through Long Canyon. It's a nice hike and we
welcome the rest when we break for camp.

![03](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/1988/19880701_trinityalps/03.jpg)
![04](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/1988/19880701_trinityalps/04.jpg)
![05](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/1988/19880701_trinityalps/05.jpg)
![06](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/1988/19880701_trinityalps/06.jpg)

We head up to Deer Creek Pass for the big view of the trip. If we had more
supplies and time, I suppose we could go down the other side, but it's back
the way we came.
