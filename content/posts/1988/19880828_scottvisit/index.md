We make a visit to Santa Cruz while my brother Scott is in town. We check out
the the view from the wharf. The [boardwalk](https://beachboardwalk.com) looks
like a lot of fun also.

![01](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/1988/19880828_scottvisit/01.jpg)
![02](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/1988/19880828_scottvisit/02.jpg)
![03](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/1988/19880828_scottvisit/03.jpg)

We then head out to Steamer Lane and the surfing museum. There's a nice view of
a sailing regatta and someone catching a wave. It's always a nice day to visit
Santa Cruz. It's really quite a warm spot in comparison to home in Half Moon
Bay.
