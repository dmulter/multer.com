Charlotte's friend Jana from Colorado has come out for a visit. We make a trip
up to San Francisco on a windy day.

![01](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/1988/19880601_jana/01.jpg)

On a drive through the back country we come across a very cool red barn. It
makes a great excuse for a stop along the way.
