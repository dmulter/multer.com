Charlotte and I are down in Santa Cruz for Thanksgiving dinner at Valri's
house. Pam and Lyle came down from Half Moon Bay with us. We're also joined by
our friend Stewart who's down from Berkeley. Chris is looking very sharp in
his little outfit.
