I've been racing on Sundays up in Fremont and really enjoying it. Monica joined
me last weekend, and this weekend Charlotte came to check it out also. We've
been lucky with the beautiful weather, and the last day for the series is the
best day yet. Last week there were a number of crashes in my group including
one right in front of me. Every other week including this one has been totally
safe. I was very happy with my form this early in the season, and felt like I
had a lot of strength left for the end. Now I just need to keep working on my
positioning for the finish.
