Gabe, Doug, and I planned to take out the boat this morning for some fishing,
but the fog had a different idea. It's been beautiful every day, though a bit
windy. That's why today and the thick fog early was such a surprise. Trying to
roll with the punches, we headed to the Santa Cruz Wharf for some fishing
instead. We caught quite a few small fish including jack smelt, mackerel,
anchovies, tiny flounder, and even a starfish. The day turned out very will
indeed.
