We're off to the [aquarium](https://www.montereybayaquarium.org) at Gabe's
request to see the new great white shark they're hosting. Unfortunately all
the pictures of the giant tuna, sea turtle, sunfish, barracuda, and the great
white shark didn't come out. It was a pretty small shark anyway. We did manage
to get a shot of the sardines. Kind of like a shark.
