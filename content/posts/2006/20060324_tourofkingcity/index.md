My friend George put together a three day bike ride that started in
Watsonville, headed down to Carmel, then over the mountains to King City, and
then finally back through Hollister returning home. Tony and Jeff made up the
rest of our tiny peloton, with Erik saving the day with ride support. Our
first day was a beautiful ride along the coast and then out to Carmel
Village.

![01](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2006/20060324_tourofkingcity/01.jpg)
![02](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2006/20060324_tourofkingcity/02.jpg)

The next morning was a bit wet and windy, but it calmed down quite a bit by the
time we got rolling. The hot tub last night really helped prepare everyone for
another long day of riding.

to King City. A nice flat road with a good tailwind made for loads of speed

Our last leg headed up past Pinnacles and really a magnificent stretch of road.
Unfortunately we're short on photos, but we did run into a group heading south
to Big Sur when we got to Hollister. We then loaded up at Togos before heading
out for the final push back to Watsonville.
