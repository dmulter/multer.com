George was nice enough to let us use his new boat until he gets up. So I've got
the pleasure of launching his boat with a bit of help from Charlotte and Gabe.
You know we'll be doing lots of fishing from the boat.

![02](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2006/20060712_bootjack/02.jpg)
![03](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2006/20060712_bootjack/03.jpg)
![04](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2006/20060712_bootjack/04.jpg)

My mom came up for a week this year for the first time. It's great having her
up so it's time to start showing her the sites. First stop is Jacobsville and
the south entry. I love the way Portage and Superior are so different on each
side of the entry.
