We had a nasty storm blow through last night that included booming thunder,
lightning, and a load of hail. This morning we were greeted to a wintery scene
the likes of which I've never seen in all the years I've been here.

Of course this all looks like loads of fun to the kids. Only children that live
in Santa Cruz think it's novel to go down a slide or jump on a trampoline
that's covered in ice.
