Charlotte and Val take the kids down to a new swimming hole that Charlotte
spotted on a recent hike. Turns out it was packed, which I guess is related to
this being the first day of summer and it's 95 degrees outside. Instead they
head down a very steep trail with a rope that managed to scare a few of the
kids to death. The reward is what looks like a really nice swimming spot
anyway. A few nice shots of the Roaring Camp train for good measure.
