Matt and I are off to [Yosemite](https://www.nps.gov/yose/) to enjoy an amazing
day in one of the most fabulous places anywhere. It's a great chance to spend
a big block of time discussing work at the same time we get in some
photography. The weather cooperated nicely and the water was really flowing.
