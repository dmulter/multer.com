Nemo and her crew headed out to Santa Cruz Reef today to see what the rockfish
catch was like. We were rewarded with this nice red, a wide variety of small
rockfish, mackeral, jacksmelt, and more. The weather got a bit rough towards
the end, but these salty sailors were up to the challenge. Looking forward to
our next trip.
