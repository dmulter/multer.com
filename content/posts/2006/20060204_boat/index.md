It's been a long time coming, but I've finally gone off the deep end and
purchased a boat. Living in Santa Cruz, fishing with Gabe, and loving the
ocean in general have conspired to drive me temporarily insane. I've kept my
eye open for a particular model of boat for some years, and I finally chanced
on one a guy was selling out near Fresno. A quick drive to check out the boat
and the deal was almost done. A few weeks later that resolved an engine
problem and we've got a new addition to the family. The [C-Dory](https://www.c-dory.com)
16 foot Angler model may be small, but it's one awesome boat, especially for
these waters. Welcome little Nemo to the family.
