The family is heading out to Grandma's place near Philadelphia for Passover.
We'll be spending the week visiting grandma, Lynda, and the rest of the
family. We'll also be heading out to New York for a Seder with Carole and a
whole pile of people. Scott and his family had to cancel at the last minute
because of Bridget mom's death. We'll really miss them on this trip and will
be thinking of them.
