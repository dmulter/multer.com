Monica and Mackenzie are over at Sarah's house to make a little trouble. First
they help in loading up Sarah's new iPod. Mackenzie then shows the Japanese
writing she recalled from a recent dream. Surprisingly, it means absolutely
nothing.

![01](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2006/20060309_marshmallowman/01.jpg)
![02](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2006/20060309_marshmallowman/02.jpg)

Their next great idea was to build a marshmallow man in effigy of a substitute
teacher they aren't too fond of. I think they may be the first to practice
marshmallow voodoo.

![03](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2006/20060309_marshmallowman/03.jpg)

![05](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2006/20060309_marshmallowman/05.jpg)
![06](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2006/20060309_marshmallowman/06.jpg)

Sarah's pet Axolotl watches from a safe distance. Monica thinks this is the
coolest pet ever.

![07](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2006/20060309_marshmallowman/07.jpg)

Mr. Marshmallow and his friends are finally complete. I think he knows
something bad is coming once he's mounted to the cardboard. His happy pretzel
face gives a hint of what's next.

![08](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2006/20060309_marshmallowman/08.jpg)
![09](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2006/20060309_marshmallowman/09.jpg)

![11](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2006/20060309_marshmallowman/11.jpg)

A sad site indeed, as he is consigned to flames of woe. Goodbye Mr.
Marshmallow, may the place you're going be a better one than this.
