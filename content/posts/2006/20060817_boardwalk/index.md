Nick's working his first real summer job this year. Our friends Matt and Paula
own a few restaurants on the Santa Cruz Boardwalk and they were nice enough to
offer Nick the chance to work there. I never imagined getting such a cool job
when I was a kid. Who wouldn't buy a hot dog on a stick from this guy!

![01](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2006/20060817_boardwalk/01.jpg)
