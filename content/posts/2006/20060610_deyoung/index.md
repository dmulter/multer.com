The plan was to make a trip up to San Francisco with the family, but Nick had
lots of homework and Gabe wasn't up for it. Monica and Charlotte made it a
girl's day out and visited the [de Young museum](https://www.famsf.org/visit/de-young). Gabe and I played some
tennis and went for a bike ride around the neighborhood.
