Charlotte, Valri, and Gordon headed over to the [UCSC Arboretum](https://arboretum.ucsc.edu) to see
what spring has served up. It's getting close to the time where Gordon heads
back to Bootjack, so these are some of the last trips the girls will be doing
with their dad.
