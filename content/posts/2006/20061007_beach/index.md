I'm taking Gabe and Doug out for some boogie boarding over at Manresa this
morning while Charlotte checks out Open Studios. The swell wasn't too big, but
boy it was a fantastic day. Charlotte meets us over at Seascape park for some
lunch and goofing around. Gabe thinks he's some kind of mutant bunny, and I
think Doug is out for the hunt.
