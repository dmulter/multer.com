I head out to the east hills of San Jose today for Stage 2 of the Tour of
California. It is such a treat to have a real professional stage race right in
my own backyard. Of course it would have been even better if they went through
Santa Cruz, maybe next year. I parked at the base of Sierra Road where a big
flock of turkeys greeted me. Hope there isn't a message in this coincidence.

![01](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2006/20060221_tourca/01.jpg)

The climb is something like 3.5 miles with 1800 feet of climbing over an
average 10% grade. It's a very nice climb, though Jamison or Alba are way
harder. Some cowboys and cows are the only other company at the top of this
very scenic and cold mountain top.
