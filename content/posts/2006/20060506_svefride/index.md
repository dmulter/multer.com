It's time for our annual fundraising bicycle ride again. The ride is a great
excuse to ride with friends and raise money for our schools at the same time.
Charlotte puts in a huge amount of effort every year for this event, and it
really shows. The ride was a little smaller this year, but we still raised
loads of money.
