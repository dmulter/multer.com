Time for our second trip out on the bay with our new boat Nemo. This trip's
firsts include Gabe and actually doing some fishing. Other than forgetting to
unclip the trailer bow hitch and no fish, it turned out to be a very
successful trip. We made it out to Soquel Hole along with almost fifty other
boats, though mooching didn't produce any bites. We'll start trolling after a
few more learning trips I think.
