Gabe's class went for a field trip on the [O'Neill Sea Odyssey](https://www.oneillseaodyssey.org) today.
Definitely a trip I wanted to volunteer for. After suiting up all the kids in
life jackets, we proceeded to enjoy a great little cruise out the harbor
towards the boardwalk. The rain managed to stop just in time to give us a
truly spectacular day. After sailing, we headed back for some science labs
where the instructors discussed navigation, ecology, and biology. I think
everyone really enjoyed the microscope view of Plankton from Sponge Bob, or by
his less well known name of copepod.
