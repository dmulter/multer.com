We've got a small break in the weather and I've got to squeeze in a trip before
we head out for spring break. I'm not sure we'll be able to get in any
fishing, but Gordon and I are off to Soquel Hole to see what's up.

![01](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2006/20060407_fishing/01.jpg)
![02](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2006/20060407_fishing/02.jpg)
![03](https://s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/multer.com/content/posts/2006/20060407_fishing/03.jpg)

Here we are on our very first launch. Other than forgetting to remove the
transom straps, things go very smoothly. Unfortunately the weather and water
isn't very smooth and it's a rough ride out to the hole. No fishing on this
trip, but chalk it up as a great learning experience.
