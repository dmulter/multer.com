The kids and I met Charlotte over in Monterey after she finished the Big Sur
Half Marathon with her friend Michelle. She finished early which blew our
opportunity to visit the aquarium before picking her up. And she's definitely
too tired to start walking around for a few hours more. Awesome job on your
first marathon run!
