Charlotte took the long way to Bootjack this year. She's flown out to Chicago
and then heads out for the long drive north. They decided to stay a night up
at Manitowoc. The [Wisconsin Maritime Museum](https://www.wisconsinmaritime.org) looks like an interesting
tourist stop along the way.
