Charlotte and Paula are off to a new location for one of their famous long
walks. This time they're out at Nisene Marks down in Aptos rather than the
usual Scotts Valley and Felton spots. I think they got carried away given the
four hours they were gone and the sixteen miles they covered. Looks like they
had plenty of fun.
