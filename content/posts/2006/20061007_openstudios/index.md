Charlotte, Val, and Carol head out to [Mattie Leeds](https://mattieleeds.com)
during Open Studios this year. Val is taking a break from the Open Studios
thing this year, but it's still a great opportunity to check out other local
artists. Mattie has been very busy expanding on his very unique home and
studio.
