Another day in [Yosemite](https://www.nps.gov/yose/) means more great hiking.
Today we head out from the Ahwanee Hotel towards Mirror Lake. Finally Gabe has
his chance to climb all over lots of great boulders. Monica also found a great
pinecone to take home with us.
