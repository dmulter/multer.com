Welcome to the new home for the Multer family! I put a ton of effort into posting stories to the site for many years, but ultimately life got in the way. I feel bad for all the years the site sat idle, but the long wait is over. I've setup a whole new structure that will be much easier to maintain and add to as the years go on. It's also a much better approach for others to post content in a simple and unified way.

Another big step in building the new site was finding a new way to handle all the Multer family genealogical data. I also finally bit the bullet on that one, by migrating the content to WikiTree. It's a great service that's totally free, flexible, and much easier to collaborate with other family members. You'll find the link in the People section.

It'll take time to migrate all the story and genealogy content, but I've made a big start before switching out the old site. Don't fear any lost content, it's all coming soon. Keep checking back as I'll be continuing the migration effort at least weekly. You should also watch for new content mixed in with the old stuff.
