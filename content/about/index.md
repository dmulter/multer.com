Welcome to the new home for the Multer family! I put a ton of effort into
posting stories to the site for many years, but ultimately life got in the
way. I feel bad for all the years the site sat idle, but the long wait is
over. I've setup a whole new structure that will be much easier to maintain
and add to as the years go on. It's also a much better approach for posting
content in a simple and unified way.

Another big step in building the new site was finding a new way to handle all
the Multer family genealogical data. I also finally bit the bullet on that
one, by migrating the content to WikiTree. It's a great service that's totally
free, flexible, and much easier to collaborate with other family members.
You'll find the link in the Genealogy section.

I hope you enjoy the new website.
