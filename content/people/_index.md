Here you'll find a information for people in the Multer family and our
genealogical ancestry. My great-grandfather Max Multer emigrated from eastern
Europe in the late 1800s and moved to New York. We've since spread out across
the country and connected with a variety of other families. You can find the
Multer genealogy on [WikiTree](https://www.wikitree.com/genealogy/MULTER). I encourage you to explore our tree
without an account, or help contribute with a free account. Note that all this
information is secure and free, but all copyrights apply. Do not duplicate
and/or reproduce in any form without express permission.
