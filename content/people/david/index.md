Hello, and welcome to my website. I live in Wheat Ridge, CO with my fabulous
wife Charlotte. My son Gabe and his wife Laini live nearby in Golden, CO. My
daughter lives in Felton, CA close to where Charlotte and I raised our family
in Santa Cruz, CA. My oldest son Nick is no longer with us, and we miss him
terribly every day.

I grew up in New York, though my high school years were spent in Hollywood, FL
much to my dismay. I was an early computer software guy who received his BS in
Computer Science from RPI in Troy, NY. I met Charlotte at my first tech job
out of college in Boulder, CO. Work then brought me to the Bay Area where we
married and raised our beautiful family.

My career spanned a number of big companies like Apple and Shopify, plus
numerous startups I kicked off or joined early. I got to play a part in some
pretty breakthrough technologies like the Internet, mobile phones, the Cloud,
and more. I'm happy being retired now and spending most of my time cycling and
gardening. My LinkedIn profile is a great high level view of my career. You can
also see all the gritty details in my [resume](resume).

I hope you enjoy this website!
