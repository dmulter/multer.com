Here you'll find a information for people in the Multer family and our
genealogical ancestry. My great-grandfather [Max Multer](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Multer-52) emigrated from eastern
Europe in the late 1800s and moved to New York. We've since spread out across
the country and connected with a variety of other families. You can find the
Multer genealogy on [WikiTree](https://www.wikitree.com/genealogy/MULTER). I encourage you to explore our tree without an
account, or create a free account to view living persons and contribute.

A huge thanks to [Ellen](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Siegel-1371), [Stacey](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Multer-68),and [Daniel](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Multer-80)
for providing a wealth of great information on branches of the Multer family I
had lost track of. Their ongoing efforts to stay connected with other family
members plays a huge part in keeping this information up to date. The next
difficult step is trying to track down Max's ancestors in Eastern Europe.

I have to also give a big thanks to [Bea](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Valentine-5684), David, and [Karen](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Hoyem-29)
for their contributions connecting the Valentines, Zelmanows, and Hoyems.

Another big area is trying to connect to Multers that appear to be related. We
need help finding the connection between the Solomon Multer family and how
they may be related to Max Multer and his ancestors. Thanks to Phyllis for all
her great information.
